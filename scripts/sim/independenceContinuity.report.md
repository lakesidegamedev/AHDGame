# Scotland and Wales continuity qualification

Source base: `origin/development` at `599e26fa7b`.

Run `npx tsx scripts/sim/independenceContinuity.ts`. The replay opens no MongoDB connection and contains synthetic fixtures only. It runs the production expansion, economic promotion, v2 board promotion and law-program conversion shells against the stateful in-memory database driver, in both SCO/WAL orders.

Before conversion, the fixture contains 20,000,004 residents, 1,000,000 million units of regional GDP, 3,000,000 capital units, 301 corporate workers, £140,281.25 of regional wallet cash and £100 million of sovereign bond face. National treasury cash is £1 trillion, $1 trillion and ¥1 trillion in separate books; department cash is £2,000,060, $1,700,051 and ¥1,300,039. Both conversion orders preserve these totals, checked per currency. Resource ceilings, depletion, plant holdings, inventory witnesses, fractional age cohorts, regional funding and outstanding foreign wage principal are also checked.

The replay then runs three budget, treasury and national metric turns. Each turn updates all 13 successor regional budgets, settles five sovereign fiscal books (UK, US, JP, SCO, WAL), and refreshes national metric owners without missing due readings. The migrated wage debt remains payable through the actual payable-settlement rules.

Law succession also seeds a conflicting UK national choice, a separate unopposed national law, and a missing national opening reference. In both orders the regional L10 choice becomes the successor's single national program, its effective turn is preserved, and the unopposed UK law survives. The real reviewed-law catalogue can load the resulting current choices, the missing default remains proposable, and all three subsequent treasury turns deliver national funding to the promoted law. National department authority and cash do not increase during conversion; the former regional requests release only their funded amounts into regional budget headroom. This deliberately preserves the inherited national envelope rather than adding the old local request on top of it. Protected national sources are not superseded. Families with no national equivalent remain regional. Missing legal references use reviewed opening defaults without new appropriations. Existing departmental funding is recorded as replaceable inherited authority so a later bill does not purchase the same authority twice.

Additional tests cover housing stocks and construction pipeline counts, funding-record identities, pending-payment rejection and repeat-migration protection, balanced wallet ledger entries with the conversion turn, and zero readiness for an independent country with no armed forces.

Limits: this is a bounded economic migration and fiscal replay, not a full-world market benchmark or complete political referendum replay. It does not predict profitability or growth. No new geological detail is invented: installed extraction recipes determine reserve placement, with GDP allocation for undeveloped resources. No production data is changed. Previously completed independence conversions are not retroactively repaired.
