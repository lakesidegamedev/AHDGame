## What changed

- Computer-run countries make far fewer consumer goods, close to what real buyers actually order, so retail plants find buyers and retail prices recover.
- Computer-run countries ask for about a quarter of the electricity they did, so player power plants face a small shortage instead of a huge one.
- Ireland's computer-run economy is 60% of its former size, on both the buying and the selling side.
- No new demand or supply was added anywhere. Every change shrinks an existing flow.
