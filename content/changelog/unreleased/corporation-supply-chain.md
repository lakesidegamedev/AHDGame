## What changed

- Supply chain sits between Commodities and Tech under Operations.
- Company management can see actual suppliers, customers, quantities, prices and payments from the last completed turn.
- Filter your sites by country, state or region, sector type and individual location. Separate open-market trades, agreements and internal deliveries.
- Household and government customers are grouped by their spending wallets. Internal deliveries are excluded from company cash totals.
