## What changed

- A recent fix made the Balanced Budget and Fiscal Discipline approval bonuses national only, so governors who ran a surplus lost them. A state or province now earns both from its own budget: Balanced budget at a surplus of 0.5% of its GDP or more, and Fiscal discipline at 1% or more with debt at or below 90% of its GDP. National results still never count toward a state's rating.
