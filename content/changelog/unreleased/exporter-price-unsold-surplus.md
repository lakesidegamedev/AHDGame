title: Unsold export surplus lowers the national price
summary: A country's surplus only counts as exported once it actually ships abroad, so goods that find no foreign buyer now push the national price down.
tags: [economy, trade, commodities]
badges: [patch]

Countries whose surplus goods find no foreign buyer now see their national price fall, so they can compete for exports again. Countries short of a good also keep their higher price until imports actually arrive.
