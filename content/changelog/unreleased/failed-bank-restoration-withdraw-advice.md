## What changed

- A bank that failed after the withdraw page counted its maturing bonds twice
  and showed far more free to withdraw than it really had is reopened with its
  old charter, loans and customer deposits.
- The game covers the shortfall the bank had when it closed, and the deposit
  insurance fund gets back what it paid out for that closure.
