## What changed

- The older German law list no longer seeds into new worlds. Germany uses its law book only.
- The Länder income tax and VAT shares now follow the enacted income tax and sales tax laws in that book.
- The Länder trade tax uses the standard rate until the law book gains a Land-level lever for it.
- Opening tax rates for Germany in 1979, 1991 and the modern presets are unchanged.
