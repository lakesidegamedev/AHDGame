## What changed

- Resource grants, NPP fund grants, central bank reserve seeding, Patreon tier changes, sandbox access and single-player access are refused unless a staff account is behind them.
- Corporation repayments run through the heal tool need a staff account id for who asked.
- The resources grant form has an optional reason that is stored with the record.
- Admins get a Grants and adjustments screen under Economy, with filters for kind and recipient.
