- Subsidiary capital injections reserve one transfer slot and replay frozen, balanced settlement legs and receipts.
- Liabilities include bank loans, operating and tax arrears, and IMF principal alongside bonds.
- Tax forecasts consolidate site losses and apply loss relief consistently. Sector tax details read the turn's recorded assessment and distinguish assessment from cash payment.
- Recorded margins use income and revenue from the same snapshot, including received dividends. Extreme losses are no longer numerically clipped.
- History keeps frozen market values and currency evidence. Company currency changes require the recorded conversion basis.
- Bond history distinguishes actual coupon credits and issuer charges from scheduled estimates, including FX fees and funded sovereign payments.
- Missing currency quotes cannot silently become 1:1 operational conversions or fabricated transaction anchor evidence.
- Personal net worth history retains negative equity and avoids substituting present liabilities for missing historical evidence.

Recorded sector assessments and bond settlement evidence appear as turns complete after deployment. Older incomplete records are labeled unavailable or estimated; this update does not manufacture historical receipts or alter balances as a backfill.
