## What changed

- Plant wages paid workers about an eighth of the value plants add. Most of the
  rest stayed with companies as profit, so households, who only spend what they
  are paid, bought about a fifth of everything sold.
- Behind a new switch, off until it is turned on: each plant raises its wages
  toward a target share of the value it added last turn (60% by default).
- The raise never takes more than a set share (75% by default) of what the
  plant had left last turn after base wages and site costs, so a plant that
  made little or no profit pays its normal wages and no plant is pushed into a
  loss by the raise on last turn's numbers.
- Wages never fall below the normal payroll. The higher wages are paid to the
  households of the plant's state like any other wage.
