## What changed

- Each country now has a succession rule for its leader: who acts, for how long, and whether a deputy, an election, a legislature vote or a party vote fills the job. Brazil and Nigeria promote the vice president for the rest of the term.
- After a dissolution the outgoing government is a caretaker government until the new parliament produces a result. It cannot introduce new major bills, and the government page says so.
- A government page also shows who is acting while a prime minister is being chosen.
- If nobody files for a by-election, a candidate from the seat's previous party stands, so the seat is not left empty.
