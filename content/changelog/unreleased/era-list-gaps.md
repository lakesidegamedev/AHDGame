## What changed

- New 2027 worlds open with the same authored world alignments as 2023, and 1999 and 2007 worlds open with the 1991 ones, instead of every nation starting with the same lean toward Washington. Members of alliances and other organisations now get their opening alignment in these worlds too.
- In new 2027 worlds the Liberal Democrats, Japan's Liberal Democratic Party and Brazil's Workers' Party start as major parties, as they do in 2019. Germany's Greens start as major parties in 2023 and 2027 worlds as well. Party tiers still update every turn from how each party is actually doing.
- Singleplayer now offers 2027 as a start date.
- Election results pages for the Austrian, Finnish and Greek parliaments, the Italian and Spanish senates and the Brazilian Chamber of Deputies now show how many votes a seat cost, as other proportional chambers already did.
- Existing worlds, including the live world, are unchanged. The alliance and party changes apply when a world is started.
