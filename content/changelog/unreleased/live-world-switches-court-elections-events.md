## What changed

- New worlds start with all four on.
- The running world keeps its current behaviour after the update: empty by-elections stay empty, Supreme Court seats wait for a player President, surprise cases are ruled on when they are filed, and players who have seen every dated event get none that turn.
- Each can be turned on separately.
