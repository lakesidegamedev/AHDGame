## What changed

- A change in commodity prices now feeds into inflation at about a third of its old weight. Rises and falls count the same.
- The most commodities can add to inflation in a turn is now 3 points, down from 9. The most they can take away is 1.5 points, down from 2.25.
- Holding the prime rate below neutral now pushes inflation up twice as hard as before. Raising rates above neutral is unchanged.
