## What changed

- Taking a foreign-owned corporation or sector now raises world cold-war
  tension, once per taking. The tension ledger names both countries, so the
  incident is visible to everyone rather than only to the government that
  ordered it.
- The size follows what was paid. A fair-value buyout adds a little, a
  discounted taking more, and a seizure with no compensation the most. Taking a
  whole sector adds the sum of its individual takings.
- Domestic takings are unchanged, and no eligibility, compensation, or approval
  numbers moved in this change.
