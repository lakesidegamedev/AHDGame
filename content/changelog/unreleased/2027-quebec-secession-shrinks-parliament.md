## What changed

- When Quebec secedes, its 78 MPs leave the House of Commons and the chamber is counted as 265 seats. A government now needs 133 seats for a majority instead of the old 172.
- The Senate is counted province by province, so Quebec's 24 senators leave with it and the Senate shows 81 seats.
- The United Kingdom works as before: the Commons still shrinks when Scotland or Wales leaves, and the House of Lords is unchanged.
