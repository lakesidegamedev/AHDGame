## What changed

- A site that sells all its output but earns less per unit than it costs to make is trimmed a little each turn by the game. The sector page used to call this "Ramping up", which made it look like output was about to rise.
- It now shows "Trimmed: losing money", with a note on what you can do: raise the price you can sell for, cut input and wage costs, or accept the lower run.
