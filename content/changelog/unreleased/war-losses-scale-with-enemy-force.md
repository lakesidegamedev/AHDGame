## What changed

- In each battle, a side's casualties now scale with how much fighting power its enemy actually has at the front.
- Even fights are unchanged. The weaker side's losses are unchanged too.
- A much stronger attacker meeting a thin defence now loses a fraction of what it used to, down to a floor of one tenth of a normal battle's losses.
