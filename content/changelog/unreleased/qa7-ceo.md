## What changed

- A character on the same account as a corporation's CEO no longer sees that corporation's Manage tab, private financials or credit file, and can no longer list it for sale, abandon it, rename it, cancel builds, issue bonds or change budgets.
- The same applies to a parent corporation's CEO: only that character can direct its subsidiaries.
- A character that is not the CEO still gets the notice naming which of your characters runs the corporation, with a link to switch.
