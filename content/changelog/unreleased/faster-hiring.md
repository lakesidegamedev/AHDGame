title: Companies hire faster
summary: Companies now fill open jobs within a few turns when people are looking for work.
tags: [economy, labour]
badges: [minor]
areas: [backend]
---

- Companies now fill open jobs within a few turns when people in the state are looking for work. Before, only a small part of a company's open jobs was filled each turn, so plants stayed short of workers even where unemployment was high.
- Hiring is still limited by how many people are looking for work, so a state with very low unemployment can still run short.
