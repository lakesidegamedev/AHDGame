title: Private company profit uses the right currency
summary: Outside viewers of a private company now see profit converted from the company's home currency instead of as raw local amounts labelled in ₳ or dollars.
badges: [patch]
areas: [frontend]
tags: [corporations, currency, display]
---

When you look at a private company you do not run, sector profit is still visible, but it is now converted from the company's home currency (for example yen) into your display currency. Previously a missing currency stamp could show yen figures as if they were already ₳ or dollars.
