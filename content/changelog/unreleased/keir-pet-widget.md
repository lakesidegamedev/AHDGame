## What changed

- A new Keir widget sits at the top of the experimental side tray. Keir gives you
  one tip based on your own situation, the same suggestions as the Actions
  suggestions page, and writes a new one every 12 hours. When nothing stands out
  he shares a general tip instead.
- Keir is animated. He waves hello, reacts to his tip, hums and sips tea, dozes
  off when you leave the page alone or late at night, and wakes up when you
  come back.
- Pat Keir or make him tea to build your bond, shown as five hearts. Tea is
  ready every four hours. Visiting on consecutive days builds a streak.
