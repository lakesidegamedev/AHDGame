title: Optional Discord requirement for new characters
summary: Admins can require Discord linking for future character creation while existing characters remain playable.

---

The kill-switch board has a default-off Discord requirement for new characters. When enabled, accounts must link Discord before creating another character, including after a world reset. Existing characters remain playable without linking.

Registration labels Discord linking Optional or Required. Email registrants can link the new account immediately after signup, and the character creator prompts existing accounts to link without losing their draft.
