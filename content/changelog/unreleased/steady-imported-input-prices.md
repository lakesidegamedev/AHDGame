date: 2026-11-21
title: Prices for scarce imported inputs hold steady between turns
summary: >-
  A country's price for an input it mostly imports no longer swings up and down
  every turn, so companies that depend on it stop flipping between a full run
  and a short one.
tags: [market, corporations]
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine]
---

- A country's market price for a commodity now moves at most the normal pace
  each turn, like world and national prices already did. Before, it could jump
  by a third in one turn and fall back the next.
- Those swings changed how much companies could offer for scarce imported
  inputs, so some companies won their supplies one turn and lost them the next.
  Their output, revenue and share price flipped turn to turn as a result. They
  should now stay steady when nothing else changes.
