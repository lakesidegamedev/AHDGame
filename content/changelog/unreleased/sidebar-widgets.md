## What changed

- With experimental features on, tablets and desktops get a side tray on the same
  side as Ask Keir. Closed, it is a slim strip with a count of anything waiting.
  Open, it shows your widgets, and Ask Keir is a tab inside it.
- On very wide screens the open tray moves the page over instead of covering it.
- Widgets so far: My company (cash, share price, last turn's revenue and income),
  stock tickers for the global market, your home exchange or any other exchange,
  and review queues for staff.
- Choose, order and remove widgets from the tray's Edit button. You can also put
  one widget beside the links of each desktop menu, such as a ticker in World or
  review queues in Staff. Your layout saves to your account.
- Staff menu links now have their own icons.
