title: "Merger rules in Germany, Japan and China stay as passed"
summary: >-
  Worlds started before Germany, Japan and China moved to their new law books
  keep the Competition Act they already had, and new worlds no longer offer a
  second competition law that does nothing.
tags: [corporations, legislation]
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine]
---

Merger review in Germany, Japan and China now follows the competition law in
their new law books. In a world started before that move, the country keeps
running its existing Competition Act, at the level its legislature set, until
the new competition law is passed. Without this, those worlds would have
switched to the new law's default overnight and ignored any Competition Act
bill already passed.

New worlds no longer list the old Competition Act for these three countries,
since passing it would not have changed anything.
