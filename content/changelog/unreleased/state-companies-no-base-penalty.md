title: Well-run state companies are as efficient as private ones
summary: State-owned companies no longer pay a flat efficiency penalty. Corruption, weak governance, mandates and an oversized state sector still cost them.
tags: [economy, nationalization]
badges: [minor]
areas: [backend]
---

- State-owned companies used to lose at least 5 points of margin just for being state-owned, and most lost far more.
- That flat penalty is gone. A state company in a clean, well-governed country with no mandates now runs like a private one.
- Corruption, poor governance, price controls, guaranteed jobs and nationalising too much of the economy still reduce state company margins, up to the same limit as before.
