## What changed

- Price trend lines are now grey, with a coloured dot for the latest move, so a
  line never looks red next to a rising price.
- My race shows your vote share and the leader's as bars against the halfway
  mark. My office shows approval as a bar and the budget, growth and
  unemployment as tiles.
- Due soon marks each item by how soon it closes.
- The sector map in a desktop menu groups small sectors so every label fits.
