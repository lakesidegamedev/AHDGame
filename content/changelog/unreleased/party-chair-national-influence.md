title: "Party chairs build national recognition"
summary: >-
  National party chairs earn National Political Influence again, scaled by how
  organized their party is. CEO recognition is lower.
tags: [politics, parties, balance]
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine]
---

A national party chair earns up to 0.2 National Political Influence a turn.
The rate scales with the party's national organization and reaches the full
0.2 at organization 50, so the chair of a well-organized party ends up about as
well known as the head of a top-10 company. The chair of a party with almost no
organization earns almost nothing until the party builds it.

CEOs earn less: 0.2 a turn for the 10 largest listed corporations (was 0.3)
and 0.05 for other qualifying corporations (was 0.15).

Vice chairs, treasurers and state party chairs still earn only Party
Influence from their party office.
