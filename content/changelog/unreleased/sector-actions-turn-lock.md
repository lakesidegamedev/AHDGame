date: 2026-10-30
title: Building and plant changes wait for the turn to finish
summary: >-
  Building, expanding, converting or abandoning a plant is paused for the
  minute or so a turn takes to process, so a change cannot stop the turn.
tags: [corporations, turns]
badges: [patch]
areas: [backend]
---

If you try to build, expand, convert or abandon a plant while a turn is
processing, the game asks you to try again in a minute.
