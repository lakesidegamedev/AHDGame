## What changed

- An automatic plant picks its asking price from the prices buyers have said
  they will pay. When another company's top bid sat a fraction above the
  household limit, every plant in the market picked that slightly higher
  price. Households could not pay it, so they bought nothing from those plants
  and the food went unsold while shoppers went short.
- Behind a new switch, off until it is turned on: when two prices would earn
  within 1% of each other, the plant takes the one more buyers can pay.
- Manual and cost-plus prices set by players are not affected.
