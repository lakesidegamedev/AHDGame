title: Turn action refresh still grants after mid-turn spending
summary: Personal action-point grants at turn refresh no longer miss when you spend actions while the turn is processing.
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine]

Spending action points while a turn is resolving no longer cancels your personal turn grant (base, office and leadership bonuses). Party influence bonuses were already applied this way. Hoarding limits and energy caps are unchanged.
