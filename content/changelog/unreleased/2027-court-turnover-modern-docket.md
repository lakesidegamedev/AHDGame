## What changed

- From 2027, a seated justice with no scheduled departure can leave office at the same steady rate as a justice you confirmed (a median tenure of fifteen years). The seat opens for the President to fill.
- From 2027 the Court hears surprise cases about modern themes: algorithms and data, tenant screening, the grid, offshore wind, platforms, gig work, schooling and election rules.
- From 2027 a surprise case comes up about once every one and a half to two years, up from once every five.
- When every case for an era has been heard, a case can return twelve years after it was last heard.
- Worlds started from earlier dates keep their existing case rate.
