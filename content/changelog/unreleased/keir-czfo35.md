## What changed

- A resident's savings in the old currency are converted into a savings account
  in the new currency, with accrued interest and the opened status carried
  over. Before, the balance converted but the account did not, so the bank page
  asked the player to open a new one while the old account was left behind.
- Northern Ireland now borders Donegal, Midlands and Kildare for party
  growth once it is part of Ireland, so its residents can join Irish parties
  that are active in the border regions.
