## What changed

- Government transitions clear Canada's cabinet and restore ministers' elected offices.
- Legislative chair elections, Speaker removal motions, cabinet appointments, dismissals, resignations and reshuffles commit their related changes together.
- Cabinet offers from an outgoing government are withdrawn; accepting an offer cannot leave a nominee without their bank office after an interrupted write.
- Acting minister expiry, Supreme Court confirmations and Federal Reserve board appointments recover without losing office or career records or restarting confirmed terms.
- Bill transitions retain durable receipts, so a later interruption cannot lose earlier enactments from phase totals or political effects. Same-turn retries do not apply legitimacy, confidence or regime effects twice.
