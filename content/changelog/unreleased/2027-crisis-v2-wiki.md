## What changed

- The Crises page covers what a crisis is, the four stages, what raises and lowers severity, the do-nothing forecast, the limit on how many crises run at once, spread between countries and the crises page.
- A new Crisis Responses page covers which office can pull which response, what a response costs in treasury, company cash, approval and actions, the fast-track emergency bill, and the Who did what record.
- The Global Response Crises page now points at World Crises, where the conflicts page moved.
- The unused decision-flow diagram from the old page is removed.
