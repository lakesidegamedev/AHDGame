title: Bulgarian founding runoffs keep their candidates
summary: >-
  A cleanup step was removing the first-round candidates of Bulgaria's founding
  election before its second round could open. The runoff then had nobody on the
  ballot, never settled, and kept the world stuck in its founding phase.
tags: [bugfix, elections, bulgaria]
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine]
---

Bulgarian founding races now keep their candidates until the national count is
seated. Once it is, the founding phase can end and the calendar starts.
