## What changed

- A vote to remove a chair, vice chair, treasurer, committee member or campaigner stays open for at least 24 hours from when it was proposed. It cannot pass before then, even if enough members vote yes sooner.
- Once the 24 hours are up, the vote passes as soon as enough members still vote yes. If it does not have enough yes votes when voting closes, it fails as before.
- The proposal card shows the earliest time a removal vote can pass, in your local time.
- The person being removed is told when the vote opens, including the 24 hour minimum.
- Committee members can withdraw their vote on any open proposal, or change it, until the proposal closes.
- The yes votes needed on a removal vote now leave out the person being removed, matching how the vote is counted.
