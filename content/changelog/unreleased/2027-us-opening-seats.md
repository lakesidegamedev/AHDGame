## What changed

- The US House opens with 256 Democratic and 179 Republican seats, state by state, following the races called after the November 2026 midterms.
- The US Senate opens with 54 Democratic-aligned and 46 Republican senators, so Democrats hold the Senate outright.
- Governors now follow the 2026 results: 29 Democratic and 21 Republican.
- State senates now use the newest available party split in each state instead of 2020 numbers.
- A few races were still being counted when this was set. Those seats keep the party the earlier projection or the sitting chamber had, and will be corrected once results are certified.
- Every seat holder is still a made-up politician. Only the party split comes from the real elections.
- The 2027 president and vice president are unchanged, and worlds started from any other date, and the live world, behave exactly as before.
