## What changed

- Only the seated or acting holder of a cabinet seat can perform that office's
  actions. A vacant or machine-held seat leaves the action unavailable until
  someone is appointed.
- This covers trade embargoes, the foreign-affairs actions and the finance
  ministry's National Corporation management.
- The head of government keeps their own powers, including war, peace, setting
  the rate when the bank is under government control, and nationalization.
