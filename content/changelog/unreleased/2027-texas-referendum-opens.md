## What changed

- Texas joins Quebec and Alberta as a region that can leave its country through the referendum process: the Governor requests, the President grants or declines, a campaign runs, and the state votes.
- A passed vote goes to Congress as a consent bill that must clear the House, the Senate and the President's desk. Once enacted, Texas becomes an independent country with a 150-seat House of Representatives, a Chief Minister and six regions.
- The US House loses Texas's seats, the Senate loses two, and the Electoral College loses Texas's electors.
- Texas Independence Desire still has to reach 60 before the Governor can ask, and it starts at 32.
