title: Party offices grant Party Influence

  National and state party officers earn Party Influence through their party roles.
  Their party offices no longer grant passive National Political Influence.
tags: [politics, parties, balance]

This applies during and after the founding elections. Party officers who also hold
a position that grants National Political Influence still earn that position's bonus.
