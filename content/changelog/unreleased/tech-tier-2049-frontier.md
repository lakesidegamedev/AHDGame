## What changed

- A new research tier opens in 2049 and stays current from then on. Main nodes cost 75 R&D score, specializations 150 and capstones 225.
- Every sector has a full 2049 Corporate and Sector lane, including specializations and capstones.
- Five breakthrough production methods, each unlocked by a 2049 Sector node:
  - Energy: Commercial Fusion.
  - Extraction: Space Resources.
  - Healthcare: Gene Therapy at Scale.
  - Real estate: Autonomous Districts.
  - Technology: Neural Interfaces.
- A breakthrough also needs research across its whole sector. Every company in the sector together must have spent enough R&D score on research from the 2039 tier onward before anyone can build it, so a breakthrough can arrive early in the tier, late, or not at all. The strategy picker shows "awaiting a research breakthrough" until then.
- None of the breakthrough methods is free. Each uses existing goods and carries a heavy input bill.
- Frontier programmes: once a company owns all nine core 2049 technologies in its track, it can fund repeatable programmes from the Tech tab. Each adds 0.4% plant output or cuts plant inputs by 0.6% for the primary sector, costs 15% more than the last, and stays inside the existing output and input caps. Programme effects never expire.
- Retired methods:
  - Conventional power retires in 2039. Successors: Advanced Fission or Renewables.
  - Standard combustion vehicle manufacturing retires in 2049. Successors: EV Focus or Autonomous Vehicles.
  - A retired method is closed to new sites and to retools. New sites in that sector start on the first successor the company can run.
  - Sites already on a retired method keep running, but their inputs cost 1.5% more for every year since retirement, up to 15%.
  - Switching a retired site to one of its successors costs 60% less than a normal retool. AI-run companies convert their own sites automatically.
- Worlds before 2039 see no change. Retirement and breakthroughs apply only where tech trees are on.
