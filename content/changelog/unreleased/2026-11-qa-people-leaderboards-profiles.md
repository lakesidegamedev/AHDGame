## What changed

- The national influence line on your profile now says the leader shown is the leader of your own country, and its tooltip spells out how the per-turn gain is worked out.
- Switching the leaderboard to Net worth no longer shows the old career rows with the wrong values while it loads.
- The leaderboard now explains Lives (characters a player has created) and Achievements (different achievements earned by any of the player's characters), and the party column is called Party influence.
- A character's achievements panel shows both that character's count and the account-wide count the leaderboard uses.
- The new-player banner and checklist stop appearing on characters older than 30 days.
- The character switcher lists each character's office, national influence and actions.
- Search suggests close name matches when you misspell a player, and says which word matched when a result only contains your search inside a longer word.
- The wiki glossary now defines Life and says there is no Prestige Points stat.
