## What changed

- There are 15 sectors. Founding forms, sector lists, wiki pages, index funds and union lists no longer offer Automobiles or Entertainment.
- Manufacturing plants now produce vehicles alongside steel and building materials. Vehicle Assembly and Trucks and Heavy Machinery remain as retool options for plants that want to build only vehicles.
- Media covers everything entertainment used to: studios, venues and streaming now run as media recipes, and the film and music product lines are retired.
- Where a country's economy used to give weight to Automobiles or Entertainment, that weight now sits with Manufacturing or Media.
- Mines founded by AI corporations are sized by how much of what they dig up is actually short in the market, not by how much ore the ground still holds. Existing mines add capacity only when the metals and fuels they sell are still in short supply and the mine as a whole is selling most of its output. Remaining deposit still caps how far a mine can grow.
- The 2027 world is reset when this ships, so no stored companies are migrated.
