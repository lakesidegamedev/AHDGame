title: "Smoother first hour for new characters"
summary: >-
  Clicks during a turn now confirm clearly instead of looking lost, filing for
  a race shows what it costs and whether you qualify before you commit, and the
  tutorial, wiki and second-character flow tell new players the truth.
tags: [onboarding, elections, tutorial, wiki, accounts]
badges: [minor]
areas: [fullstack]
---

While a turn is processing, actions take a few seconds to confirm. The Actions
page and the first-days panel now say so, and the first-days campaign counter
moves the moment a click lands, so nobody clicks twice and pays twice.

Before you file for a race you now see a full summary: filing is free, which
eligibility rules you pass or fail, who else is running and who holds the seat,
when filing and the race close, and what withdrawing costs. If you fail a rule,
the button says why. A race whose filing has closed says when it closed and
points you to the races still open at home.

The tutorial resumes at the step you left, gives the real starting donor level,
and no longer promises a small local seat as your first race: filing is only
open during a race's primary window, so it points you to what is open now. The
wiki's starter numbers now explain that campaign funds scale with the world's
era (about 345,000 in a 2027 world).

The national Economy page no longer hangs on its loading placeholders if the
server is slow; it retries and offers a Try again button.

On accounts with more than one character, each character now has its own
inbox, alerts, achievements and join date, instead of showing the other
character's. The welcome mail is sent once per character, and staff accounts
can start a new character from the character switcher.
