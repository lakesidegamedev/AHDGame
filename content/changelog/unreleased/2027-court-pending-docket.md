## What changed

- A surprise case no longer rules the same week it appears. It shows as pending on the docket and is heard between twelve and thirty-six weeks later.
- The ruling follows the justices sitting on the day of the hearing, not the day of filing, so a vacancy filled in between can change the result.
- Curated historical cases and crisis challenges are unchanged.
