## What changed

- From 2027, parliaments in Germany, Japan, China and Ireland can pass an AI Liability and Transparency Act, a Data Protection Overhaul, Housing Supply and Zoning Reform, a Critical Minerals Stockpile Act, a Cyber Resilience Mandate and a Platform Content Law.
- From 2030 they can also pass an Automation Tax, a Wage Insurance Act, a Carbon Border Adjustment, Heat Labour Standards, a Pension Age Indexation Act and Nuclear Licensing Reform.
- Each law runs from a strong version on one side, through "no statute", to a strong version on the other. Every choice has winners and losers: building more homes helps renters and young people but lowers home values, a robot tax protects jobs but slows investment, and a fixed pension age protects older workers while costing the budget more every year.
- New laws start with no statute in force, so nothing changes in a country until a bill passes. Laws that open in 2030 cannot be proposed before then.
- Governing parties run by the game can sponsor the new laws too.
- Worlds that start before 2027 are unchanged until their calendar reaches these years.
