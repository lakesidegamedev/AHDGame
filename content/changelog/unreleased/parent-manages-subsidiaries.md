date: 2026-10-30
title: Parent companies can retool and rebrand their AI-run subsidiaries
summary: >-
  If your company controls a subsidiary that an AI manager runs, you can now
  change that subsidiary's sector strategies and its logo yourself, and the AI
  manager will not undo your strategy choice for 48 turns.
tags: [corporations, subsidiaries]
badges: [minor]
areas: [fullstack]
---

- The CEO of the controlling parent company can change the strategy of any
  sector of an AI-run subsidiary. It works exactly like a CEO retooling their
  own sector: the subsidiary pays the same retool cost, and the same
  transition time and cooldown apply.
- After the parent picks a strategy, the subsidiary's AI manager leaves that
  sector's strategy alone for 48 turns, so your choice is not reversed as soon
  as the cooldown ends.
- The parent's CEO can also upload a new logo for the subsidiary.
- On the subsidiary's company and sector pages you will see a short "Managed
  by" note with these controls. The Directions tab of your group page links to
  each AI-run subsidiary's sectors.
- Only the CEO of the company that controls more than half of the votes in a
  formal subsidiary can do this. Other shareholders cannot. A subsidiary with
  its own player CEO stays in that CEO's hands.
