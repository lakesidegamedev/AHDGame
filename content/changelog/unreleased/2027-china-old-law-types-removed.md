## What changed

- The older Chinese law list no longer seeds into new worlds. China uses its law book, plus the provincial resource tax lever the book does not have.
- The provincial budgets now read the corporate tax rate from the China book.
- The minimum wage floor follows the China worker security law.
- Opening tax rates for 1979, 1991 and the modern presets and opening program spending are unchanged. The 1953 opening uses the China book's tax rates.
