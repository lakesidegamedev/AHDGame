## What changed

- The older Japanese law list no longer seeds into new worlds. Japan uses its law book, plus the prefectural resident tax and fixed asset tax levers the book does not have.
- Electorate priorities, the minimum wage floor and the reset tax menu now point at laws in the Japan book.
- Opening tax rates and opening program spending for Japan are unchanged in every preset.
- Worlds without the old prefectural grant law fund prefectures from the national state grants line.
