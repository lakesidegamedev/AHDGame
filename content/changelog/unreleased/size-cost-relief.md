- The running cost for dominating a market is switched off.
- Together, sprawl, management overhead and the dominance penalty take at most 7.5 points from a sector's margin. Each shrinks by the same share, so the breakdown still shows where the cost comes from.
- Marketing strength now fades by 1.5% a turn instead of 3%.
