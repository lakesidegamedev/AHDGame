## What changed

- Election pages for legislative races show the whole chamber: a seat arc in party colours with the majority line, and a table of votes, vote share, seats, seat change and swing against the chamber's previous election. If a government formed after the vote, it is listed.
- Presidential election pages list the winner, share, margin and electoral votes for every state.
- Primary results show delegates won, where the primary awards them.
- Party pages show a seat history for each chamber the party has contested: a line chart and a table with seat change and vote share at every election.
- Wiki page titles and generated text no longer use long dashes.
