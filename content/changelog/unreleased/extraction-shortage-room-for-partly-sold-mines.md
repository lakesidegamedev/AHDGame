date: 2026-10-17
title: Extraction mines that sell part of their output can grow into a shortage
summary: A mineral mine that sells more than half of what it digs can now claim room from buyers nobody is serving, instead of being held to last turn's sales.
tags: [extraction, market, supply]
badges: [minor]
areas: [engine]
---

- A mineral mine that sells only part of its output has been allowed, since the shortage-allowance change, to grow toward unmet demand in the market it sells into. For a mine in an exporting country that allowance always read as zero, because the country's own book is balanced by its exports however short its foreign buyers are.
- That gap is closed: the allowance now also counts the foreign buyers this country is not serving, at the same measured share per turn as the sold-out allowance already uses.
- Mines that sell nothing, or almost nothing, are unaffected. The change is small and gradual, and re-reads each turn what the mine actually shipped.
