## What changed

Some departments had an account but no law that could put money in it, so they sat at zero. Each now has a national funding law with five options, from a small budget to a large one:

- **Intelligence service funding** (US, Canada, UK, Japan). The money goes to the intelligence appropriation that pays for operations on the Intelligence tab.
- **Diplomacy and foreign aid** (US, Canada, UK, Japan): the foreign ministry, embassies and development aid.
- **Homeland and public safety** (US, Canada, UK, Japan): border protection, emergency management and national safety agencies.
- **Trade and export promotion** (US, Canada, UK, Japan): trade missions, export advice and export credit.
- **Veterans services** (US, UK, Japan): veterans health care, benefits and reintegration.

Each starts at "no dedicated law" in the current world, so nothing changes until a bill passes. They are national laws only.
