Commodity pages include regional supply, demand and prices from economy-visible countries. Hidden countries remain hidden.

NPP management and organization building offer x1, x5 and x10 controls. Repeated actions keep existing costs, stat caps, permissions and pressure pricing, stop when the next action is blocked, and report partial completion. National HQ can repeat organization builds in each selected region.

Sovereign bond listings, detail pages and the trade modal show the issuer's current credit rating, crisis state and last recorded default. Missing issuer data is labeled unavailable. Speculative ratings are identified explicitly.
