title: "New production methods take time to build and carry a one-off cost"
summary: >-
  Switching to a late-game method now keeps the old method running until the new
  one is built, and adds the method's downside to the switching bill.
tags: [corporations, research]
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine, backend]
---

The build time recorded on late-game methods is now enforced. When you switch a
sector to one, the sector keeps running its old method until the build time has
passed, then the usual phase-in starts. Cancelling during the build costs nothing
and returns the sector to its old method at once.

Each method's downside is also priced. Adopting it adds a one-off charge, as a
share of the sector's daily revenue, on top of the normal retooling fee. Waivers
on the retooling fee do not cover it.
