## What changed

- A new Due soon widget lists what is waiting on you with a deadline, soonest
  first: bill and veto override votes in your chamber or state legislature,
  bills waiting for your signature or veto as president or governor, company
  and privatization votes in companies you hold shares in, party leadership
  ballots, and confirmation votes on nominations.
- Each row shows what to do, how many turns are left, and links straight to the
  page where you act.
- It sits first in the side tray by default, and its count shows on the closed
  tray. Players who already customised their tray can add it from Edit.
