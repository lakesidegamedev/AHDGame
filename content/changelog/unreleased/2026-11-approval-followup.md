## What changed

- Event and crisis approval changes respect the same positive cap before and after a turn.
- Regional histories keep country-qualified identities, existing history and retry receipts, and unclamped scores.
- Approval breakdowns explain smoothing and bounds. Fresh-world national pages include national fiscal effects.
- Election coattails include local budget, address and fairness effects before the first snapshot.
