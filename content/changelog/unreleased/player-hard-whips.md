## What changed

- Player whips are advisory on every vote type across national and state/regional chambers in every country.
- Hard player whip requests are rejected, including requests that omit the mode.
- The bill page sends advisory player whips and keeps hard NPP whips available.
- Existing hard-whip history and vote-revert snapshots remain available; old hard whips do not consume the advisory attempt for any target.
