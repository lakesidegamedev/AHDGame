## What changed

- Each turn, Canada's regional figures are rolled up into national ones, such as GDP growth, as they are for the other player countries. An independent Quebec gets the same after it secedes.
- A computer-run Bank of Canada chair now sets rates from Canada's measured growth instead of assuming the economy is on target.
- Computer-run governments in Canada and Quebec can now see how their economy is doing when they choose what to do.
