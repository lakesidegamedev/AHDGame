## What changed

- After a state's own hospitals have served its patients, any healthcare demand left over is offered to hospitals in bordering states of the same country.
- Hospitals in states with little local demand now sell more of their output, which shortens their payback.
- Patients never pay more than their local limit, only one border is crossed, and nothing crosses a national border.
