## What changed

- Each background country in a 2027 world (Ireland, Brazil, Nigeria, Russia, Poland, Hungary, Romania, Bulgaria, France, Italy, Spain, Sweden, Turkey, Austria, Finland and Greece) now starts as a whole economy built from its 2027 population, GDP, industry mix and natural resources.
- The traded part of that economy enters its own national market. American goods can sell into French or Brazilian demand, and their surpluses can compete in the United States, subject to the usual tariffs, embargoes and shipping.
- Background economies grow at their own long-run rate each year. Wars, sanctions and crises cut their output and slow that growth until they pass.
- Ireland no longer appears among the 2027 globe's economic powers; it is a background country like the other fifteen.
- Worlds started from any other date, and the live world, behave exactly as before.
