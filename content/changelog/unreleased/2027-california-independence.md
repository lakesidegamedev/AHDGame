## What changed

- California joins Texas as a US state that can leave through the referendum process: the Governor requests, the President grants or declines, a campaign runs, and the state votes.
- A new minor party, the Bear Flag Independence Party, exists in the 2027 world. California's independence mood starts at 28, well below the 60 the Governor needs to ask.
- An independent California would have a Premier, an 80-seat Assembly elected across six regions (Los Angeles, the Bay Area, San Diego and Orange, the Inland Empire, the Central Valley, and Sacramento and the North), a Regional Council and Regional Commissioners.
- California keeps the US dollar and the Federal Reserve. The US House, Senate and Electoral College shrink by California's share when it leaves.
