## What changed

- Canada's cabinet page lists the federal ministers, from the Deputy Prime Minister to the Government House Leader, and lets the Prime Minister appoint and dismiss them.
- If Quebec becomes independent, the Premier gets the same page for the Executive Council of Quebec.
