## What changed

- The modifiers you could read on a sector but that changed nothing (home location, sector fit, state specialisation, economic model fit, strategy changes, tariffs, export premium, expropriation risk) now move the revenue the sector sold. Each one is a percentage of sales, up or down.
- Local disasters, regional conditions and strikes cut revenue in the affected state.
- The monopoly charge is back as a cut to sales. Holding more than half of a state's sector costs up to 3.75% of sales on sites in that state, and holding more than 30% of a country's sector costs up to 3.75% on all your sites in that country. Both rise in a straight line from their threshold to 100%, and the two add up.
- Dominance cost is back as higher running costs. Past half of a sector in a state, your sites in that state cost up to 3.75% more to run, rising in a straight line from 50% to 100%. It applies in that state only and shows as its own "Dominance cost" line.
- Building more capacity while you dominate a state costs up to 15% more at 100% share (it was up to 3 times as much).
- Group size management overhead is charged again on funded sectors.
- All the revenue effects together, monopoly charge included, are capped at 30% of a sector's sales, up or down.
- Subsidies are paid by the government and appear as their own "Subsidy received" line on sector and company finances. They are not counted as sales.
- Each sector shows the effects that applied this turn by name and in money, so sales plus effects add up to total revenue.
- Inflation, national debt and deficit are labelled "already in prices". Prices carry them, so they no longer pose as margin effects.
