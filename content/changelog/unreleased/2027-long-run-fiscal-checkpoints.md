## What changed

- Budget tables from 2027 to 2050 are generated for the five biggest economies, with checks that policy changes move them and that no country is left paying more than half its revenue in interest without a restructuring.
- A new test covers a bond maturity wall that lands exactly on a fiscal year change, and confirms debt and cash stay balanced.
- No change to how the game plays today.
