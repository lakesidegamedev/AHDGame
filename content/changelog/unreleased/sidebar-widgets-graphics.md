## What changed

- Stock tickers show a small price trend for each company over recent turns,
  with a green or red bar for the direction of the last move.
- My company now charts share price and cash over recent turns, plus a bar for
  each turn's income, green for profit and red for a loss.
- New: Sector map. Tiles for each sector on an exchange, sized by market cap and
  colored by how prices moved since last turn. Pick the global market, your
  home exchange or any other exchange.
- New: Net worth. Your net worth now, the change since last turn, and its trend.
- New: Commodity prices. Eight key commodities with price, change and trend.
  Each links to its commodity page. Players without a company get it in their
  tray by default.
