## What changed

- **Sixteen new crises, each with a start and an end year.** Heat domes, megafire seasons, megadroughts, water rationing, grid emergencies, a rare severe solar storm, orbital debris outages, a housing crash when cheap mortgages reset, a pension funding cliff, a run on a tech lender, a climate insurance retreat, a chip plant fire, an AI model failure scandal, a port workers strike and a depot lockout over automation. Every one gives leaders a choice, a shared recovery fund and a real outcome. Themes that fade, such as the housing reset or the chip fire, stop appearing after their years. Every year from 2027 to 2050 has at least six to draw from.
- **Thirty new personal events**, ten each for the 2020s, 2030s and 2040s. They only appear in their own decade: AI assistants at work, robotaxis, lab-grown meat, insurers pulling out, fusion plants next door, Mars shares and more. Some are for officeholders or company chiefs only.
- **Six shared historic moments.** Everyone gets the same one-time event: the return to the Moon, a hundred years of the World Cup, the first commercial fusion attempt, departure for Mars, a hundred years since 1945, and the half-century mark in 2050.
- **New country stories for the UK, Germany, Japan and China.** Three each, set from 2027 onward, covering heat networks, balcony solar, empty houses, megaquake advisories, group-buy chats and electric car price wars.
