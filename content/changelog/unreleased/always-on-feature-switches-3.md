## What changed

- Conflicts is a permanent part of the game. The World menu always links to the Conflicts pages, and foreign affairs, intelligence, doctrine, peace offers, truces and war declarations no longer depend on a world setting.
- Character stats, international bloc alignment and the Influence tab, subsidiary corporations, US House districts and detailed demographic positions were already on in the live world and in every new world, so play is unchanged for them.
- Single-player setup no longer offers these as toggles. They are always on in local worlds too.
- The admin feature gates panel, the corporations panel, the world admin tab and the Conflicts admin page no longer show switches for them.
