## What changed

- The intelligence office has a Cyber operations panel for the person who directs the service. Pick any country, see the chance the operation gets through and the chance you are named, see the cost, and launch.
- The panel lists active truces and recent operations by and against your country. A sponsor is named only when the target traced the operation. You can stand down from any country to open a truce.
- Full countries run by the game (such as the UK, Germany, Japan and China) now probe the rival bloc leader when their leader is hawkish and the odds favour them, and stand down when the leader is dovish or tension falls. Each government is limited to two operations in a long stretch, with a gap between them, and pays from its intelligence appropriation.
- A country with a player directing its intelligence service is never run by the game for cyber operations.
