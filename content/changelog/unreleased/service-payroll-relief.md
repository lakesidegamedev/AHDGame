title: Lower staff costs for technology, finance, healthcare and real estate
summary: Wages for these four industries drop back halfway to where they were before 9 October, while a wider rebalance of business costs is prepared.
tags: [corporations, economy, labour]
badges: [minor]

- Technology and healthcare businesses now spend about 35 instead of 40 parts in every 100 of income on staff, finance about 33 instead of 40, and real estate about 21 instead of 32 (before the era adjustment).
- This is a temporary step: it lifts healthcare and real estate margins straight away, and it will be replaced by a full rebalance of business costs.
- Nothing changes for other industries.
