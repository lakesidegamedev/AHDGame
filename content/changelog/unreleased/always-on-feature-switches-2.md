## What changed

- Crisis decisions, crisis aid bills, scheduled world events, automatic sector seeding, the new-player onboarding checklist, the end-of-season recap, legislation shifting voter leanings and turnout, macro growth convergence and settlement crises are now permanent parts of the game.
- These systems were already switched on in the live world and in every new world, so play is unchanged.
- Single-player setup no longer offers these as toggles. They are always on in local worlds too.
- The admin feature gates panel, the crises admin page, the sector seed panel and the German question admin page no longer show switches for them.
