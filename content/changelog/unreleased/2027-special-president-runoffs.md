## What changed

- A special presidential election in France, Romania or Russia (2027) now has two rounds. A candidate with more than half the votes wins outright. Otherwise the top two go to a short runoff and the larger vote wins.
- The winner takes office like any other president: the previous cabinet is cleared and the win counts as an elected term.
- When a ruling party picks a new leader, that leader starts with fresh party confidence instead of inheriting the old leader's standing.
- Votes by a legislature or ruling party only ever pick computer-run politicians, never a player.
- One-party executive pages now show who is acting while the top office is vacant.
- Russia's 1991 presidency says plainly that it is filled through its own constitutional ballot, with no special election scheduled.
