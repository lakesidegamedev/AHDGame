## What changed

- Leaders and heads of government retire in the stretch just before their term ends, at the same overall rate as before. Very old leaders (85 and over) can still step down mid-term, and a death can happen at any time.
- When a head of government or national leader dies or retires, the news feed posts the departure and their party seats a successor.
- Backbench retirements are unchanged and post no news.
- Worlds without NPP turnover switched on behave exactly as before.
