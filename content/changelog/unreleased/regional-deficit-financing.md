## What changed

- Removed the annual funding shortfall block on regional bill signatures.
- Authorized regional services can be financed through borrowing instead of automatic spending cuts.
- Regional budgets show debt and annual interest payments. Surpluses repay debt before building cash reserves.
- Regional debt carries 5% annual interest. Approval falls by one point for each 10% of GDP in debt, capped at 25 points.
- Negative accumulated state cash is carried into debt on the first settlement. Turn retries do not repeat borrowing or interest.
