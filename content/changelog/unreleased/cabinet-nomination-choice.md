## What changed

- A nominee who wins the Senate vote but cannot take the seat because of a
  central bank office is no longer rejected without a say. They get an offer to
  take the cabinet seat, which stands down the bank office, or to stay where
  they are.
- An unanswered offer lapses after three turns, the seat stays vacant and the
  President can nominate again.
- Both the nominee and the President are told when an offer is made, accepted,
  declined or allowed to lapse.
