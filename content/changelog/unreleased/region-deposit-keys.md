  real deposits had none either.

Bulgaria's coal and timber were listed under region names the 2027 map does
not use, so no Bulgarian region could mine anything. They now sit in the
southeast (Maritsa lignite) and southwest (Pernik coal, Rila and Pirin
forests). Łódź now has the Bełchatów lignite, Hungary's Great Plain has its oil
and gas, and Dobrogea has Black Sea gas.

A check now fails if a resource entry names a region the map does not have.
