---
date: 2026-10-31
title: Loans and buyouts count money the way it really is
summary: >-
  Credit checks no longer count income from before an economy reset or treat
  borrowed bank money as wealth, and a company can no longer be bought for
  less than the cash it holds.
tags: [economy, banking, corporations]
badges: [patch]
areas: [backend]
---

- Bank loans and credit lines judge your income only on turns since the last
  economy reset, averaged over the turns that have actually passed.
- Your credit line limit now subtracts what you owe on bank loans, the same
  way it already subtracted credit line debt.
- Buying a player-run company now costs at least the cash and assets it
  holds, and a company founded or floated this turn cannot be bought until
  the next turn.
- When a buyout includes shares held by the stock market, the market is paid
  for them instead of the government treasury.
