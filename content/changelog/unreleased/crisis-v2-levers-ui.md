## What changed

- Crisis cards on the Crises page and the Actions page show where the crisis is (early warning, onset, peak, aftermath), its severity with a small history line, and a projection of how bad it gets if nobody acts.
- If your office or company can respond, the card lists each response with its real cost (treasury money, company cash, approval, actions) and how long before you can use it again. Pulling one asks you to confirm first, and any refusal is shown on the card.
- Legislature responses raise an emergency bill instead of acting directly.
- The crisis page has a "Who did what" log: for each turn, which office holder used which response, or took no action, and how severe the crisis was then.
- Crises that leave lasting damage list it, with how long it takes to heal. Nothing is shown when there is none.
- The count next to Crises in the World menu now includes crises where you have a response available.
- Crises that have not moved to the new engine look and work as before.
