- A new admin tool can rebuild every company, market and bank from the era seed while keeping parties, laws, budgets, elections and the game calendar as they are.
- If it is ever used, every character's personal cash returns to the amount they started with, campaign funds stay, and national debt stays with each treasury.
- The tool reviews the world first and only runs against the exact plan an operator approved, and the world stays paused afterwards until it has been checked.
