## What changed

- Texas has its own country definition (the Republic of Texas), kept out of every era and invisible to players until it secedes.
- An independent Texas would have a Chief Minister, a 150-seat House of Representatives elected across six regions (North Texas, Houston and the Gulf Coast, Central Texas, South Texas, West Texas and the Panhandle, and East Texas), a Regional Council and Regional Commissioners.
- Texas keeps the US dollar and the Federal Reserve.
- When Texas does leave, the US House shrinks by its seats, the Senate by two, and the Electoral College by its electors.
- Referendum requests for Texas remain closed; nothing can pass into the new country yet.
