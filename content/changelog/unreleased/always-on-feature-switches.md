## What changed

- Granular poll breakdowns, the granular electorate in primaries, corporate deals, intelligence operations and military sabotage effects, computer-run countries starting and joining offensives, department finance, law administration, regional legislation finance, extraction strategy adoption, live election results and the trade-exposure embargo model are now permanent parts of the game.
- These systems were already switched on in the live world and in every new world, so play is unchanged.
- Single-player setup no longer offers these as toggles. They are always on in local worlds too.
- The admin feature gates panel and the conflicts admin page no longer show switches for them.
- A region's political metrics page now applies regional law delivery the same way the turn does.
