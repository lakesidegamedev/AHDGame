## What changed

- Aid pledges on the crisis response list show a size slider from a small share
  up to the crisis's cap, with the amount in your own currency beside it.
- The size you pick is what gets pledged. Leave it alone and the default share
  is used, as before.
- Pledges above the cap are still refused and the response is refunded.
