title: "Sign-in and sign-up pages match the rest of the site"
summary: >-
  The sign-in and create-account pages now use the site's own button styles,
  and on sign-up the Create account button is the clear main action.
tags: [ui, accounts]
badges: [patch]
areas: [frontend]
---

The Lakeside, Apple and Discord buttons on the sign-in and create-account pages
used solid white, black and blue fills seen nowhere else in the game. They now
use the same card-style buttons as the other sign-in options, with each
service's logo for recognition.

On the create-account page, the Discord and Apple buttons no longer outshine
the form. Create account is the one filled button, and the quick sign-up
options sit beside it as secondary choices. Nothing changes about how any
sign-in method works.
