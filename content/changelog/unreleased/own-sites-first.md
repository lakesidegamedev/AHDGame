## What changed

- Your sites that use a product you make are now served from your own output
  before any outside buyer. Before, whether your own site got supplied first
  depended on the order buyers happened to be processed in, so an outside
  buyer could take everything while your own site ran short.
- Your output goes to your nearest site first: one in the same state, then
  one in the same country, then one abroad.
- Supply agreements you signed still come first.
- This is switched on separately once it is live, and this note will say so.
