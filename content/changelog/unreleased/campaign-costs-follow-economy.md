## What changed

- Regions used to be measured against a fixed national average from the start of the world. As the whole economy grew over a long game, every region drifted to the highest or lowest setting.
- Income and action costs now use the current national average, so a richer region stays moderately richer than a poorer one for the whole game.
- A fresh world starts with exactly the same numbers as before.
