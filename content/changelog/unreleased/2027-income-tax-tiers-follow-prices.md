## What changed

- A player's tax tier compares their yearly earnings with the national median. The median grows with real wages only, so rising prices used to push people up through the tiers over the years.
- Earnings are now adjusted for the country's accumulated price level before the comparison, both when tax is assessed and when tax is withheld at the source.
- Worlds where prices are not tracked behave exactly as before.
