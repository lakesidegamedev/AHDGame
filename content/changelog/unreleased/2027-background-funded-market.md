## What changed

- Each background country in a 2027 world now has its own trading account in its own currency. Every turn it offers the traded part of what its economy makes and bids for the traded part of what it needs.
- American companies can sell to these countries and buy from them in the ordinary market, at market prices. Sales and purchases are paid through the normal money system and currency exchange.
- Embargoes and closed trade routes stop this trade, the same as for any other buyer or seller. Local services such as healthcare, property and haulage stay at home.
- A background economy follows its real trade: if its exports stop selling, its industry slowly shrinks; if they keep selling out, it slowly grows.
- Worlds started from any other date, and the live world, behave exactly as before.
