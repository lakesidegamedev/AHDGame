When a sector is short of workers, the shortage note under Workforce now shows:

- How many workers are available in the state.
- How many workers all employers in the state want to hire.
- Your pay compared with the state average, when wages are on.
- The share of your jobs you can fill at your current pay. Staffing moves toward it a little each turn.

Every employer in a state hires from the same pool. Raising pay only wins you a bigger share compared with other employers there; it does not add workers. The build capacity dialog repeats the pool size when the state is already short.
