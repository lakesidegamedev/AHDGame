date: 2026-10-26
title: Coalition chairs can nominate any coalition MP for Prime Minister
summary: >-
  A party chair who also chairs a coalition can now nominate a Prime Minister
  from any of the coalition's parties, not only from their own party.
tags: [government, coalitions, parliament]
badges: [patch]
areas: [backend]
---

When a party chair also chaired a coalition and their own party was large enough
to bid alone, the nomination list only offered their own party's MPs. The
coalition now takes precedence whenever it qualifies, so MPs from every partner
party appear in the list. A party-only bid is still used when the coalition does
not reach the seat threshold.
