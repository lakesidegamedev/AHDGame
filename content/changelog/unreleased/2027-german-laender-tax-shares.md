Each German Land is funded mostly by its share of national income tax and VAT.
After Germany moved to its new law book, those shares read the rate from a tax
bill, and until such a bill passed they came out as zero, leaving every Land on
the federal grant alone. The shares now use the national budget's income tax
and VAT rates whenever no bill has set them, the same rates the budget page
shows.
