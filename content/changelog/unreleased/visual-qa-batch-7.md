## What changed

- On the US map, Rhode Island, Connecticut, New Jersey, Delaware and Washington DC are labelled out in the ocean with a short line to the state, so the north-east labels no longer pile up.
- Party and coalition cards say "No Discord server" when there is no invite.
- Discipline watch rows always name an office, "No office" when there is none.
- The Build Organization share dilution list names every party, including in the preview before you build.
- The presidential field table explains what Actions and Levels count, right under the table.
