title: Make the grouped cards navigation your own
summary: >-
  Players trying experimental features with the grouped cards layout can pin
  destinations to the top bar, reorder each menu, and reach settings, Ask Keir,
  themes and sign out from a new gear button.
tags: [navigation, settings, experimental, desktop]
badges: [minor]
areas: [fullstack]

On wide screens the bar now spans the window, with the logo at the far left.
Hover a destination in any dropdown and click its star to pin it, or open
**Edit navigation** from the gear to drag destinations into your own order.
Your layout is saved to your account. The gear also lets you hide the Ask Keir
tab, which can be turned back on there or under Settings.
