## What changed

- Regional laws passed as bills now shift the lean of the voter groups they touch in their region, along the axis the law is about.
- A far-left or far-right choice pulls hardest, a centre choice pulls nothing, and the pull fades back after the law is repealed like other lean effects.
- National laws are unchanged.
