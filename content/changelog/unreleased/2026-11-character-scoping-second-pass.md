## What changed

- Older corporation alerts, such as R&D breakthroughs, now stay with the
  character who runs that corporation. Other characters on the account no
  longer see them or count them as unread.
- If the page briefly fails to load, the header keeps your session and retries
  instead of showing Sign in and Register.
- The header shows a placeholder instead of zeros for actions, funds, cash,
  influence and favorability until your numbers arrive.
- Opening a CEO Office link for a corporation you do not run now says the
  office is only for its CEO.
- Equal shareholders on a corporation's Ownership tab always appear in the
  same order.
- The achievements counter on profiles now matches the badges listed. Locked
  badges show only in the expanded view.
- Search no longer lists the same bond several times.
