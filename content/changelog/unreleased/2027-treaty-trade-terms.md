## What changed

- While the Critical Minerals Club has terms in force, its members send more of their rare earth, iron and coal surplus to each other, and the goods arrive a little cheaper than from outside countries.
- While the Arctic Agreement has terms in force, its members do the same for oil, natural gas and freight.
- When the terms lapse and are not renewed, trade between members goes back to normal.
- Trade with countries outside the treaty is not changed, so a buyer outside the club gets a smaller share of a member's surplus.
- A sanction between two countries still closes trade between them, and the exporter's surplus goes to other buyers. This is now checked by tests.
