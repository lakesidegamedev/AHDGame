## What changed

- A second pandemic can start from 2031 in countries with thin public health systems and crowded cities. Early travel measures, hospital surge capacity or a rapid vaccine platform each change how it goes.
- A sovereign debt wave can start from 2028, but only when at least three heavily indebted, slowing economies are in trouble at the same time. A contagion firewall, emergency lending or a debt restructuring each soften it.
- A power grid cascade can start from 2030 in summer, where the network is already thin. Storage, demand response and new links between regions each help.
- A critical metals squeeze can start from 2028 when world tension is high and an economy leans on electronics and defence. Alliance supply deals, a stockpile release or recycling and new mines each help.
- Each crisis comes in waves with an onset, a peak and a recovery, always ends on its own even if nobody acts, and rests for two years before it can return. A country already under two severe crises is skipped.
- Nothing here is scheduled. A country with strong health, grid or finances never rolls for the matching crisis.
