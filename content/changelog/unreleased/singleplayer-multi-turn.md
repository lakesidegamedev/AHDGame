title: Play several turns at once in singleplayer
summary: >-
  End turn can now play 3, 5 or 10 turns in a row and give you a summary of
  what happened, stopping early when something needs you.
tags: [singleplayer]
badges: [minor]
areas: [fullstack]
---

In a normal singleplayer world, the desktop End turn control has a turn count
next to it. Pick 3, 5 or 10 and the game plays them one after another while
the toolbar shows which turn it is on. You can stop after the turn in progress
at any time.

The batch stops by itself when something needs you: an election you are in is
decided or a race you could run in opens, a vote or an offer is waiting for
your answer, a crisis reaches you, you lose an office, or your character is
gone. The toolbar says which one stopped it.

When it ends, the Summary shows the change in campaign funds and actions,
offices won or lost, the new inbox items grouped by kind, and notable events in
your country.
