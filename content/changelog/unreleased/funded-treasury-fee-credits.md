## What changed

- The incorporation fee for a subsidiary spin-off, the premium for founding a
  company in a low-confidence economy, and the index fund charter fee now land
  in spendable Treasury cash as one recorded payment that cannot apply twice.
- Previously these fees raised the Treasury's headline balance but not the cash
  it could actually spend.
