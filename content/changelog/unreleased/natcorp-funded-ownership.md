- Nationalization and privatization carry funded input stocks, unsold goods and operating bills to their successor without creating cash.
- State holdings keep distinct production methods when they share a region and sector.
- Treasury remittances use the turn's booked income. Public enterprise profit figures use turn units.
- Public service benefits reflect delivered sales and private competition, and exclude mothballed sites.
- Persistent unpaid operating bills count toward the existing financial distress grace period.
- Merging a state enterprise back transfers its residual cash and issued debt before removing its shell.

- Held shares and bonds, their recorded cost basis, and outstanding tax or operating arrears follow the successor when a corporation is absorbed.
- Partial funded transfers are valued on the facilities and stocks actually assigned to the buyer, including whole-facility rounding.
