summary: >-
  The party influence breakdown on profiles now includes state chair, state
  vice chair and caucus leadership bonuses, matching what each turn pays.

State party chairs and vice chairs and caucus leaders already earned their
party influence bonus every turn, but the profile tooltip left it out, so it
showed a smaller leadership bonus and a lower net per turn than players
actually received. The tooltip now counts every leadership role.
