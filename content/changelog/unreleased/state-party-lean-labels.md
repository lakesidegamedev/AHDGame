## What changed

- The map, the home state picker and the candidate file label each state or
  province by which of the country's two main parties it favours and how
  strongly: Swing, Leans, Likely or Solid.
- The scale is set by how far apart the two main parties sit in that country, so
  a province that leans only a little still shows its side.
- Candidate, party and character position labels are unchanged.
- A country with fewer than two parties keeps the old left to right label.
