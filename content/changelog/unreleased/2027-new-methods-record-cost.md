  The methods opened by the 2039 and 2049 research tiers each show what they

No late-game method is free. Each one now records its inputs, a build time in
turns and a plain-words downside, and a test fails if a new method is added
without them. Advanced fission, for example, takes four game years to build and
carries a heavy licensing and safety bill.
