date: 2026-10-28
title: The spin-off action shows how long its cooldown has left
summary: >-
  A corporation's structure page now says how many turns remain before it can
  spin off another subsidiary, instead of only saying so once you open the
  dialog.
tags: [corporations, subsidiaries]
badges: [patch]
areas: [frontend]
---

The spin-off slot on a corporation's structure page now shows the remaining
wait, for example "Available again in 12 turns.", while the cooldown runs. The
slot still opens the spin-off dialog as before, and the dialog still blocks the
spin-off until the cooldown ends.
