## What changed

- The notification badge no longer stops at 9+.
- The inbox no longer opens on "all caught up" while unread notifications wait in the other tabs.
- Alerts that belong to your whole account are labelled as shared by all your characters, so they do not look like duplicates.
- The Game Guide welcome mail now says it lists the figures you started with.
