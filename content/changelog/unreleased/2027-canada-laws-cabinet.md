## What changed

- Canada has its own law book: six tax laws plus the same program laws as the other countries, written for Canada (health transfers, pensions, supply management, child benefits and more).
- Canada's opening tax rates are set from combined federal and provincial rates.
- A 19-seat cabinet with departments, metric panels and two ministerial orders per seat.
- Merger review for Canada is handled by the Minister of Industry.
- Canada still cannot be joined. This only prepares the rules for when it opens.
