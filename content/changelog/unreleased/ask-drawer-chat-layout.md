## What changed

- Your question appears as a chat bubble on the right.
- While Keir researches, a small deck of cards shows the latest steps. Tap Show to see them all.
- Finished answers have a "Researched N steps" pill, plus copy, helpful, report and share buttons.
- Sources, live game data and answer details now live together behind one Sources button.
- Follow-up questions are folded away until you open them.
- Keir may ask a quick "did this help?" after an answer.
