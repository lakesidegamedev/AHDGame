## What changed

- Until now, every plant of yours took its own company's output before any outside buyer, so a supplier site could end a turn with nothing sold.
- The sector page now has a "Sell to outside buyers before my own plants" switch for the CEO. With it on, outside buyers are served first and your own plants get what they leave, free of charge.
- The switch is per sector and off by default, so nothing changes until you turn it on. It applies from the next turn.
