## What changed

- In a market where buyers go short, a plant that sold everything asks a
  little more next turn. The raise started from the market's average price.
- When one large seller prices well below the rest, the average barely moves.
  Ordnance buyers got about a tenth of what they ordered while its price rose
  only about 2% a turn.
- At the next step of the switch, a plant that sold out raises from the higher
  of the market price and its own last price. A plant with goods left over
  still gets no raise, and the existing price ceiling still applies.
- Prices set by players are not affected.
