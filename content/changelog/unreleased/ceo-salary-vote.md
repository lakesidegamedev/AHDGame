## What changed

- Saving a salary above the allowed share of trailing daily net income opens a CEO pay vote instead of changing the salary. The rest of the save still applies, and the salary stays the same until the vote passes.
- If the company has no positive net income, any pay rise needs a vote.
- Lowering pay, holding it, and rises within the share need no vote.
- Private companies, NPP-run companies and state-owned companies are not affected.
