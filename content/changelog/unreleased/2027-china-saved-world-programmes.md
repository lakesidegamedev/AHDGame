summary: >-
  In worlds started before China moved to its new law book, the programmes it
  already runs keep their cost in the national budget, and its minimum wage
  stays in force.
tags: [budget, legislation, labour]

China moved to a new law book in this release. Worlds started before that still
run the programmes passed under the old laws: pensions, health insurance, the
army and the rest. The budget now keeps pricing those programmes as before, and
the minimum wage China already set keeps applying. Without this, those spending
lines would have dropped to zero and the wage floor would have disappeared.
