## What changed

- Computer-run companies together can order only part of a shortage each turn, and at most three new plants per country and industry. Orders already under construction count against the shortage.
- A company is only founded in an industry once its price has stayed above normal for two turns in a row, so a one-turn price spike no longer triggers a rush.
- Player companies, including those on autopilot, are not limited by this.
