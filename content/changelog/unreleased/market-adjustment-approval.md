## What changed

- A visible "Market adjustment" chip joins the approval breakdown while the event is open. It is never hidden inside other numbers.
- Loss term: 4 points per 100 percent of capacity losing money above 40 percent, capped at 2.
- Unemployment term: half a point per percentage point of unemployment above its level when the adjustment began, capped at 2. It stays off until unemployment is confirmed to follow the funded economy.
- The chip is capped at 4 points per country and fades over 12 turns after the event ends.
- Responses count only through the losses and jobs they actually change.
- When three or more countries are adjusting at once the news wire carries a world headline, and another when it eases below two. Headlines carry no effects.
