---
date: 2026-10-20
title: Tech trees now improve your plants
summary: Every profit-margin and sale-price tech now makes plants produce more, use fewer inputs, or need less labour, and tech is cheaper and faster to research.
tags: [corporations, research, plants]
badges: [minor]
areas: [fullstack]
---

- Profit-margin techs are gone. Each one now gives a plant effect that fits its sector: cheaper labour for service and media sectors, fewer inputs for industry, energy, agriculture and defence, and more plant output for technology, extraction and the Corporate lane.
- Output sale price techs now raise plant output by the same percentage.
- Techs you already own switch to their new effect automatically. Nothing is refunded or lost.
- Total plant output from techs is capped at +12% and total input savings at 30%.
- The Research position panel shows your plant output and input bonuses instead of tech margin.
- Tech R&D costs are 20% lower and R&D spending earns about 20% more points per turn.
- Marketing strength techs are unchanged.
