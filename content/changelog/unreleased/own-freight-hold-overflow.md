## What changed

- A company's own trucks in a state stay reserved for its own goods even when
  the state's freight is crowded. Before, rival goods served earlier could use
  the extra congested capacity and take the trucks held for the owner, so the
  owner's building materials or other market goods got no cover at all.
- States that are not crowded behave as before.
