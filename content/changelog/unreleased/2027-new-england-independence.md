## What changed

- Any of the six New England Governors can request an independence referendum once the population-weighted independence desire across the six states reaches 60. The six states open between 18 and 30, and the New England Independence Party carries the cause at elections.
- The President decides, a campaign runs, and the six electorates vote as one. Each state has its own campaign targets, and a larger state moves the pooled result more than a smaller one.
- If the vote carries and Congress consents, the six states move to New England together and keep their names and borders. Their companies, parties, residents and treasury share move with them.
- The Governor of the most populous state, Massachusetts at the start, becomes interim Premier. The other Governors stay on as Commissioners until the first elections for a 90-seat Assembly and a Regional Council.
- New England keeps the US dollar. The US House, Senate (12 seats) and Electoral College shrink by the six states' share.
