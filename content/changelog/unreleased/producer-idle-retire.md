- Under the upcoming market, plants that have sold nothing for several turns will slowly retire their unused capacity once the transition window has closed, and never a mothballed plant.
- When capacity is retired, the owner gets a decommissioning grant of half its book value, paid only from the country's unspent public funding and shared out evenly when that funding runs short. The sector page shows what was retired and what was paid.
