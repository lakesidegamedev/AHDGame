## What changed

- A company with no cash can still run a plant when the plant's expected sales
  pay for the run. It could only borrow against 90% of those sales, so a plant
  with a thin margin stayed shut. A new setting lets that share be raised.
- A bankrupt AI company kept its plants idle for 30 turns before they passed to
  a funded owner. A new setting lets that wait be shortened.
- Both keep today's values until they are set.
