  The Whip count on a bill page now shows the whips for the chamber currently
  voting, so a bill in the Senate no longer shows every party as a free vote.

## What changed

- Once a bill moves to its second chamber, the Whip count shows that chamber's
  whips instead of only the first chamber's.
