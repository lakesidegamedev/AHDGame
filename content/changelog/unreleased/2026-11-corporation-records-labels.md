## What changed

- A technology you paid for keeps showing as owned after its decade ends. It no longer switches to "Baseline, no cost, granted automatically".
- Construction money now means one thing on the sector page and the financial statement: the part of what you paid that is still to arrive. Each build order shows the full amount paid, and its Cancel button names the exact refund (75% of the unfinished part).
- The plant headline reads "built" instead of "running", and the extra line says the plants still being built are not counted in it. Build progress reads "in this order finished".
