title: Plants only pay wages for the staff they actually have
summary: >-
  When a region cannot supply every worker a plant wants, the plant now pays
  wages only for the staff it has, instead of the full crew.
tags: [economy, corporations, labour]
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine]
---

- When a region is short of workers, a plant already produced less and employed fewer people, but it was still billed wages for its full crew. It now pays only for the staff it actually has.
- Pay per worker is unchanged. Fully staffed plants are unaffected.
