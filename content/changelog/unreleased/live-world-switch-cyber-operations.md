## What changed

- New worlds start with cyber operations on.
- On the running world, no government launches a cyber operation after the update: not a player's intelligence service, not a computer-run government and not a background government. A player who tries is told cyber operations are not open in this world yet.
- Operators can turn it on when the world is ready.
