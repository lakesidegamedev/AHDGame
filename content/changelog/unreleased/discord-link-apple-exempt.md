## What changed

- Accounts signed in with Apple can create a character without linking Discord.
- Email and Google accounts still need to link Discord before creating a character; Discord sign-ups are linked automatically.
- The sign-up page explains the three ways in: Apple (recommended), Discord, or email or Google plus a Discord link.
- Accounts with Apple linked can unlink Discord in Settings.
