## What changed

- The two starting banks in each full country are named after real banks, for
  example JPMorgan Chase and Bank of America in the United States.
- New computer-run companies in the United States, United Kingdom, Germany,
  Japan, China and Canada draw from a list of real companies for their
  industry, and show that company's logo where one is available.
- When every real name for an industry is in use, new companies fall back to
  the invented names used before. Earlier eras keep invented names throughout.
- Companies already running under invented names in a modern world are renamed
  once. Player-run and state-owned companies are not touched.
- The names are used for flavour only. The game is not affiliated with or
  endorsed by any of these companies.
