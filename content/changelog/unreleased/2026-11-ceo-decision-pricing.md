## What changed

- The research unlock dialog shows your research score after the purchase and an estimate of the quality change it causes.
- The changes beside Marketing, Logistics and R&D on the Overview are labelled as forecasts for next turn, with what actually changed last turn beside them.
- The Budget tab states the autopilot budget rule in plain words, shows your current targets, and lists the last budget change autopilot made to each budget.
- The bulk build preview shows profit per turn and payback for each sector, or the reason there is none.
- Sector input lists include advertising when the market buys it as an input.
- A short input with no offers in the sector's state says so, and shows how many offers exist elsewhere.
- The tooltip on an output sold outside shows what buyers paid against your ask and the regional price, and which kinds of buyers bought it market-wide.
