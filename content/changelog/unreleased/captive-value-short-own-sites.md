## What changed

- Your plants send their output to your own sites first. The game judged those
  deliveries at the market price, so a mine feeding your own steel plants could
  be cut to a sliver of capacity when the market price fell below what mining
  cost, even while your steel plants could not get enough iron.
- When your own sites are left short of a good your company also makes, what
  your plant sends them now counts at what those sites would pay for it, up to
  the good's normal price. A plant like that keeps running, and steps up until
  your own sites have what they need.
- Nothing changes when your own sites can get all they need from the market.
