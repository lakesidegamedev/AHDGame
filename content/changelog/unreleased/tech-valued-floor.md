## What changed

- The minimum cash price was set above what most techs are worth over 48 turns, so most corporations paid the same flat minimum for every tech.
- Techs with a running plant value now only need to meet a tenth of that minimum. Their price is what they save or earn per turn times 48.
- Techs with no running value, such as build discounts and shields, keep the full minimum.
