## What changed

- In a new world, government spending on health and defense and household income from pensions and welfare now reach the producer market at a minimum level, instead of scaling with a plant economy that has barely started.
- As players and companies build, the measured size of the plant economy takes over and nothing changes for a mature world.
- No new money is created. It is the same budget the treasury already spends; only more of it is bought from producers.
