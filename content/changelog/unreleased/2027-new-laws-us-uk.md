## What changed

- From 2027, Congress and Parliament can pass laws on AI accountability, data privacy, housing supply and zoning, a critical minerals reserve, cyber security for critical services, and duties for large online platforms.
- From 2030 they can also pass an automation tax, wage insurance, a carbon border charge, heat safety rules at work, a pension age tied to life expectancy, and faster nuclear licensing.
- Each law has four strengths above "no statute". Every one has winners and payers: building more homes helps renters and first buyers but costs open land, an automation tax protects workers but slows productivity, and a carbon border charge rewards clean producers while raising prices in the shops and straining trade ties.
- The automation tax and the carbon border charge now bring in revenue for the treasury. The other laws cost money to run.
- All twelve start with no statute in force, so nothing changes until a bill passes.
- A law cannot be proposed, by players, cabinets or parties run by the game, before the year it opens. A law that has passed stays in force for good, even if a later era closes it to new bills.
- These are national laws only. US states and UK regions cannot pass their own versions.
