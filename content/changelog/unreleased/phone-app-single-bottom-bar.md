## What changed

- Inside the phone app, the experimental bottom navigation is no longer drawn
  under the app's own tab bar. The app's tab bar and the standard menu button
  stay, so the page keeps its room.
- Phone browsers are unchanged. A later app version brings the new navigation
  into the app's own bar.
