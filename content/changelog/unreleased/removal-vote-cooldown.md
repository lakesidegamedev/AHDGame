## What changed

- A vote to remove a chair, vice chair, treasurer, committee member or campaigner cannot be opened for 24 turns after that person was elected or appointed to the seat.
- If a vote to remove someone fails or runs out, another vote to remove them cannot be opened for 24 turns.
- The proposal form greys out anyone who is protected and says why and from which turn a removal vote can be opened.
- A person still cannot vote on their own removal.
