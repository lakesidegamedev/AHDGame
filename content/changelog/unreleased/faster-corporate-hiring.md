## What changed

- When a region has people looking for work, about three quarters of a
  company's open jobs now fill each turn, up from about a third.
- A plant that is short of staff can now get back to full staffing in about
  three turns at the going wage, instead of ten.
- Paying above the region's going rate now makes you hire faster, not only win
  a bigger share of scarce workers. A strong pay premium can get a plant fully
  staffed in two turns.
- Losing staff when a region runs short of workers is unchanged: it still
  happens gradually, so you have time to react.
