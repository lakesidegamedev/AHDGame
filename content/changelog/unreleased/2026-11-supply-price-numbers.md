## What changed

- A short input on a sector page now shows how much the plant needed, how much arrived and how much was missing, per day, with the reason: sellers sold out, every offer was above your limit, or supply was on offer but did not reach the plant. A short flag only appears when this plant actually fell short.
- The pricing panel lists what you asked, what you were paid on average and the price that cleared the lane, so the three are no longer mixed up.
- Supply contract screens now say units per day, name the reference price the percentage is applied to, and show the contract price per unit, the money per turn and the total over a fixed term.
- The most you can commit to in a supply contract no longer goes above the output your plants last reached.
- The bulk wage preview shows the wage bill per turn. It was 24 times too high.
- A state's sector badges list only the inputs and outputs of the plants running there.
- The Commodities tab labels its price column as the market price and explains why a sector page can show a different input price.
