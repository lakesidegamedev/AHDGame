## What changed

- Six kinds of crisis can open from conditions in the game: a recession, a banking panic, a government debt crisis, a jobs shock in one region, an energy shortage and a wave of public unrest. Each opens only after the warning sign has lasted several turns in a row.
- How bad a crisis gets now follows the economy: unemployment, slow growth, inflation, bank stress, borrowing costs, debt and public mood. Higher spending on welfare and social security, and a lower central bank rate, make a recession less severe.
- A crisis now hits other parts of the game: factories produce less, energy supply and demand shift, banks come under panic, investors demand more to lend to the government, people move away from the worst-hit regions, and the government loses approval and votes.
- A crisis applies its effects once per turn, even if a turn is retried.
- Nothing changes in a live world until the crisis system is switched on.
