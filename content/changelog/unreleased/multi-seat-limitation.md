title: Keep cabinet and central bank appointments separate
summary: Current ministers cannot also become central bank chairs or US Federal Reserve Board members.
badges: [patch]

---

Cabinet appointments and central bank chair nominations now check current office membership across countries. US Federal Reserve Board seats have the same restriction. Leaving either office restores eligibility for the other.

Pending confirmations and chair acceptance recheck eligibility. Acting cabinet appointments and parliamentary reshuffles follow the same restriction.
