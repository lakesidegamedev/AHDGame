Germany's laws are now authored in the same law book as the other major
countries. Each law moves the nation's metric board, costs and earns money
through the shared price rules, and opens at a level that matches the era.

Germany also gets its own opening metric board for 1953, 1979 and 1991, an
opening approval baseline, opening tax rates (income tax 38%, corporate tax
30%, payroll 12%, VAT 19%) and regional differences between the Länder that
come from each Land's own data.
