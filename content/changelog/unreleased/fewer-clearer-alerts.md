---
date: 2026-10-29
title: Fewer, clearer alerts
summary: >-
  Routine company reports stay in your inbox instead of buzzing your phone,
  expired supply agreements arrive as one summary, and alerts name places,
  parties and resources in plain words.
tags: [notifications, inbox, mobile]
badges: [minor]
areas: [fullstack]
---

- Phone and desktop notifications now only carry alerts you need to act on or
  that are about you: votes to cast, offers to answer, races won or lost,
  attacks on you or your company. R&D results, bond schedules, credit rating
  moves, expired contracts and crises in your home state stay in the inbox.
- When several of your supply agreements end at once you get one alert that
  lists them, instead of one alert per agreement.
- The end of a crisis is its own alert and no longer counts as something you
  need to act on.
- Alerts say "Hokkaido" and "Rare Earth Minerals" instead of internal codes,
  primary results name your party, and credit rating alerts say whether the
  rating went up or down.
- Notification settings are grouped like the inbox, and alerts that also reach
  your phone and computer are marked.
- Phone apps can send themselves a test alert to check that notifications
  arrive.
