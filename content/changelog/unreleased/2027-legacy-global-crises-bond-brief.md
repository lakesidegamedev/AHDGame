title: "Global crises count in leader legacy, briefs find every country's bond, subsidised pay is not shown as lower"
summary: >-
  Worldwide crises now count towards a departing leader's legacy, background
  country briefs always find each country's nearest bond, and workers under the
  hiring subsidy are no longer shown as paid less.
tags: [government, world, labour]
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine, backend]
---

- A worldwide crisis such as a pandemic now counts towards the legacy of a leader who governed through it, even when no country list is stored on it.
- Background country briefs now read one nearest-maturity bond per country, so a country whose bonds all mature late no longer reports having no bond.
- The hiring subsidy lowers what the employer pays. The pay shown per worker, which unions use for dues, is now taken before that cut, so subsidised workers do not look worse paid.
