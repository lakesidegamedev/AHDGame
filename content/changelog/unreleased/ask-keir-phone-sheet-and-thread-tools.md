  On phones Ask Keir opens full screen and stays steady while Keir answers.

  and are grouped by date, as on the Ask Keir site.

On phones the Ask Keir panel now opens as a full screen sheet that slides up
from the bottom. Sending a question puts the keyboard away, and the panel
stays fixed to the visible screen, so its header no longer slides off the top
while Keir is answering. The answer only follows new text while you are at the
bottom, so you can scroll up and read without being pulled back down.

Keir is easier to see: the round button shows his face instead of a tiny full
figure, he sits beside the research steps while he works, and the header shows
whether he is researching or answering.

New on each answer: Share copies a public link to the thread (or opens the
phone share sheet), and Report asks what went wrong with the same reasons as
the Ask Keir site. Your threads are grouped into Pinned, Today, Yesterday,
Previous 7 days and Older, search shows the matching line, threads can be
renamed, and pin, rename and delete show on touch screens, with delete asking
for a second tap.
