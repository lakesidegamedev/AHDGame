## What changed

- In a 2027 world, the sitting US president starts with both of his terms counted. He cannot enter the 2028 presidential race, and his party puts forward another candidate, such as the vice president, who remains eligible.
- In a 2019 world, the sitting Irish president starts with both of his terms counted, so he cannot stand again for Uachtarán.
- Computer-run politicians now follow the same executive term limits as players. A term they win in play counts toward the limit, just as it does for a player. Founding and acting terms do not count.
- Player term limits are unchanged, and worlds from other start dates seat their leaders exactly as before.
