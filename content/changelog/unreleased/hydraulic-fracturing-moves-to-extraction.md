## What changed

- Hydraulic Fracturing is now an Extraction method that produces oil and natural gas. It needs steel, chemicals, energy, freight, construction services and building materials, and earns a little more per unit of capacity than Oil & Gas.
- The Shale Extraction research now unlocks it. The Shale Fracking research in the Energy tree is gone.
- Energy sectors that were set to Hydraulic Fracturing are returned to Conventional with the same capacity. The retool fee they paid is refunded.
