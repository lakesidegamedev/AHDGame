title: No-confidence motions end when the premier leaves office
summary: >-
  A vote of no confidence now ends when the premier it names has already left
  office, instead of running on and cancelling the nominations to replace them.
tags: [politics, elections]
badges: [patch]
areas: [backend, engine]
---

A no-confidence motion is tied to one premier. When that seat becomes vacant
before the vote closes, the motion now ends as cancelled and its result cannot
cancel any leadership nominations opened during the vacancy.
