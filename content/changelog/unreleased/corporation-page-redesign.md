title: A new look for corporation pages
summary: Corporation pages have a clearer header, a live quote board, folder tabs and a layout that works on phones, in every site theme.
tags: [corporations, interface]
badges: [minor]
areas: [frontend]
---

- The corporation header now shows the name, listing, headquarters and chief executive together, with a quote board for the share price: its change on the last turn, a price line with the previous close marked, the recent low and high, and the bid, spread and ask.
- The key figures sit in one strip under the header. On a phone four headline figures show first, and the rest open with one tap.
- Tabs are folder tabs joined to their page. On a phone the tabs stay at the top of the screen while you scroll.
- Notices above the tabs, such as bond repayments, nationalization and CEO offers, share one style.
- Everything follows the display theme chosen in your settings.
- The Overview's key statistics read as three columns, market, earnings and balance sheet, each with one chart: market value against book value, where revenue goes, the leverage rating on its scale, and cash against debt.
- Sectors on the Overview show how revenue splits across regions in one bar, and list as cards on a phone.
- The Overview sidebar shows your position as a share stub, open votes with the turns left, and ownership as a ring with each holder's share count. On a phone these sections fold away until you tap them.
- The Simple view tags what needs you, sets its figures in a grid and ranks the biggest sectors.
- On a phone the Overview opens in Simple unless you have chosen otherwise.
- The income statement draws each line's share of revenue as a bar. The balance sheet shows two rings, what the corporation owns and who has a claim on it, with every line one tap away.
- Bonds show on a maturity timeline above the bond table, sized by what is repaid, with issues due soon in the warning colour.
- The credit rating sits on the full rating scale, and Peers lists your corporation beside the country and sector averages.
- Charts has a row of metric chips over the chart and a trend line for each metric in the table. On a phone the chart comes first.
- Snapshot draws each change as a bar that grows right for a rise and left for a fall.
- The product studio sets a product in development out as a card, with its figures in a grid and decisions waiting on you marked in the warning colour.
- Opening a plant sector's margins now shows how it gets from capacity to net profit, beside the margin build and production policy, with Build capacity, Details and Abandon to hand. Abandon asks you to confirm first.
- Viewing a rival's plants, the margins drawer offers Split rival plants, which opens the split planner on that sector's page.
- Active extraction contracts show how much of their term has run.
