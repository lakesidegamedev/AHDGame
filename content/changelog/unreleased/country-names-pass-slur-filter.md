---
date: 2026-10-28
title: Niger and Guinea no longer trip the slur filter
summary: >-
  News posts, mail, wiki pages, debate posts and names that mention Niger,
  Nigeria, Guinea, Equatorial Guinea, Guinea-Bissau or Papua New Guinea are no
  longer refused. Slurs are still blocked.
tags: [moderation, bugfix]
badges: [patch]
areas: [backend]
---

The slur filter now recognizes these country, people and place names before
checking for slurs, so they pass while the slur itself and its misspellings
stay blocked.
