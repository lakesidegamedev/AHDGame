title: "Selected tabs and filters look the same everywhere"
summary: >-
  Every tab bar, view switch and filter now shows the selected option as a
  filled pill in the theme's main colour, instead of a mix of underlines, dark
  pills and white pills.
tags: [ui, themes]
badges: [patch]
areas: [frontend]
---

Tabs on corporation, party, executive, legislature, contests, market, policy
and many other pages used three different ways to show which option was
selected: a coloured underline, a dark pill or a white pill, sometimes all on
one page. They now all use the same filled pill in the theme's main colour,
with the other options as plain text that lifts when you point at it.

This covers the corporation page tabs, the legislature's chamber switch and
view tabs, the Campaign Manager filters, period and range pickers on charts,
and sort and filter chips. Choice cards in forms, selected table rows and the
site menu keep their own styles.

The Settings page's inner panels now use the same surface colours as the rest
of the game instead of a shade found only there.
