## What changed

- Rookie of the Week is judged by account age, not character age, because a world reset recreates every character.
- The contests intro says that ties are ranked by a fixed entry order, that only positive scores place, and that one character can win several contests.
- Government approval says it measures from the day the leader took office and does not reset each week.
- The round countdown shows the turn the round settles on.
- Contests opened in the same week now share one round number.
