## What changed

- The Campaign Manager map spans the whole centre column and grows to fit your screen, instead of sitting in a small box beside the race details.
- On most screens the race details now sit under the map. Very wide screens keep the map and race details side by side, with more room for the map.
- The race finder and side panels run the full height of the screen.
