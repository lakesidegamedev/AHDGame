Extraction corporations can open matching government tenders from an empty Contracts tab. The tab explains open access, reserved capacity, signing fees, royalties and default, links active concessions to their award records, and keeps revoked agreements in history. Loading failures show a retry action. Existing agreements remain visible while new issuance is paused.

Regional resource pages link to the bidding board, show capacity and output per day, include outstanding tenders in contract headroom, and let national and regional officials manage concessions issued by their own level. Headroom and resource reads respect the selected country.

Multiple concessions for the same company, region and resource now add their capacity shares together instead of replacing one another. All of the company's sectors share that combined allocation. Open-access operators receive the remaining pool.
