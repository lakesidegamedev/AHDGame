## What changed

Germany, Japan and China now carry the 2027 to 2050 laws in their single law
book, with five levels each, the same as the United States and United Kingdom.
Climate, automation and cyber laws still shape their economies as before.
Worlds already running pick up the new laws without losing anything they had
enacted. Enacted later-era laws from the old Germany, Japan and China lists
keep working in those worlds.
