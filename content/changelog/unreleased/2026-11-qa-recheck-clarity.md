## What changed

- Output growth reads the economy's current rate every turn, the same figure the budget shows as Real GDP growth.
- Poverty explains that it is the game's own poverty line, so it will not match published half-of-median figures or compare one for one across countries.
- Treatment delay and housing burden say what their scale means, for example that 20 is the typical wait at the 1991 start.
- The national budget shows a Housing line when housing programs exist, even if they are booked as infrastructure. The environment line says water is counted there.
- Vacant Supreme Court seats show when they opened, until which turn the President can nominate, and that the Administration files a nominee after that. Addresses such as /courts now open the court page.
- The premier note now appears on the Canada elections page, the province page header and the Premier's Office.
- On the approval page, each condition shows its average margin across regions and the page explains the 3pp limit so the numbers add up.
