## What changed

- Era progression, which covers the decade crossings, era-aware scoring and cabinet and metric changes by year, always runs.
- Sector technology trees are always available: the Tech tab, unlocks, frontier programmes and the build and growth effects.
- Nuclear programmes, the covert track and Cold War tension no longer check a world switch.
- Play is unchanged in the live world and in new worlds, since all three were already on.
