summary: >-
  The opening tax rates and program spending for the US and UK now come from
  plain data instead of the retired law catalogue. Opening budgets are unchanged.

The US and UK opening budgets were still being worked out from the old law
catalogue behind the scenes. Those inputs are now stored as plain data, so the
old catalogue can be retired. Every opening budget in every era comes out exactly
as before, and no player sees a difference.
