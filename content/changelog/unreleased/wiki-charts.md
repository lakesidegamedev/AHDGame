Wiki pages that describe a value changing over time or across a range now include a chart. You can see how debt changes borrowing costs, how influence decays, how a vote pool is released over an election, how upkeep grows against military power, and more.

Every chart is drawn from the same numbers the game uses, so a chart cannot go out of date when a rule is rebalanced. Hover or tap a chart to read exact values.
