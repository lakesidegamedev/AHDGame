Household money used to be only the wages plants paid, so states with few plants had almost no shoppers, and many goods had no household buyer at all.

- **Spending power.** Every populated state now has households with a wallet. Each turn they are topped up toward a target set by the state's population and its average wage, including the pay your companies set. The top-up is new money, phased in over about three days, and it is paid on top of wages, benefits and growth income.
- **New shopping list.** Households now spend on food, housing, healthcare, medicines, energy, natural gas for heating, vehicles, electronics, retail, advertising and media, network services, financial services, software, construction services, freight, consulting, building materials, plastics and chemicals. Food takes a smaller share of the list and housing a larger one.
- **Advertising and media.** The advertising product is now called Advertising & media, and households buy it.
- **Cheaper goods sell more.** Household demand now rises when a good gets cheaper, so lower prices sell more units.
- **On screen.** The central bank's money supply tab shows the spending power top-up created last turn and since the world began, next to new household income.
