title: Management overhead for very large corporations
summary: Corporations with far more plants than most now pay a small management overhead on every sector, so the biggest corporations stop snowballing.
tags: [corporations, plants, balance]
badges: [minor]
areas: [fullstack]
---

- Corporations with more than 300 whole plants pay a management overhead on the margin of every sector they own.
- The overhead grows by a fixed number of margin points each time the plant count doubles, so it rises slowly and most corporations never pay it.
- State-owned corporations are exempt.
- The sector page lists it as Management overhead in the margin breakdown.
