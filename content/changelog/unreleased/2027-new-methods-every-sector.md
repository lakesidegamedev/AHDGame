## What changed

- New methods: Agent Asset Management (finance), Agentic Storefronts (retail), Solid-State Vehicles (vehicle makers), Agentic Property Operations (real estate) and Autonomous Deep Mining (extraction).
- Each is unlocked by the first research node of its sector in the 2039 tier and needs its own inputs.
- Every sector now has research to do and a recent new method in 2045 and in 2051. Countries with less ready industry still adopt each method years after the leaders.
