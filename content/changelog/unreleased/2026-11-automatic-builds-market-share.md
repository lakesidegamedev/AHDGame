## What changed

- Companies on autopilot and computer-run companies add new capacity only in markets where buyers have unmet demand for that output, the same room the build dialog shows. A market with no room, or one that cannot be measured, gets no automatic build. Builds that answer a measured shortage are unchanged.
- Market size, your share and competitor rows now use the revenue a sector actually earned last turn, the same figure the finance screens show. The dominance cost and antitrust checks read the same share, so what a screen shows is what the game applies.
