## What changed

- One credit rating per company on the masthead, Overview, Financials, Credit and Bonds tabs. The what-if panel now applies the same ownership adjustments as the rating, so its coupon matches the headline coupon.
- The Credit tab explains why the composite can differ by a point from the weighted components (the turn blends in last turn's score), and labels the coupon as the 2 year rate.
- "Annual income" is now "Annual operating income", and "Book equity" is "Equity used for score" to separate it from the balance sheet book value.
- Issue Shares counts the new cash when it shows the price after the issue, and says the market may move it.
- Market depth on the stock and bond screens never exceeds the shares or bond units that exist.
- Bond issuance shows the cap per issue, the debt room left and the largest issue allowed now as separate labelled figures, and states the unit face value once.
- The quote card's percentage and dollar change both measure against the same previous price.
