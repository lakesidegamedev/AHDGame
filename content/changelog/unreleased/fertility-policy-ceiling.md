## What changed

- Increased the positive fertility policy ceiling from 0.24 to 0.40 children per woman across supported countries.
- Retained actual delivery requirements, the existing adjustment speed and the negative-pressure penalty.
- Compared both candidate ceilings across supported country cohorts and calibration sensitivities before selecting the new limit.
