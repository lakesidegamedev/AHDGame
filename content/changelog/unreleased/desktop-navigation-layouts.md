## What changed

- Experimental users can choose three desktop dropdown layouts in Settings:
  grouped cards (A, the default), section browser (B), and compact directory (C).
  Standard dropdowns remain available, and the choice saves to your account.
- Dropdowns group existing destinations and put personal shortcuts and frequently
  visited pages in clearer places. Pixel icons follow your selected theme.
- The site logo, fonts, colours, bar controls, and mobile navigation stay the same.
  Turning experimental features off restores standard desktop dropdowns.
