## What changed

- Experimental players get grouped card dropdowns on desktop. Standard dropdowns
  remain available in Settings, and the choice saves to your account.
- Each dropdown opens under the tab you clicked and is only as wide as its groups
  need, instead of stretching across the screen.
- Dropdowns group existing destinations and put personal shortcuts and frequently
  visited pages in clearer places. Pixel icons follow your selected theme.
- The section browser and compact directory trial layouts are gone. Anyone who
  picked one now gets grouped cards.
- Turning experimental features off restores standard desktop dropdowns.
