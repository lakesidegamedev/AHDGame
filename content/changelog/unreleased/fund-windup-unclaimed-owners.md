title: Fund wind-ups finish when a holder has gone
summary: A dissolved fund holder no longer stops every other investor's payout.
tags: [funds, economy]
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine]
---

- When a fund closes and one of its investing companies has since been dissolved, the remaining investors are now paid as normal instead of the whole payout stopping. The share with no owner goes to the central bank reserve of the fund's currency.
