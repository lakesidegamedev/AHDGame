## What changed

- The era photo on the left is shown in full colour with the era name and a short description.
- One-click sign-in (Lakeside, Apple, Google, Discord) now comes first, with email and password below it.
- The sign-in buttons share one consistent style instead of four different colours.
- You can show or hide your password while typing it.
- "Create an account" sits at the top right on desktop.
- On phones, the era photo appears as a header above the form.
