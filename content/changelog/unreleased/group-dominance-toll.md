## What changed

- The commodity build toll compares the whole group's potential output of a commodity, owned plus queued, with what the world wants of it.
- A group is the top corporation and every corporation it controls through more than half of the votes, directly or through other subsidiaries.
- Players and NPPs are priced the same way, and the build quote shows the group figure.
