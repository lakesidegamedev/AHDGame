## What changed

- Rookie of the Week now drops veterans from the current round as well as new ones, and every weekly contest shows the same round number.
- Career scores on the leaderboard show whole numbers, and the office column names the life it belongs to.
- Country outlook stories are written from each country's own growth, prices and debt, and rating upgrades are no longer filed as debt strain.
- Welcome mails say the funds and actions are your opening figures, not your balance.
- The experimental features hint now says whether the switch is on or off for you.
- Rows with no opening law say "No dedicated law at the start of the game".
- Corporation pages show their description, including the IMF.
- The /war address opens the conflicts page, and country pages link to wars and foreign affairs.
- A race page says why the sitting officeholder is not on the ballot.
