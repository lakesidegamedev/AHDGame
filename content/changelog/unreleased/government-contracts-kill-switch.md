title: Government contracts admin kill switch
date: "2026-10-30"
badges: [patch]
areas: [fullstack]
tags: [admin, government-contracts]
---

Admins can enable or disable new government tenders, bids and awards across all countries from the kill-switch board. The switch defaults to enabled and takes effect without restarting the server.

While disabled, signed contracts continue to settle and deliver. Pending bids can still be withdrawn and tenders canceled. Contract boards show a notice and disable new-contract controls.
