title: "Japan can still pass tax bills in running worlds"
summary: >-
  In worlds started before Japan's new law book, Japan's income, corporate,
  payroll, consumption and tariff tax bills work again through the bill
  wizard and the cabinet.
tags: [legislation, budget]
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine]
---

Japan's tax laws moved to its new law book. Worlds started before that move
still hold Japan's older tax laws, and the bill wizard and the cabinet only
looked for the new ones, so every Japanese tax bill in those worlds would have
been refused.

Both versions of each Japanese tax law now count as the same tax. The wizard
offers whichever one the world has, and a bill cannot set the same tax twice
by naming both.
