summary: >-
  A sector waiting for an automated method to be built keeps its full crew and
  pays no automation levy until the new method is running.
tags: [corporations, automation]

While a sector waits for an automated method such as humanoid robotics to be
built, it still runs its old method. It now keeps its full crew and pays no
automation levy during that wait. Workers are let go, and the levy starts, on
the turn the new method comes online.
