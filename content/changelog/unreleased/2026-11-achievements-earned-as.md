## What changed

- Every achievement now records the name of the character who earned it. On a profile, an award earned under a different name says "Earned as" followed by that name, both on the badge and on the full achievements page.
- The "Recently unlocked by" list names the character who earned each award, even after that character has retired.
- Awards earned before this change pick up the name of their character where that character still exists. Awards from characters that are already gone show as before.
- Staff granting an achievement by hand can enter the earlier character's name, so a restored award shows who earned it.
