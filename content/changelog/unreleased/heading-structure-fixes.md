summary: >-
  The legislature's composition card no longer repeats the chamber name as a
  second heading, and the shadow cabinet and the elections chamber card are
  proper sections in the page outline.
tags: [accessibility, ui]
badges: [patch]
areas: [frontend]
---

The legislature's composition view now titles its chart card "Senate
composition" (or the matching chamber), so the page no longer has two headings
that both say "Senate".

On a parliamentary executive page the shadow cabinet is its own section, and
on the elections page the chamber composition card is a section heading, so
the page outline lists them in order.
