## What changed

- The Canadian dollar starts 2027 worlds at 1.37 per US dollar, the 2024 average.
- The Canadian dollar now belongs to Canada instead of being treated as a stand-in for the US dollar.
- Admin readiness checks now know what a healthy Canada looks like: 13 provinces and territories, five parties and a 343-seat House of Commons.
- Canada still cannot be joined, and currency trading is unchanged in live worlds.
