## What changed

- Stance choices (lockdown, stay open, bailouts, reform and the rest) no longer say they cut severity, because they never did. The line is hidden for them.
- Small pushes on bank stress now build up. A bank only enters a panic once the stress adds up to a full turn, so a single stance choice no longer panics every bank. Lender forbearance no longer adds bank stress at all.
- Governments run by AI take at most one stance per crisis, picked by how interventionist the government is. They no longer repeat the same stance every cooldown.
- Crisis response costs paid from the treasury are now converted to the country's own currency instead of being charged as raw anchor-currency units.
- A stance is marked as used before its effects are applied, so an interrupted turn cannot apply the same package twice.
