summary: On a corporation's Commodities tab, each state now opens to show its inputs and outputs, with amounts and market value.

- On a corporation's Commodities tab, the By region table now shows each state's input cost, output value and net value at the current market price.
- Open a state to see its inputs on the left and its outputs on the right, with the amount of each commodity per turn and its value.
- The commodity flows table now lists what the corporation consumes before what it produces.
