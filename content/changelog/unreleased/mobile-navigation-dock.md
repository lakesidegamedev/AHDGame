## What changed

- With experimental features on, phones show a bottom dock instead of the menu
  button: Company (or Market if you have no company), your home state, the nation
  you are viewing, Actions, and More.
- Your home state and nation open a sheet of grouped cards, the same groups as the
  desktop grouped cards layout, with your party, race and offices first. The nation
  sheet also lets you switch to another country.
- More holds search, your profile pages, the world, help and staff pages. Every
  destination from the old phone menu is still there.
- Your stats bar now sits directly above the dock. Notifications and your profile
  menu stay at the top.
- Settings, Experimental Features has a new Phone navigation choice: the bottom
  dock (default) or the standard menu. The choice saves to your account and does
  not change your desktop layout.
