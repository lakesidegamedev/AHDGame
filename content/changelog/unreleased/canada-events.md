## What changed

- Smoke Over the Valley: help at a clean-air shelter, buy air purifiers for seniors, or wait for the wind to change.
- The Trucking Permit (politicians only): campaign against trade barriers between provinces, help a brewery with its forms, or buy a stake in it.
- The Northern Housing Visit: visit a remote community, fund building materials, or decline.
- The Old Arena: back a new rink, patch the roof, or invest with a developer.
- These events only reach characters in Canada.
