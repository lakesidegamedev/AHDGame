## What changed

- Ireland, Nigeria and the United States get 1979 regional metric overlays, blended from their 1953 and 1991 values.
- Poland, Hungary, Romania, Yugoslavia, Bulgaria, Czechoslovakia and the Soviet republics get 1979 starting exchange rates.
- Nigeria's 1979 budget spending is trimmed from 87 percent to about 52 percent of GDP.
- Only commodity price scaling remains before the 1979 era is fully seedable.
