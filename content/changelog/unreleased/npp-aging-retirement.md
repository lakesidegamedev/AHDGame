## What changed

- Computer-run politicians in office could only leave by losing an election, and only died of old age at the highest autonomy setting. Over decades the same faces held every seat.
- A new turnover switch, on for every new world, lets them die of old age whatever the autonomy setting, and adds retirement: from age 70 a politician has a 5% yearly chance of stepping down, rising to 35% a year at 80 and above.
- A politician who retires or dies is replaced at once by a younger member of the same party in the same office. Their cabinet posts, government role and leadership pass to the successor, as deaths already did.
- Politicians seeded without a birth year are given one (age 35 to 70) so they age like everyone else.
- Player characters do not age. Worlds that do not have the switch keep the old behaviour.
