title: Loss-making corporations settle faster each turn
summary: >-
  Corporations that cannot cover a turn's operating loss from cash now settle
  in shared batches instead of three separate payments each. The amounts owed
  and paid are unchanged.
tags: [performance, corporations, turn]
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine]
---

Operating losses that cash cannot fund settle as one guarded book per currency,
with each corporation's unpaid amount recorded on the same write as its cash.
