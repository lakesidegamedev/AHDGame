## What changed

- A new research tier opens in 2039. Main nodes cost 60 R&D score, specializations 120 and capstones 180.
- The Corporate lane covers agent-run operations, automated compliance and supply networks that survive disruption.
- Every sector has a full 2039 Sector lane, including a specialization and capstones.
- New production methods, each unlocked by a 2039 Sector node:
  - Energy: Grid-Scale Storage Utility and Advanced Fission.
  - Chemicals: Direct Air Capture.
  - Healthcare: Biofoundry Biologics.
  - Manufacturing: Humanoid Robotics.
  - Technology: Agentic Services.
  - Agriculture: Cellular Agriculture.
  - Construction: Printed Mass Timber.
  - Defense: Drone Swarms.
  - Telecommunications: Orbital Constellations.
  - Logistics: Orbital Logistics.
  - Media and Entertainment: Personalized Media and Personalized Entertainment.
- None of the new methods is free. Each one uses existing goods and leans heavily on energy, electronics, rare earths, chemicals or software, so it pays off only where those inputs are available.
- Worlds that have not reached 2039 see the tier as locked. Nothing changes for companies before then.
