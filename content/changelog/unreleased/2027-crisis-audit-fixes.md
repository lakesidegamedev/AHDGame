title: "Crisis responses refund in full, healed scars ease unemployment, successors get their own names"
summary: >-
  A crisis response that fails partway now returns all the money it took,
  population scars bring unemployment back down as they heal, and an office
  filled by succession shows the new holder's name.
tags: [crises, elections]
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine, backend]
---

If a crisis response fails after the treasury has paid, the treasury, your
corporation and your actions are all refunded instead of the money being lost.

Population scars from a crisis used to raise unemployment and never lower it
again. As a scar heals, unemployment now comes back down by the same amount.

When a legislature or party vote fills an empty executive office, the office
now shows the successor's name rather than the previous holder's.
