## What changed

- Automatic crises and disasters always spawn on schedule. The pause control still stops new ones.
- Living conflicts always run, including the Vietnam war ladder, the Cold War dials and the pandemic, terrorism and Northern Ireland effects on budgets and politics.
- Crisis responses that read national income, such as the safety-net share, now use the same smoothed national income figure as the budget.
- Play is unchanged in the live world and in new worlds, since both were already on.
