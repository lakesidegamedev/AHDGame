## What changed

- Alberta joins Quebec as a province that can leave Canada through the same process: the Premier requests, the Prime Minister grants or declines, a campaign runs, and the province votes.
- A passed vote goes to Ottawa as a consent bill. Once enacted, Alberta becomes an independent country with an 87-seat Legislative Assembly, a Premier and six regions.
- Sovereignty Desire still has to reach 60 before the Premier can ask, and it starts at 24.
