## What changed

- Several parliaments (including the Polish Sejm, the Bulgarian and Romanian
  assemblies, the Czechoslovak chambers, the Soviet congress and the Italian,
  French and Spanish senates) required a party to win 20% of a region's vote
  before it could take any seats there. In a field of five to seven parties
  usually only the largest cleared that bar, so it won every seat in the
  region. Poland's Sejm ended up entirely one party on about a quarter of the
  vote.
- These chambers now use the same 10% bar as the other proportional
  parliaments.
- A party with less than half of a region's vote can no longer take all of
  its seats. If the bar leaves too few parties to cover half the vote, the
  next largest parties are let back in until it does.
- Applies to each chamber's next election. Seats already held are not
  changed.
