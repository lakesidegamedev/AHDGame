## What changed

- Stores kept cutting their own capacity: each turn a store planned its next run from how much of its last run it resold, and the store's capacity decided how much household spending reached it, so it resold less and cut again. A store now always keeps its full capacity open to shoppers and still pays wages, site bills and supplies only for the share its sales use.
- The store markup stays at 40%. The planned step down to 35% is cancelled.
- A company's revenue now counts the full value of the goods its stores sold, with Cost of goods resold as its own cost line, in its history, charts and financials. Profit is unchanged.
- The state economy page shows the room to build and the Build here button for stores again. Stores list no product, so the page had no figure to show and hid both.
