## What changed

- Canada's national budget covers federal and provincial government together, so it lines up with the combined tax rates in its law book.
- Revenue opens at about 40% of the economy and spending at about 42%, with defence at 2%.
- When a world opens with Canada, its law book is fitted to this spending mix and sized to what the government collects, the same way the United States and Britain open in 1991.
- Canada still cannot be joined. This only prepares the budget for when it opens.
