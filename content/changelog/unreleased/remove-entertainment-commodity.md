- Entertainment services are no longer a traded commodity. Households and governments no longer buy it, and its share of household spending now goes to the other goods households buy.
- Entertainment media and streaming, cable and publishing strategies now sell advertising instead. Their capacity is adjusted once so each plant's output is worth the same as before.
- Planned economies' media now sells advertising, bought by their own state media budget.
- Countries no longer found new firms because of an entertainment shortage.
