## What changed

- When founding a public company or going public later, you can float as little as 1% of the company. The old minimum was 10%.
- A normal IPO still tops out at 49%, so the founder keeps majority ownership.
- A dual-class IPO, where the founder keeps extra votes per share, can now float up to 90%. The old cap was 75%.
