## What changed

- An independence consent bill is now sponsored by the departing country's own government and names that country, rather than always saying the United Kingdom.
- Game event posts about independence name the right capital: Westminster for Scotland and Wales, Ottawa for Quebec.
- The handover that turns a region into a new country can now split Quebec into Montreal, Laval, Laurentides and Lanaudiere, Monteregie, the Quebec City region, Estrie and Centre-du-Quebec, the Outaouais and Abitibi, Mauricie and Saguenay, and Eastern and Northern Quebec.
- Quebec still cannot call a sovereignty referendum. That opens once an independent Quebec can hold its own elections and pass its own laws.
