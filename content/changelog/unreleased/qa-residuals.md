## What changed

- US homelessness now starts from the January 2024 point-in-time count for each state, about 23 people per 10,000 nationally. It previously started near 8.
- US crime now includes each state's 2024 property crime rate instead of an estimate from income and city size.
- US corruption now differs by state, based on federal public corruption convictions per resident from 2014 to 2023. It was the same in every state.
- NATO now includes Czechia, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Croatia, Montenegro and North Macedonia in the eras after they joined. Countries that left NATO during play stay out.
- Screens that show amounts in ₳ (contests, sectors, corporation finances, bond holdings, union pensions, trade and the wiki diagrams) now explain that ₳ is the game's shared accounting unit.
- Metric pages now say when every region started at the national figure because no regional measurement exists, so equal regional values do not look like a fault.
