date: 2026-10-19
title: Imports priced at the exporter's price, and spare exports reach short buyers
summary: Commodity imports now cost the exporting country's own market price, and spare exports no longer sit unsold while buyers abroad go short.
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine]
tags: [commodities, trade]
---

- Imports are now priced at the exporting country's own market price for that commodity, instead of a flat default price. Cheap and expensive exporters now compete on what their goods actually cost.
- Spare exports set aside for a buyer who then bought cheaper elsewhere are offered again to buyers abroad who are still short, instead of going unsold.
