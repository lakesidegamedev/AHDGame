## What changed

- Surveys take 6 turns instead of 12, before the era adjustment.
- A company can run 6 surveys at once instead of 3.
- Company success odds now start at 40% instead of 25%, still reaching 80% with strong research.
- A successful company survey grants a royalty free extraction contract over the capacity it found, for 96 turns, within the usual 75% contract limit.
