## What changed

- When the National Committee opens a vote to remove a chair, vice chair, treasurer, committee member or campaigner, that person gets a notice with the number of yes votes needed and the turn the vote closes.
- When the vote passes, the removed person gets a notice saying the committee removed them and when they can stand again.
- When the chair is removed, the vice chair gets a notice that they now hold the chair's powers until a new chair is elected.
