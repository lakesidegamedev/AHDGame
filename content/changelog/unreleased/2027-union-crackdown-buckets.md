## What changed

- Resolving a union ban strike, and holding a crackdown posture, now lowers the executive's standing with the labour-leaning buckets of that country's census, or the business-leaning ones for the back-down path.
- The labour and business groups are defined per country for the US, UK and Ireland, with a low-income versus high-income split everywhere else.
- Because the groups are now real census buckets, these costs reach voters in every country instead of only where the old voter types existed.
