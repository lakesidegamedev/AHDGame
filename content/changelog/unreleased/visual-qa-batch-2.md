## What changed

- Character stats on your profile show a row of points in each stat's own colour, with a matching icon.
- The money history on your profile opens with a clear "Show the last 30 days" button.
- Campaign Manager folds unopposed races into one "Show unopposed races" line, so contested races are easy to find. Your own and watched races always stay in the list.
- The Campaign Manager map legend says what its shades mean.
- The stock market chart's axis shows game dates instead of real clock times. Hover a candle for its clock time.
- The market overview shows each stock's price in plain text and only the change in colour, and says the change covers the last 24 hours.
- A country's national history chart prints its lowest and highest values and the turns it covers.
- The policy page opens on its first title instead of every statute at once. Pick a title in the side list to open it.
- The policy side list says which axis each coloured number is on.
- When every listed crisis has the same severity, it is shown once instead of on every row.
- A sector's run report explains when last turn's output reads above the production limit because capacity has dropped since.
- Notification settings are grouped into folded sections showing how many alerts are on. Searching opens the matching sections.
- Notification previews end at the first full sentence, and Snooze and Archive sit right under the message.
