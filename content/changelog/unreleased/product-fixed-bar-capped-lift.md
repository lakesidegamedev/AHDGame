## What changed

- A product's quality is measured against the target set when development started. Growing the matching sectors during development no longer raises that target at launch, so the funding levels shown in the studio, including All in, mean what they say.
- A hit lifts revenue up to the size of the business the product was developed for. If the matching sectors have grown since, the lift is scaled down to that size, and the launch message says so.
