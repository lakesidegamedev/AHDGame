## What changed

- A new Leadership tab in career history lists posts taken and left: heads of international organizations, Speakers, Presidents pro tempore, majority and minority leaders and whips, national and state party chairs, vice chairs and treasurers, caucus chairs, central bank chairs, military commanders and corporate CEOs.
- Leaving an office with no resignation, removal or defeat on record now shows as "Left office", so a term that simply ends no longer stops the history mid-sentence.
- New achievement, Chair of the Assembly: lead any international organization.
- Posts already held when this update arrives are recorded on the first turn after it, dated from the original election where the game kept that date.
