## What changed

- Property crime was scored against an old scale built for 1990s total crime, so a modern rate looked excellent and small changes in unemployment or poverty moved it by thousands per 100,000. It now uses a scale centred on its era's national level, the same way violent crime does.
- Under the strongest pressure, property crime now moves by about a quarter instead of doubling.
- Property crime that has already risen eases back gradually over the coming turns rather than resetting at once.
