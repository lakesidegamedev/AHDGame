## What changed

- Interrupted congressional leadership resolutions preserve nominations and officeholders for retry.
- Cabinet and VP confirmations preserve office assignments, career history and action pools on retry.
- Caucus chair elections commit membership roles, terms and results together.
- Head-of-state appointment votes remain retryable until the office assignment commits.
- Party leadership rechecks voter and candidate membership, country and retirement at resolution.
- Notifications and achievements run after successful commits.
