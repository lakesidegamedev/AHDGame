## What changed

- The orbital debris cascade crisis can no longer open before 2042. It already needed a country with satellite operators; the date now matches when those fleets first appear.
- The party trend charts record history for every country that runs the full simulation, including ones not yet marked as launched.
- Worlds before 2042 and the live world are unchanged.
