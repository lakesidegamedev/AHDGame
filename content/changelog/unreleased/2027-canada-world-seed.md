## What changed

- Canada's 13 provinces and territories, its five parties and its national budget are set up when a 2027 world is created, once Canada is switched on.
- Each party starts with a local organization that matches how it did in that province at the April 2025 election. The Quebec party starts strong in Quebec and from scratch everywhere else.
- Canadian voters are grouped into nine blocs, such as suburban families, Prairie conservatives, Quebec francophones and new Canadians, each with its own turnout and leanings.
- The 343-seat House of Commons opens with a government still to be formed. A party needs 172 seats for a majority.
- Canada still cannot be joined. Live worlds are not changed.
