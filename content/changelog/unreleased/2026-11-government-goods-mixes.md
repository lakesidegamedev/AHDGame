## What changed

- Every government budget line now buys a mix of goods instead of only healthcare services and weapons. Healthcare also buys medicines and equipment, defense buys vehicles, electronics and fuel, education buys buildings, software and food, infrastructure buys construction, steel and raw materials, and administration buys consulting, telecoms and software. A government that spends more on one line shifts what it buys toward that line's goods.
- The commodity page lists which budget lines buy a good and how much of each line goes to it. Government orders still add up to the market total.
- Building a plant now buys a wider mix of goods, including timber, energy, consulting, freight, iron and plastics.
- Computer-run companies now build faster into a shortage, ordering up to half of the unmet demand per turn and up to six plants per country and product at a time.
