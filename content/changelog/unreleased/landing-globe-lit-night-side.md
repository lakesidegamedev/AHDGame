## What changed

- The globe lights the Earth from the real position of the sun, with one smooth edge between day and night.
- Cities glow warmly on the night side and fade out through twilight.
- Borders and coastlines are thin and sharp, and the United States and Canada stay lit.
- The globe only moves while you drag it. Routes, satellites and the automatic tour are removed.
- The picture shown before the globe loads now matches the new look and the time of day.
