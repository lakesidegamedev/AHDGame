date: 2026-10-19
title: Companies buy advertising
summary: Consumer-facing companies now spend part of their input budget on advertising.
tags: [economy, media]
badges: [minor]
areas: [backend]
---

- Retail, manufacturing, vehicle, agriculture, financial, telecom, technology and real estate companies now buy advertising as an input, about 7% of what they produce.
- Most of them make the same margin as before, because their other inputs were reduced to pay for it. A few very profitable methods simply earn a little less.
- Media gets about twice as much demand for advertising as before.
