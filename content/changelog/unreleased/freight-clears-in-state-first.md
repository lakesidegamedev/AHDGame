date: 2026-11-01
title: Freight sells to its own state before anyone else
summary: >-
  A state's haulage capacity now goes to buyers in that state first, before
  buyers elsewhere take it from the national market. No change to prices or to
  any other commodity.
tags: [market, freight, corporations]
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine]
---

Freight is sold in the state where it is produced. Household and company orders
now take their own state's freight offers before the wider book is cleared, so a
buyer sorted earlier in the turn can no longer buy a distant state's haulage out
from under the local demand it was meant to serve. Unsold local freight still
reaches the national market as before.
