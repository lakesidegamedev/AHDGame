Naval and air supply changes now save even when a formation is undamaged. Aircraft no longer lose supply to fleet berth crowding, and Airlift supports nearby regions. Fleets can draw on nearby home ports without being stationed on land, and sea and air routes cross the Pacific.

Defense holders can offer naval or air basing access for another nation to accept. Either side can revoke it, and wartime enemies cannot use it. Regional port networks support three berth units per port-rating point; host and guest navies share their physical capacity. The command page explains support bases, berth limits and estimated next-turn supply, with a color-coded comparison of stations.
