  Independence carries regional cash, invoices, funding and resource capacity into
  the successor regions, and their regional budgets continue processing.

- Existing household and operating-payment cash moves to successor wallets without a new endowment. Outstanding invoices follow those wallets, including invoices owed by foreign companies.
- Resource capacity and depletion follow the same placement plan as extraction plants. Resources without an installed producer retain the existing GDP-based allocation.
- Funding amounts and settlement records divide rather than duplicate. Scotland and Wales continue regional-budget processing after independence.
- Fractional demographic cohorts and optional housing stocks are conserved. New national metric histories begin at independence, fiscal values use the successor budget, and empty opening armed forces have zero readiness. Inherited estimates are labelled as proxies.
- Enacted regional service laws become national laws where the same family is offered nationally, taking precedence over inherited UK choices. Other national laws carry over. Missing legal references use the reviewed opening defaults without creating new appropriations.
- Promoted laws use the existing national funding envelope. Their separate regional requests are released, protected national transfers remain protected, and funds are not granted twice.
