- The wiki explains approval badges, their two numbers, the margin cap and the lines that make the badges add up.
- The wiki explains sourced versus provisional metrics and how a domain score is worked out, and the metrics board says the same.
- The currency page names the accounting unit symbol, and the spawn funds figure now matches the welcome mail.
- Party pages say their member counts include non-player politicians.
- The suggestions board shows loading marks instead of zero counts, and explains gaps in suggestion numbers.
- /help, /leaderboard, /achievements and /country/<code>/senate now open the right page.
- Settings: one language list in both places, a clearer experimental features hint, and plain notes that there is no display-currency choice, two-factor sign-in or privacy switch.
- Bug reports have no public list; the confirmation and the admin notice now say so.
