## What changed

- AI-run companies pay part of each turn's profit to households. That payout
  was 60% of profit before tax, and tax took another half or more, so a
  profitable company paid out more than it earned, ran out of cash and had to
  stop every plant it owned.
- Behind a new switch, off until it is turned on: the payout is 60% of profit
  after this turn's tax. It is never paid from savings, and none is paid while
  the company's cash is below zero.
- At the next step of the switch, an AI company with no savings above its
  reserve stops founding new businesses and adding capacity until it has some.
- Player companies are not affected.
