- When a company makes a taxable loss, the loss is now banked. When it returns to profit, the bank offsets up to 80% of its taxable profit each turn until it is used up, the same way real corporate tax law treats losses.
- This applies to every form, including the owner tax on pass-through companies. State-owned companies are not taxed and are unaffected.
- Your financials show how much loss you have carried forward.
