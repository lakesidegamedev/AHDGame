Canada is being added to the 2027 world as a full country. This first step adds
the groundwork: the 13 provinces and territories, the 343 House of Commons seats
split between them, the 105 appointed Senate seats, opening party strengths for
five invented parties, and the starting economy, currency and central bank.

Nothing changes in running worlds, and Canada is not selectable by players yet.
Later steps add elections and government formation, laws and cabinet, the
economy hooks, Quebec's sovereignty politics, and the country page and map.
