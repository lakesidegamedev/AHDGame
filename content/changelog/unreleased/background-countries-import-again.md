title: "Countries outside the player economies buy imports again"
summary: >-
  Since the wider economy switched on, countries outside the player economies
  had no import budget and bought nothing. Their budgets are back, so companies
  can sell exports to them again.
tags: [economy, market, trade]
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine]
---

When the wider economy started, its budget and the import budget of countries
outside the player economies were booked in a way that made the second replace
the first. Those countries entered each turn with nothing to spend, so their
import orders went unfilled and companies lost those export sales. Both budgets
are now booked together, and both are paid every turn.
