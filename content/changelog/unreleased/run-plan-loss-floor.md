## What changed

- A plant never planned to make less than it sold last turn. A plant selling
  every unit below its running cost therefore kept running in full, its company
  lost money every turn, and when the cash ran out every plant it owned stopped
  at once. Car makers did this when car prices fell sharply.
- At the next step of the switch, a plant whose units sold for less than their
  inputs, wages and site costs makes about a tenth less each turn, even below
  last turn's sales, until it pays its way again.
- Plants that sell at a profit keep the same guarantee.
