## What changed

- While a product is in development, your R&D budget pays for it first, and
  only what is left over earns research points. The Tech tab and the CEO budget
  panel used to show a gain that was never delivered. They now show +0/turn,
  name the product being funded, how much development cost is still to pay, and
  how fast your points are fading.
- Laws passed under the newer program system (such as a care cost cap) now show
  up in the Code of National Law and in state law books. They file under the
  right title, such as Healthcare, show the chosen option and the turn they took
  effect, and count toward "last enacted".
