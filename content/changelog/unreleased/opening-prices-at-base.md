summary: >-
  A fresh world now starts the funded market with every commodity price at its
  base price instead of the highs left over from the setup turn.
tags: [economy, corporations, world]
badges: [patch]

---

A freshly reset world used to carry the setup turn's prices into the funded
market: most goods opened at 1.7 to 2.5 times base, and financial services at
2.4 times. Computer-run companies rushed into whichever sector looked dearest,
overbuilt it, and then lost money as the price fell back.

Switching a world to the funded market now puts every commodity price, in every
country and state, back at its base price, and clears the scarcity and memory
multipliers that had piled up. Fixed prices set by an admin stay as they are.
Money, stock and plant capacity are not touched.

Prices then move on supply and demand as usual from the first funded turn.
