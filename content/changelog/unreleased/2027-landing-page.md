## What changed

- The 2027 landing page has its own headline text and era tags: automation, climate pressure, cyber operations, treaty trade, ageing populations and the Quebec question.
- A news ticker runs along the globe from January 2027 to 2050, with each headline filed from a place on the map.
- The idle globe tour now visits the years ahead: Canada opening to players, the Quebec referendum, cyber operations, the AI labour shock, treaty trade, the research tiers and the shared moments such as the return to the Moon.
- The country list shows the real 2027 world: the United States and Canada are open to players, the United Kingdom, Germany, Japan and China run on their own, and sixteen more countries are marked as background nations.
- The globe colours those countries the same way, instead of showing the 2019 roster.
- Other start dates are unchanged.
