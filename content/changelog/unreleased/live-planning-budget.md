title: The market plans production more carefully each turn
summary: >-
  The market gets more time to settle how much each plant should make, so
  production plans swing less from one turn to the next.
tags: [economy, turns]
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine]
---

- Each turn the market works out how much every plant should make. To keep
  turns short it had time for only one pass, and thousands of plants ended a
  turn with plans still moving by a lot, which fed swings between gluts and
  shortages.
- It now has time for about four passes. Turns take about a minute longer.
