title: Defence tender maximums follow live lot prices
summary: Open defence tenders raise their maximum when plant costs climb past the published cap, including yen-scale budgets.
badges: [patch]
areas: [backend]
tags: [defence, government-contracts]
---

When a plant's current permitted price sits above a listed defence tender maximum, the open tender maximum is raised to that live floor and the reserved funding is topped up. New tenders still default to a maximum that covers the live floor, so yen-scale budgets are not frozen below what plants can bid.
