## What changed

- Every wiki page has a section tree on the left with all categories and pages. The page you are on is highlighted and each group collapses.
- Article pages show an At a glance box on the right when the page has a key and value table or an infobox, followed by the page contents. On phones the tree opens from a bar at the top, the glance box sits above the article and the contents become a small dropdown.
- Tables, formulas, headings and links in articles are tighter and easier to scan. Election pages, party pages, seats, leadership pages and learning paths use the same look.
- Press / to search, j and k to move between sections, and g then t to jump to the section tree.
