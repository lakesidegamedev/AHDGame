## What changed

- A fusion breakthrough can happen from 2042 in research-heavy countries with a working energy framework. It is good news: fast-track licensing, demonstration funding or a grid build-out each bring cheaper power sooner, and doing nothing still helps, only less.
- An election integrity crisis can start from 2028 where trust in the count is already weak. Platform rules, independent audits or civic education each change how far it goes.
- Both come in waves, end on their own, and rest for two years before they can return. Neither is scheduled. Fusion does not count as a severe crisis, so it never blocks real crises from being tracked.
- The year-by-year content check now covers 2027 to 2059, and every year has living crises, events and offers.
