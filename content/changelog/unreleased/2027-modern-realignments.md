## What changed

- From 2028, voters with degrees drift left on social questions and voters without them drift right, wherever enough adults hold degrees for the divide to show.
- From 2028, younger voters lean further toward climate action and new housing, while the oldest voters drift the other way as cohorts replace one another.
- From 2030, in any state where unemployment stays above 6.5 percent, low income and non-degree voters swing toward the populist mix of economic left and social right.
- From 2030, in any state where net migration runs above 0.8 percent a year, non-degree, middle income and older voters move toward the restrictive side.
- Your laws can push back against all of these, the same way they can against earlier realignments.
- Worlds started from earlier dates are unchanged.
