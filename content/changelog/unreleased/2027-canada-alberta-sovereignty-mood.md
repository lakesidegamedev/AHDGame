## What changed

- Alberta starts with a Sovereignty Desire of 24, well below the 60 needed to request a referendum, and it drifts each turn like Quebec's.
- The Alberta Sovereignty Party starts with a small share of the Alberta vote and none elsewhere. Rising desire moves Alberta votes toward it and away from the two largest federal parties.
- The Premier of Alberta sees the Sovereignty tab with Federalist, Autonomist and Pro-Sovereignty stances and Ottawa as the national government.
- Alberta cannot yet hold a referendum. That opens once its successor country is built.
