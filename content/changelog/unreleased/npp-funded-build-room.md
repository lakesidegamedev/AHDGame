## What changed

- Computer-run companies now judge the room to build from the same order book your build screen uses. Before, they missed orders from governments and from companies buying materials to build. Timber, ordnance and construction services could look full to them even when buyers were going without.
- How much they build each turn now depends on the size of the shortage. Before, every country was limited to six new plants per good each turn. In a large shortage they now order up to half of the unmet demand in a turn, counting only what is not already being built.
- Plants under construction count against the shortage only for the part not yet running, and only for their own resource. A new gas well no longer counts against a timber shortage.
- Construction services and freight are measured across the whole country, because they sell anywhere in it.
- Nothing changes for markets that are already supplied. Computer-run companies still need a profitable plant and enough cash before they build.
