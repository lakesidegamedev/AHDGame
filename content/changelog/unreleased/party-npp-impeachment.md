title: Let party legislators file articles of impeachment
summary: Authorized party chairs can direct a seated NPP to file impeachment articles from legislature and executive office pages.
tags: [legislature, impeachment, npps]

---

Party-sponsored articles use the existing bill-sponsorship authority and availability checks.
Presidential cases require a lower-house sponsor; governor cases require a legislator in that state.
Each voting stage now lasts 24 turns. New articles are blocked for 72 turns after the target's latest election win, including re-election. Voting thresholds, target checks and the 48-turn refiling cooldown still apply. Existing open stages retain their saved deadlines.

Votes display the filer's name and, for new NPP-sponsored articles, the requesting player's name.

New optional case attribution fields distinguish the NPP filer from the requesting player.
Existing character-filed cases remain readable without migration; the existing impeachment collection reset policy covers both.
