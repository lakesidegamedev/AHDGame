summary: You can again try to take whole plants from a rival's sector. Marketing strength sets how many you can take, R&D sets your chance of success.
tags: [corporations, plants, competition]
badges: [minor]
areas: [fullstack]
---

- Split rival plants is available again on a rival's sector page.
- Your marketing strength against the rival's sets the share at stake: 1% of their plants at equal strength, rising to 20% when yours is twice theirs.
- Your R&D score against the rival's sets the chance the split succeeds, between 5% and 95%.
- Plants you win move to you intact. The rival always keeps at least one plant.
- The cash and marketing strength you commit are spent whether the split succeeds or fails.
- AI-run corporations now use the same rule when they attack, instead of the old capacity capture.
