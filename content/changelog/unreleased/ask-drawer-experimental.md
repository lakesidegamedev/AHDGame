## What changed

- Settings has a new Experimental Features section. It is off unless you turn
  it on, and it only changes things for you.
- With it on, an Ask tab sits on the edge of most pages. Opening it slides in
  Ask Keir below the top bar, on the left or right side as you choose.
- The drawer works like the Ask Keir site: threads, answers with sources,
  follow-up suggestions, answer modes and live game data. It uses your game
  account, so your history and daily limits are the same as on the Ask Keir
  site, and a link opens the full site.
- When you ask, the drawer can read the page you are looking at. A chip under
  the question box shows which page it will read; click it to stop sharing.
- A line under the question box shows how many questions you have left today.
  Click it for the full usage bars and the reset time.
- When you run out of questions, the drawer lists the Patreon tiers above
  yours with their current prices.
- The Patreon tiers in Settings show the current Patreon price. The Supporter
  tier was listed at $4.99; it is $3.00.
- Fixed: on a phone, a corporation page's tab bar now stays below the top bar
  instead of sliding under it.
