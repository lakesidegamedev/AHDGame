## What changed

- All 13 Canadian provinces and territories elect a provincial or territorial legislature, sized to its real seat count.
- Each legislature votes on its own four-year schedule, starting from its next fixed election date: Alberta, Manitoba, Prince Edward Island and the Northwest Territories in 2027, British Columbia, Saskatchewan, Nova Scotia and New Brunswick in 2028, Ontario, Newfoundland and Labrador, Yukon and Nunavut in 2029, and Quebec in 2030.
- Seats are shared out by each party's vote in the province, the same way Canadian Commons seats are.
- The legislatures start empty in a new 2027 world and fill at their first election.
- The Northwest Territories and Nunavut use party contests in the game, although their real legislatures run without parties.
