## What changed

- A country can have only one Cyber Attack crisis active at a time. A new operation against a country that already has one is refused before launch, with a clear reason and no charge to your appropriation.
- Computer-run governments follow the same rule and skip a rival that is already under attack.
- The Cyber Operations console shows the reason next to the target, and the wiki page explains the rule.
