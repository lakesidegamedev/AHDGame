## What changed

- Alberta has its own country definition, kept out of every era and invisible to players until it secedes.
- An independent Alberta would have a Premier, an 87-seat Legislative Assembly elected across six regions (Calgary, Edmonton, Central, Southern, Foothills and Rockies, and Northern Alberta), a Regional Council and Regional Commissioners.
- Alberta keeps the Canadian dollar and the Bank of Canada, with an energy and extraction focused economy.
- Referendum requests for Alberta remain closed; nothing can pass into the new country yet.
