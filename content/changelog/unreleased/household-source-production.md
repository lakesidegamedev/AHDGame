- Decode the 1992 CPS household/person records, ONS 1991 income deciles and public e-Stat 1989 regional table without inventing missing population weights, household composition or fiscal flows. Preserve source populations, survey weights, missing symbols and mixed annual/monthly currency units.
- Run current household source preparation during metric publication. Economic-owner records, independently settled all-household fiscal controls and actual enacted-law cash payments must reconcile before a country can adopt the household definition. Budget forecasts, player-only tax receipts and booked regional allocations are not background household receipts.
- Allocate current paid benefits only to reviewed eligible groups for the current law option. Services are not cash, and unfunded programs do not retain historical survey benefits.
- Retain one bounded latest production status with the existing world-bound board CAS. Missing data leave countries on legacy; missing inputs after adoption retain the prior measurement date. History remains append-only and independent of readiness diagnostics.
- The acquired public sources do not yet qualify any real country for activation. No synthetic reference groups, current fiscal receipts or live database changes are created by the import tools. Complete current background-household owner records and independently reconciled controls are required, not inferred from median income or GDP. Existing economic and fiscal phases do not yet emit those complete inputs; this change supplies their guarded consumer, not the missing upstream writers.
