## What changed

- In a 2027 world, the pound, yen, real, yuan, naira, krona and lira start at their 2024 average rates against the dollar instead of older placeholder values. Sweden's krona, for example, now opens near 10.6 to the dollar rather than 4.3.
- Turkey's 2027 budget, bonds and currency use the modern lira (TRY). Turkey's and Sweden's government budgets were resized to the new rates, so their economies are the same size in dollars as before.
- The Hungarian forint, Polish zloty and Romanian leu can now be traded in a 2027 world. Hungary also gets its own central bank, since its budget and bonds are in forints. Poland and Romania will get theirs once their national budgets are written.
- A 2027 world no longer creates an East German mark market, since East Germany does not exist in that world.
- Russia's regional budgets are now skipped outright when Russia is a background country, instead of starting and doing nothing.
- In every world, filing new election challengers takes far fewer database calls per turn, so turns with many open primaries finish sooner. Who gets filed, and for which party, is unchanged.
- Apart from that speed-up, worlds started from any other date, and the live world, behave exactly as before.
