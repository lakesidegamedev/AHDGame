title: Preserve wiki articles and historical office dates
summary: Wiki bootstrap preserves existing articles, and former office holders no longer appear current when historical dates are missing.

areas: [fullstack]

---

Country wiki bootstrap now preserves existing articles, edits and metadata. Archived office holders retain their real-world departure date when a world ends, and missing historical dates no longer appear as a current tenure.

Presidential wiki history uses the winner and party names frozen in election-night results. Later party or profile renames no longer rewrite those terms or create an extra tenure.

Executive handovers also record the holder's name and party label at appointment time, including parliamentary heads of government.
