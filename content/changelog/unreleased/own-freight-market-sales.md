## What changed

- Own shipments first now covers goods you sell to the general market, not only
  hauls between your own sites. Your trucks in a state carry your share of what
  leaves that state before other companies' goods.
- Goods carried on your own trucks are not billed: no freight charge to the
  buyer and no haul income for the state fleet. Anything beyond your own
  capacity bills as before.
- Companies with no freight in a state see no change there.
- Rivals shipping out of a state where one company owns most of the freight get
  the remaining capacity, so their shipments can be tighter.
