## What changed

- Your company's own sites take its output first by default. A company that
  explicitly chose to sell on the open market first was still sending its
  output to its own sites before any outside buyer.
- That choice now works. Outside buyers get your output first. Your own sites
  then take what is left, free as before, and buy anything else they need from
  other sellers.
- Companies that never changed their supply setting keep own sites first.
