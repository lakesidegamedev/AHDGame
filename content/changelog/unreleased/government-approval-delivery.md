## What changed

- Crisis choices, aid outcomes and world events commit their effects with their completion records.
- Concurrent addresses cannot stack approval bonuses by bypassing an office cooldown.
- Crisis accountability records the responsible country, region and party, including NPP responses.
- Housing approval conditions respect converted payment-burden units. Performance baselines reject incompatible metric definitions.
- Map and election approval readers use the correct stored regional and country identity.
- Fractional approval reversals retain their precision, and regional derived totals stay consistent.
- Covert discoveries commit their approval consequences with programme changes. The war-entry debt brake reads debt-to-GDP as a ratio.
