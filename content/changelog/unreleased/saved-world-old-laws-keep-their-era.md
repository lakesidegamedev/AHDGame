date: 2026-11-11
title: "Older laws in running worlds still wait for their year"
summary: >-
  In worlds started before the new law books, old laws such as China's Common
  Prosperity or Belt and Road can again only be proposed from their historical
  year.
tags: [legislation]
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine]
---

Germany, Japan, China, the United States and the United Kingdom moved to new
law books, and their old law types left the list of laws new worlds start
with. Worlds started earlier still have those old laws. Without this fix, the
old laws that only open in a later year would have been open to propose at any
time in those worlds: for example China's Common Prosperity (2021), Belt and
Road (2013) and AI strategy (2015) in a world still in 2006.

Each old law now keeps the first year it had before, so proposing it waits for
that year as it did before the move.
