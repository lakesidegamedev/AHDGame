## What changed

- Every opening computer-run private company starts with one standard plant for its sector, the same size in every country and region. Bigger economies get more room through the market, not bigger starting companies.
- A new world no longer carries a stored "unowned market" for each region and sector. Nothing about the size of a region's economy caps what a company can build; money and the goods market decide.
- State enterprises in planned economies keep their opening size, which still follows regional output.
- The seed diagnostic checks that opening private companies sit at the standard size in each sector.
- The admin tools for seeding and boosting unowned sectors are removed, and a world reset no longer rebuilds them.
