## What changed

- Quebec opens the 2027 world with sovereignty support at 35 out of 100, in line with recent polling.
- The mood moves each turn with the Premier's stance on sovereignty, how popular the provincial and federal governments are, and inflation.
- Referendum requests, Prime Minister decisions and the stance rules now work for any country with such a region, not only the United Kingdom. Nothing changes for UK players.
- Quebec cannot hold a referendum yet. That opens once an independent Quebec can actually be formed.
- Canada still cannot be joined.
