## What changed

- A breakthrough at a mine or well grows the region's deposit only when the deposit is what limits that operation, and only for the resource that is short. A well running freely gets the revenue boost but no new deposit.
- Each breakthrough still adds 1 to 15% of the current deposit, but a region's deposit for any resource now stops at three times its starting value.
- Before this, every breakthrough grew every resource the mine produced, compounding without a ceiling. Some regions had reached hundreds of times their starting deposits, so the deposit limit on new mines never applied there.
- Existing deposits are not changed by this update.
