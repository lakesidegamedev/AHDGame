## What changed

- New product: Consumer goods (furniture, appliances and household items). Manufacturing plants can switch to the new Consumer Goods recipe, which uses plastics, timber, chemicals, electronics, steel and power. It shows up in the markets, in plant output and in the build screen.
- Nobody buys consumer goods yet, so expect no sales until stores and households start buying them in a later update.
- The old retail product is now called Retail baskets so it is not confused with the new one.
- Plants now buy fewer kilowatt-hours and more raw goods: steel and vehicle plants use more iron and coal, building materials and construction use more timber, chemical and plastics plants use more gas and oil, farms use more fertilizer, and freight uses oil. Each recipe keeps the same total input cost, so margins stay about the same.
