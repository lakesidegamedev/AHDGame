title: Turns no longer stop because the market takes a little longer
summary: >-
  The company step of a turn now has ten minutes instead of five, so a large
  market finishes and the turn moves on instead of failing and retrying.
tags: [economy, turns]
badges: [hotfix]
areas: [engine]
---

- The market part of a turn had to finish within about four minutes. As the
  world grew it needed slightly longer, so a turn failed and retried for hours.
- Companies' part of the turn now has ten minutes, which gives the market
  about seven and a half.
