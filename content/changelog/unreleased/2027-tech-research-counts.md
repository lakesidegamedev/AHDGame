## What changed

- A company founded in a late-start world such as 2027 owns the baseline tech of every earlier decade. Those techs alone filled the plant output, input saving, labor, build cost and shield limits, so researching a new tech added nothing.
- Earlier-decade techs now sit outside those limits. The limits apply only to techs from the starting decade onward, plus frontier programmes and methods.
- In the current world nothing changes at the moment of the update: every company keeps the same output, input and cost numbers it had, and new research starts adding on top.
- In fresh worlds, techs from before the starting decade stay owned for prerequisites, production methods and history, but give no running bonus.
- The Tech tab shows the researched bonus against its limit, and the bonus from earlier decades on one separate line. The warning that a tech adds nothing now appears only when the researched part really is at its limit.
