## What changed

- Logistics rows show "Own trucks hauled N TEU" under the Sold reading, counting goods your own trucks carried out of that state last turn, for any buyer.
- It is separate from "to your sites", which counts only freight your own plants buy as input. Nothing about who gets freight space has changed.
