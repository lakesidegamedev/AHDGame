## What changed

- Each of the ten new long-running crises can now seed related local emergencies as it worsens, such as a freight stall after a pandemic, a lender run during a debt wave, or satellite outages after an orbital debris cascade.
- Orbital debris can only start in countries where companies run orbital constellations, and more of them raises the chance.
- Election integrity trouble is more likely where companies run personalised media feeds.
- A country already in its own sovereign default crisis is no longer hit by the debt wave on top of it.
