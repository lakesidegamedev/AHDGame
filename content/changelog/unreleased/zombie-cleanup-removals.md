The corporation guides, wiki pages and tooltips no longer talk about buying a
slice of an unowned market, which stopped existing when markets moved to plants.
Splits are described as what they are now: an attempt to take plants from a
rival. A corporation with an absent CEO is described as losing plant capacity,
which is what happens.

The wiki and guides use "voter groups" throughout, matching the poll and
canvass screens, and the old archetype wording is gone.

Admins can start or call off the German Question from the Crises panel again.
It only opens in a world where West and East Germany are still separate
countries.

Behind the scenes, an unused page-view recorder and an unused code-quality
snapshot job were removed, along with their stored data. Nothing changes for
players.
