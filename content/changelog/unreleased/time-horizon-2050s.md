## What changed

- The era calendar now runs past 2049. A game reaching 2050 is in the 2050s and gets the new-decade news post, instead of staying in the 2040s.
- Once a game passes the last research tier's authored years, that tier is labelled "2029+" rather than "2029 to 2039". Nothing you own or have researched changes.
- A research tier with no cost set now stops the build instead of quietly costing less than every other tier.
- Politician photos dated 2030 or later are now recognised by their year.
