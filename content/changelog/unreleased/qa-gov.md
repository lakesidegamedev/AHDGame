## What changed

- New executive orders signed by a party chair for an office held by a non-player official read "Name (State Party Chair, acting for Governor Name)" instead of crediting the chair alone. The Orders tab explains who the signer is.
- The Policy page header lists tariffs and subsidies next to statutes so the title counts add up, and the Economic and Social figures say whether they are an average of enacted laws or the country's position.
- State and provincial legislature member lists say each row is a party bloc with its leader, and show how many blocs and seats the list covers.
