## What changed

- Bill effects and enactment commit together, including staff signatures and court enactments.
- Election results and office assignments commit together; announcements follow successful commits.
- Party departures remove regional and committee ballots, with membership rechecked when counting.
- Regional incumbents must stand again to retain their leadership roles.
- NPP-held regional executive offices receive bills for assent.
- Regional bill history uses recorded passage, enactment and failure dates.
