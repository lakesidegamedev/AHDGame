## What changed

- In a 2027 world, births per woman start at 1.60 in the United States, 1.45 in the United Kingdom, 1.38 in Germany, 1.15 in Japan and 1.00 in China, and drift slowly toward their 2050 levels. Other countries start at 1.50.
- Family and demography policy can move a country's birth rate by up to 0.3 births per woman either way. Regions keep their own differences around the national level.
- Life expectancy rises by about 0.15 years for every in-game year from 2027 until 2060. Healthcare policy still makes people live longer or shorter on top of that.
- Worlds started from any other date, and the live world, behave exactly as before.
