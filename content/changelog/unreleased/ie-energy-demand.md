## What changed

- Background countries (those with no player-built plants) now share scarce goods on the world market pro rata to what each wants, the same way governments already do.
- Before this, the order was by country code. Austria, Bulgaria, Brazil and Spain filled their electricity demand in full, while Ireland, Greece, Hungary, Poland, Turkey and others got none, so Irish electricity prices sat at more than double the US price.
