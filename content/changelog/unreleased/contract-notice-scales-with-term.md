date: 2026-10-21
title: Long supply contracts take longer notice to cancel
summary: Cancelling a fixed-term supply contract on your own now takes notice in proportion to the term left, up to one day.
tags: [economy, corporations, contracts]
badges: [minor]
areas: [fullstack]
---

- Cancelling a fixed-term supply contract one-sided now takes a quarter of the turns left on it as notice: at least 4 turns, at most 24, and never past the contract's own end.
- Contracts with no fixed term still take 4 turns.
- The contract keeps delivering and settling during the notice, as before. Both sides can still end it sooner by agreeing a shorter term.
- Each active contract now shows when its term ends and how much notice a cancellation would take; contracts under notice show the turn they end.
- AI companies follow the same notice rules.
