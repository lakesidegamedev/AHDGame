Red used to mean four things at once: the main button colour, a loss, some
plain balances and even a "+0%" change. Now red only marks a loss or a figure
over its limit, green only marks a gain, amber marks a warning, and zero or
plain figures are never coloured. Buttons, tabs and links moved to violet in
the Default, Light, OLED and USA themes.

The Actions and Market screens now show money, percentages, changes and counts
in one consistent format each, and changes always carry a sign. The market
index chart no longer draws an empty frame: when there is nothing to chart it
says why.

New screens must follow these rules before they ship, and older screens move
over one at a time.
