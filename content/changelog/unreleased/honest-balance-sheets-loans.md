## What changed

- Outstanding bank construction loan principal counts as debt in a company's credit score and in its share price, wherever the plant under construction counts as an asset.
- Overdrawn cash counts as the negative it is in the share price instead of being treated as zero.
- Companies that paid for their builds in cash are not affected.
