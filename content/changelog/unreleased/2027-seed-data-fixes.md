## What changed

- Italy, Spain, Greece, Austria, Sweden and Turkey no longer show household
  incomes left over in older currency units. Regional incomes and growth in
  background countries are now tied to each country's 2027 national budget.
- Euro area countries open at the European Central Bank's rate instead of the
  double-digit rates of the early 1990s. Sweden opens at a 2027 rate too.
- Russia, Poland and Romania open with a national budget, a central bank,
  government bonds and a funded stock market.
- The reset command can set the new world's iteration label.
