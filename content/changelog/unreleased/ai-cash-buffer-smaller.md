## What changed

- The minimum cash an AI company keeps in reserve is lower: the base reserve drops from 250,000 to 200,000 and the smallest reserve any AI personality can have drops from 125,000 to 100,000.
- AI companies that were held just under the old reserve can now spend on growth and pay dividends sooner.
- Expansion cost, the extra cash needed to expand and dividend caps are unchanged. Player companies are not affected.
- More supply is expected in goods that stay short. Some AI companies may also run closer to their cash limit.
