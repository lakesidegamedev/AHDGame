title: AI companies sell plants outside their industry
summary: AI companies gradually sell plants outside their own industry to an AI company in that industry, so fewer plants run at a loss.
tags: [economy, corporations]
badges: [minor]
areas: [backend]
---

Each AI company can sell one plant per turn that sits outside its industry. The buyer is an AI company in that industry in the same country, and it pays book value plus the stock on hand. The plant keeps its capacity, workers and inventory. Player companies are not affected.
