## What changed

- Background countries now bring about 90% as much iron, coal, timber, rare earth and oil to market as the world's industries buy, instead of 3 to 19 times as much.
- Prices for those five resources should climb back toward their normal level over the next few weeks of turns, and extraction plants should sell nearly everything they make.
- Natural gas was already balanced and is unchanged.
- Background economies also stop being trimmed for failing to sell their surplus.
