## What changed

- Sold percentages now say that buyers split each order across every seller asking the same price, so sites asking the same price show the same figure.
- The Market Position panel labels its total and your share as capacity revenue per turn, and shows what you earned last turn on its own line.
- When paid orders stayed unfilled, the panel says whether you sold out, your asking price was above what buyers paid, or the open orders were with buyers your routes did not reach.
