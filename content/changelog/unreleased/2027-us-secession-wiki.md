## What changed

- The Referendums page has a section on Texas and California: starting desire, who decides, the consent vote in Congress and the new countries' regions.
- After Texas leaves, the US House has 397 seats with a majority of 199, the Senate has 98 and the Electoral College has 498 votes. If both leave, the House has 345 seats, the Senate 96 and the Electoral College 444.
