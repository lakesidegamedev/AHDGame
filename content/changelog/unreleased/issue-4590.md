## What changed

- A retained ground unit stays in the continuing state's army whether its station is a strategic region, empty, or a string that collides with a state id.
- Retained strategic forces still follow their station, because theirs is a real state-level location.
- A ratified Soviet split with Russia continuing now applies instead of rolling back every turn.
