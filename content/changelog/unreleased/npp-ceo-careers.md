- An NPP running your company or subsidiary now has a level from 1 to 10, earned from time in the job and how well its companies beat their rivals. A higher level cuts your management overhead, by up to 2 points.
- Every 24 turns the CEO asks for a raise. The ask grows with its level and with your company's profit, size and growth, up to 10% of profit. Ambitious CEOs ask for more, loyal ones for less.
- You can accept, make a counter offer, or replace the CEO with a new one who starts at a low level. A stubborn CEO may walk out over a low counter offer.
- If you do not answer in time, the "What the NPP may do" card decides: it accepts raises up to the share of profit you set there (3% unless you change it) and otherwise replaces the CEO.
- The salary goes to the NPP itself, who can spend it on politics and investing.
