title: "Main actions and your own standings come first"
summary: >-
  The corporation Sectors tab and the sector page now lead with their main
  action, and the Contests page shows your standings ahead of the prize pool.
tags: [ui, corporations, contests]
badges: [patch]
areas: [frontend]
---

On a corporation's Operations, Sectors tab, the New sector button now sits at
the top of the tab, above the product panels, instead of further down the page.

On a sector page, the CEO's build button now sits in the header, next to the
listing link, so it is the first action you see on any tab. The button inside
the plant panel is still there as a secondary shortcut.

On the Contests page, signed-in players see their own standings as the first
section, with a full heading, and the prize pool in the header is a compact
line instead of a large gold figure. Inside each contest, your rank and gap to
the leader now appear above the podium. Visitors who are not signed in still
see the prize pool at full size.
