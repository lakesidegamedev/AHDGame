- The Green Party and Libertarian Party join the US default roster as Minor parties, with the standard $1,000,000 default-party starting treasuries and provisional game ideology positions.
- Democrats and Republicans retain their existing IDs and opening support. The new parties receive no invented elected seats, registration, or organization.
- Libertarians appear from the 1979 preset onward; the modern Green Party appears from 2007 onward. Availability follows the parties' [1971](https://lp.org/our-history/) and [2001](https://www.gp.org/media_introduction) founding dates.
- State chapters use the parties' actual IDs and preserve developed chapters when reseeded.
