title: "China's provinces follow the national corporate tax rate"
summary: >-
  Each Chinese province keeps a share of the corporate tax raised there. That
  share now uses the rate the national government actually sets.

Chinese provinces keep part of the corporate tax collected in their territory.
After China moved to its new law book, every province used a fixed 25% rate no
matter what the national corporate tax was. Provinces now use the national rate,
including any change a passed bill makes. Worlds started before the new law book
keep the rate they had already enacted.
