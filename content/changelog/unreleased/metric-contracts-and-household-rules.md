- Define target ownership, measurement and national aggregation contracts for all 58 metrics, separately from today's published units and remaining proxy gaps.
- Distinguish actual national program payments from annual funded regional spending and service implementation percentages. Ignore ambiguous or stale opening-law settlement matches rather than choosing an arbitrary receipt.
- Add portable household income, poverty and inequality accounting with shared distributions, paid cash transfers, deductions and national poverty thresholds. These new rules are not yet activated on player boards; country adapters, persistence and migration remain required.
- Require complete reference household inputs and reconciled fiscal flows before switching a country to the new household calculations. Missing or synthetic inputs keep the existing calculation and measurement dates unchanged.
