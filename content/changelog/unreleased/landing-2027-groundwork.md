## What changed

- The landing page reads when the world opens and when the first turn runs
  from the live game settings, and falls back to the announced times
  (Sunday 15 November and Monday 16 November, 5:00 PM Eastern) until staff
  set them. Each countdown disappears once its time has passed.
- New links for the landing page: the 2027 world overview, the November 2026
  State of the Game post, the Lakeside launcher and Discord.
- Country lists in translated copy use the reader's language, so German reads
  "Vereinigte Staaten und Kanada".
- Nothing on the current landing page changes yet; the new design builds on
  this.
