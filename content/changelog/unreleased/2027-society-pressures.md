## What changed

- Humanoid robotics, agentic services, autonomous factories and autonomous freight now staff each unit of capacity with roughly half the usual crew or less. Workers they no longer need lose their jobs at once, so regional unemployment rises.
- Those workers find new jobs over a few years. A wage insurance law, an economy running above trend and a better educated workforce all speed this up.
- Pension and health spending now grow as the population ages. Japan starts with a heavier pension bill than the United States and both rise toward 2050. A pension age indexation law that ties the pension age to life expectancy keeps the pension bill much lower.
- Each region has a new Housing Pressure reading: households against the homes available. New construction adds homes, and housing supply and zoning reform decides how much planned building gets finished. When there are more households than homes, the cost of living rises.
- Each region has a new Adults With Degrees reading. It moves slowly toward a level set by university enrolment and education funding.
- Worlds started from any other date, and the live world, behave exactly as before.
