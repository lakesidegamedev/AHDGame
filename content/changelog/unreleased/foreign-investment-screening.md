title: Foreign takeovers are now screened where the law says so
summary: Under Investment Screening or a share cap, a takeover by a group controlled from abroad goes to the competition authority first.
tags: [legislation, corporations, antitrust]
badges: [minor]
areas: [fullstack]
---

- Where a country's Foreign Investment Act is at Investment Screening or one of the share caps, an acquisition or takeover by a group headquartered abroad is referred to the minister who reviews mergers, even when it is too small for a competition review.
- The minister has 6 turns to block it. If they do nothing, the deal is cleared.
- In those countries a foreign-controlled group cannot buy control of a company on the stock market; it has to make an acquisition offer so the deal can be screened.
