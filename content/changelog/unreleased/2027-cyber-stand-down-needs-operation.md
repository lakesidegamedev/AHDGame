title: "Only an attacker can stand down from cyber operations"
summary: >-
  A country can now stand down only against a country its service has run an
  operation against since the last truce.
tags: [intelligence, cyber]
badges: [patch]
areas: [gameplay, backend]
---

Standing down opens a truce that blocks cyber operations both ways and eases
world tension. Any country could declare one against any other, even one it
had never attacked, which let a country shield itself from attackers and lower
world tension again and again. Standing down now needs an operation against
that country since the last truce, and the intelligence console greys out the
button when there is nothing to stand down.
