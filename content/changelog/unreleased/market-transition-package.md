- When the new market goes live, a business whose real demand is far below what it used to earn shows a Market transition panel for a limited number of turns.
- During that window it gets fading revenue support, and its owner can convert it once to another industry for free, keeping its value, or mothball it with no upkeep.
- A first retool inside the same industry is also free during the window.
- AI companies use the same options when another industry is clearly short.
- The build planner and state economy pages now show real unmet demand, after plants already under construction, instead of unclaimed market share.
