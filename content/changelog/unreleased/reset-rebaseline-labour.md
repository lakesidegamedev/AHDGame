title: An economy reset no longer leaves workers locked out of jobs
summary: Each region's share of jobs held for companies is measured again from the rebuilt companies after an economy reset.
badges: [patch]
areas: [backend]
tags: [economy, employment, reset]

Every region keeps a share of its workforce set aside for company jobs, measured the first time companies post openings there. An economy-only reset rebuilt the companies but kept the old share, so regions whose old companies employed many people kept those jobs reserved for companies that no longer exist, and the workers stayed unemployed. The reset now has each region measure its company job share again from the rebuilt companies, and the rest of its workers return to the wider economy at the normal recovery pace.
