## What changed

- Japan's Chief Cabinet Secretary can view the records of every cabinet office in the same government. They cannot use another office's actions.
- The restricted-office notice now lists the Chief Cabinet Secretary among the offices that may view it.
- The CEO office marketing line shows the net change per turn after decay and the strength your budget holds steady at. It used to show growth before decay, which read as a rise while strength was falling.
- The Discord corporation lookup reports marketing change the same way as the website.
