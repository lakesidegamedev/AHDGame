## What changed

- Personal Campaign Donation now says what really happens: the full amount
  leaves your Cash on Hand, but only half reaches Campaign Funds and the other
  half is lost.
- The Fundraise action is not taxed by your party. Only your per-turn fund
  income is. The fundraising pages now say so.
- Out-of-state costs now say they apply to Support and Attack only, in actions.
  Barnstorm has no distance cost.
- The IMF bailout has no flat GDP penalty. The sovereign default pages no
  longer list one.
- The labour page no longer says players pass minimum wage bills. The game team
  sets that floor for each country.
- The executive term limits page now has an example of how an acting president
  from a House deadlock counts toward the cap.
- New page: Party Slate, covering how party chairs assign players and NPPs to
  races, the 3 candidate limit per race, and when an NPP accepts.
