## What changed

- Wiki pages now show "Checked against version X on <date>". If a page was
  written for an older version, it says so and offers a button to report a
  wrong number.
- When you report a wiki problem, the text you had highlighted is filled in as
  the quote, so moderators can see exactly what you mean. Moderators get a
  Reports list grouped by page with a resolved toggle.
- Wiki search ranks title and section matches first, forgives one wrong letter
  in longer words, and sends you straight to the matching section. Results from
  the docs site appear under a Docs label.
- A small ? button on the poll, impeachment, primary, corporation research and
  budget, market, bill, party leadership and war declaration screens opens the
  matching wiki article.
- Wiki links from the game now stay on the site you are using. A missing wiki
  page suggests the closest matches instead of a dead end.
