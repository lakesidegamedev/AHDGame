## What changed

- Once a Japanese law is in force, the groups it touches (for example
  older voters for health laws, rural voters for farm laws) drift left or
  right in step with the option that passed. Economic laws move economic
  lean; social and security laws move social lean.
- The drift is gradual and capped, and it fades back toward the starting
  position if the law is repealed. National laws are spread across all
  regions; regional laws act on their own region.
- Option costs, scores and budgets are unchanged.
