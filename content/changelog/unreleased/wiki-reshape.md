## What changed

- Each system page is now organised around what you actually do and decide
  there, with headings like "Setting wages" or "When the state sets a floor"
  instead of the same five sections on every page.
- Links to related systems now sit in the sentence that explains the effect.
- Where a system applies, when the page was last checked and the link to the
  exact rules now share one closing line.
- No facts or numbers changed.
