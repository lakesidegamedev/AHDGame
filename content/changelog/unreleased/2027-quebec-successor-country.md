## What changed

- An independent Quebec now has its own country record: a 125-seat National Assembly, a Premier, eight regions from Montreal to Eastern and Northern Quebec, a cabinet, and the Canadian dollar.
- Its voters keep the same groups and leanings as Quebec voters in Canada, so elections after independence pick up where the province left off.
- Quebec does not appear in any world and cannot be joined. A sovereignty referendum still cannot be called; that opens once independence is wired to this country.
