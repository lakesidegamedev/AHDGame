## What changed

- New crises can run on the old crisis rules. Off, a new crisis has no stages, blame, scars or forced events, and wars are not shown as crises.
- The rules that fill empty presidencies and prime ministerships can be switched off. Off, only the United States Vice President steps up when the presidency is empty, as before.
- Leader legacies can be switched off. Off, no legacy is recorded when a leader leaves office.
- Campaign income and costs can stay measured against each era's fixed figure instead of the live economy. Off, campaign money is priced as before.
- New worlds start with all four on. The current world keeps them off until staff turn them on, so nothing shifts in play when the update arrives.
