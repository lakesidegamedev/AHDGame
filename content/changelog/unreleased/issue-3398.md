## What changed

- Austria, Spain, Finland, France, Greece, Italy and Sweden each carry a 1979 income vintage at the national level of their ~1979 seed bundle, labelled as a gameplay level rather than a sourced statistic.
- A 1979 world stamps that vintage so income scoring uses it; 1953 and 2019 worlds keep the anchors they were calibrated against.
- Regions of those countries no longer all score the same on median income in a 1979 world.
