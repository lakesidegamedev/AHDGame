## What changed

- **Campaign tab.** The Actions slot in the navigation is now Campaign, holding
  your Actions and the new Campaign Manager. On phones the Campaign tab opens a
  sheet with both.
- **Find any race.** Filter by race type, stage (upcoming, primary, general or
  finished), region, year and party. A country selector shows any playable
  country's races, and a link to a race opens it in its own country.
- **The map.** Regions are shaded race by race; picking one draws its counties,
  constituencies or districts from that race's results, with neighbours faded
  around it. Drag to move the map, and scroll, pinch or use the buttons to zoom.
- **A race in full.** Polling, results, turnout, the schedule, primaries, seat
  counts for multi-seat races, and the seat's earlier and later races with
  previous and next arrows. Finished races keep their coloured maps.
- **Act on any candidate.** Boost, Attack and Barnstorm cost exactly what they
  cost on that candidate's own profile, a player's or an NPP's. You can also
  endorse in a general, and enter or withdraw from races in your home region.
- **My moves.** Your own actions, the same cards as the Actions page.
- **Old links still work.** Down-ballot election pages, their results and
  region pages, and lower-tier primary pages now open the race in Campaign
  Manager. The presidential election page is unchanged.
- **Campaign HQ is for presidential candidates.** Down-ballot races are run
  from Campaign Manager instead; existing down-ballot campaign HQs are archived
  with their leftover funds kept on record.
