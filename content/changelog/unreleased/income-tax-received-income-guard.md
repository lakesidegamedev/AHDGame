## What changed

- Income that is taxed as it is paid (savings interest in any currency, deposit interest, salary, dividends including ones diverted to a credit line, bond coupons and index fund dividends) never leaves a separate tax bill behind.
- Share sales are taxed when the sale happens, so the proceeds cannot be spent before the bill arrives.
- Automatic checks now run three years of turns for a player with empty wallets and confirm no unpaid tax appears, and that income not yet taxed at source is still billed.
