## What changed

- The Canada page has an Alberta section and lists the Alberta Sovereignty Party.
- After Alberta leaves, the House of Commons has 306 seats with a majority of 154. If Quebec and Alberta both leave it has 228 seats and a majority of 115.
- The Referendums page describes Alberta's six regions and what happens at independence.
