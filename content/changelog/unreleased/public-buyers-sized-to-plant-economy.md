## What changed

- A national healthcare or defense budget is sized for a whole country. The plant market is a thin slice of that country, so the old buying asked for millions of times more than one plant can make, and prices pinned at their ceiling.
- Government buyers now spend only the plant economy's share of each budget line. A country with more plants buys more, and a country with fewer buys less.
- Healthcare services and ordnance no longer sit permanently unmet in the opening world, and the same budget is no longer funded twice.
- Does not change legacy-engine worlds or what any budget pays for in the treasury.
