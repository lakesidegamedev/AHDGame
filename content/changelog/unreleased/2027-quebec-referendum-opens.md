## What changed

- Once Quebec's Sovereignty Desire reaches 60, the Premier can request a referendum. Canada's Prime Minister decides whether to grant it, and the campaign runs like Scotland's or Wales's.
- If Yes wins and the House of Commons passes the independence bill, Quebec leaves Canada as a new country with eight regions, a 125-seat National Assembly and the Canadian dollar.
- A Quebec-homed Bloc Québécois without meaningful presence elsewhere in Canada moves to the new country with its members. Members of Canada-wide parties in Quebec become Independents.
- The sitting Premier leads Quebec as caretaker while the first National Assembly, Prefect and regional council elections run. After that the Assembly sits for four years and passes its own laws.
- The Canada and Referendums wiki pages explain the full process.
