## What changed

- Countries that were planned economies when a world began got no AI-run
  banks, and nothing created them after the country moved to a market
  economy. In a world that started in 1991, these countries reached 2005 with
  no banks, so companies there could never get a loan to build.
- Each turn, any market economy without its two AI-run banks now gets them.
  Banks are only ever created once.
- These countries now have a seat for AI companies in their capital region, so
  AI mining companies can also open there.
- Banks are licensed in the currency their own accounts use, which fixes bank
  creation in worlds where these countries use their modern currencies.
