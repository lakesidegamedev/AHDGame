  When a UK nation becomes independent, recruitment slates of parties that do
  not carry over are now removed instead of being merged under one independent
  slate, so the referendum step completes.

## What changed

- Recruitment slates belonging to parties that dissolve in a seceding nation are removed with their candidate rows.
- Parties that carry over to the new country keep their slates.
