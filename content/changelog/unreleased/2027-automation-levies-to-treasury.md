## What changed

- Companies running automated methods now pay the automation tax and pension levy as a share of what they spend on inputs each turn.
- The money goes to the national treasury of the country that passed the law, and shows up in its revenue.
- Suppliers are no longer paid more because of the levy; the cost lands on the automating company's cash.
- A company that cannot cover the full levy pays what it has, up to its cash on hand.
