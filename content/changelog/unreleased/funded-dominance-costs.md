---
date: 2026-10-30
title: Market leaders no longer pay running dominance costs on what they sell
summary: >-
  Companies that dominate a market were charged a size cost and a compliance
  cost every turn, the compliance cost measured on a revenue figure the plant
  never earned. Under plants, dominance is paid when you build, so those running
  charges are gone, and old stock written down is no longer charged twice.
tags: [economy, corporations, balance]
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine]
---

- A dominant sector on the funded market no longer pays the dominance size cost
  or the dominance compliance cost each turn. Dominance is charged once, in the
  build price, as the plants design intends. The running dominance cost setting
  still applies if it is switched on.
- Compliance and growth spending on a funded turn are now measured on the
  revenue the plant actually sold. Compliance shows on its own line on the
  corporation and sector pages instead of inside running costs.
- Finished stock written down for sitting past its cover no longer charges its
  production cost a second time. That cost was already counted when the stock
  was made.
