title: See what your size and advantages cost
summary: Company pages now show management overhead, forced-sale orders, CEO level and more, so the cost of each advantage is visible before it lands.
tags: [economy, corporations, antitrust, ui]
badges: [minor]
areas: [fullstack]
---

- Overview shows your management overhead in margin points. It grows with every plant your whole group runs, subsidiaries included.
- Antitrust orders are now on the company page with their deadlines: subsidiaries your group must give up under the Subsidiary Limits or Foreign Investment Acts, and any order to bring your share of an industry under a limit. Before, these arrived only as a notification.
- When you build, the price breakdown shows how much of the order you pay extra because of your market share and your share of world demand.
- Each sector's market panel shows your exposure to a sector split: how many plants the strongest marketer already in that market could try to take, and its chance of success.
- The commodity page lists each top producer's share of the market next to its output.
- The CEO pay card shows your NPP CEO's level.
- On a pass-through company, the owner tax line shows how far the phase-in has gone.
- The credit tab shows the one-notch downgrade when a public company's CEO holds over 65% of its shares.
