The Operations section of the CEO Office has a new Build capacity across sectors button. Pick a country and a sector type, choose how much to build in each sector (a number of facilities, or a share of each sector's current capacity), and see the price of every sector before you spend anything. You confirm the exact total, and each sector is then built just as its own Build button would build it, until your cash runs out. Mothballed sectors, sectors listed for sale and sectors with a full build queue are skipped and shown with the reason. Up to 50 sectors per order.
