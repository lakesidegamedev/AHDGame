## What changed

- In a 2027 world, trend growth for the United States, United Kingdom, Germany, Japan and China starts at 1.9%, 1.3%, 1.0%, 0.6% and 3.8% and eases toward 1.5%, 1.2%, 0.9%, 0.5% and 2.5% by 2045.
- The neutral interest rate rises slightly through the 2030s, then eases back. Inflation targets stay where they are today.
- Score bands for growth, jobs, life expectancy, crime and poverty keep moving to 2050, and every other band is explicitly held at its modern value so nothing is cut off at 2040 or 2019.
- Years 1999 to 2026, older start dates and the live world behave exactly as before.
