## What changed

- Japanese laws already carried lean effects in the game's definitions, but the live world's stored laws did not have them yet. They are now written once when the game starts up.
- From the next turn after that, passed Japanese bills and governor orders shift a region's lean.
- Only existing Japanese laws are updated. Nothing else is touched.
