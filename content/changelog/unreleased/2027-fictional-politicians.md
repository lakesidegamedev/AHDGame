## What changed

- The 1991 and 2019 start dates no longer seat real officeholders. Every seated politician now gets a fictional name, the same as any other start date.
- The 2027 world opens with a fictional Republican president and vice president, two years into their term.
- Every Supreme Court that opens with a world now has fictional justices. Each seat keeps its leaning and the year it falls vacant.
- Politician portraits are generic faces only. A portrait never comes with the name of the person in the photo, and the image address no longer gives it away.
- The start-of-game introductions describe each era without naming real leaders.
- The live world is not changed.
