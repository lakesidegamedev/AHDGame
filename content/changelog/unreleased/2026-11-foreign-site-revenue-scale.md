## What changed

- A state's top sectors now rank by value in one common unit, so a foreign company's site no longer jumps to the top of an Italian, French or Spanish state.
- Losing a site (sale, takeover, closure, nationalization) now dents a company's brand by that site's real share of its revenue, even when the company also operates abroad.
- The country revenue comparison, the nationalization target preview and the investor profile add up each company's sites in one currency.
