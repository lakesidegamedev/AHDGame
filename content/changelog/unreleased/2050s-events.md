## What changed

- Eight new crises for 2050 to 2059: heat collapse, coastal storm surge flood, aquifer collapse, autonomous freight stall, automation layoff wave, engineered crop blight, synthetic media market panic and longevity pension strain. Each has a response choice, a funded recovery step and a real outcome. Some are rare random rolls and some only arm when the country is already under strain, such as high unemployment or a weak power grid. Nothing is guaranteed, and a country that is run well can go the whole decade without seeing them.
- Four new offers for the same years: an orbital solar partnership, a living ocean accord, a longevity science compact and an interplanetary supply consortium seat. Taking one up always has a price, declining is free.
- Ten new personal events for the 2050s, some for politicians, some for company owners, plus two worldwide moments: the first crewed landing on Mars and a shared pledge to restore the oceans.
- When no dated personal event fits a player, the game now falls back to the undated pool instead of skipping that player. If even that is empty, the turn records it so the gap can be fixed.
