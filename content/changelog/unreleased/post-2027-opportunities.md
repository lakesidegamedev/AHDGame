## What changed

- **Eight new opportunities.** A trade deal offer, a global minimum tax accord, a climate finance offer from the Climate Adaptation Finance Pact (from 2029), an invitation to join a shared science megaproject (from 2032), Olympic and World Cup hosting bids, a cultural breakout moment and a World Expo bid (from 2033). Every year from 2027 onward has at least four that can come up.
- **Every opportunity costs something.** Leaders choose between two ways of taking it up, each with its own price in growth, jobs, confidence or approval, and can then put money into a shared fund. The payoff only arrives if the country took the offer. Saying no, or not answering in time, costs nothing and gains nothing.
- **At most two serious crises per country at once.** Crises and long-running conflicts now count together. If a third would start while a country already has two, it waits and arrives once one of them ends, instead of piling on or vanishing. Opportunities never count toward the limit.
