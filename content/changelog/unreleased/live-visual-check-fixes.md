  underlined tabs now use the same filled selected pill as other tabs. Market

A check of the live site found controls that still marked the selected option
with a faint tint or an underline, including the market chart's time ranges,
the buy-and-sell toggles, bill filters, demographic tabs and the forex tabs.
They now use the filled pill in the theme's main colour, like every other tab.
The stock exchange picker on the market page now uses the game's own colours
instead of a black bar.

On phones, the market overview cards no longer run off the right edge, the
bill vote chart fits the screen, and a sector's headline figures sit in two
columns instead of a strip that cut off the last figure.

A corporation's initials badge is now readable on the indigo background, the
front page shows a clear focus ring when you move through it with the
keyboard, and the referral code box on the sign-up page shows its hint in full.
