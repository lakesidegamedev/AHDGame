## What changed

- The map fills the main column instead of sitting in a small card, so regions are shaded clearly for every layer.
- Hover a region for details, or select it to see a details panel with a link to the region page.
- Labels hide on regions too small to hold them and show again as you zoom in. You can also turn labels off, and zoom in and out.
- The region list is now below the map instead of beside it. Every layer each country had before is still there.
