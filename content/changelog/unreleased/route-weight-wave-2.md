title: Heavy pages download less
summary: >-
  Office, forex, party hub, central bank, sector, budget, admin, international
  organization and other heavy pages now load 30 to 70 percent less code before
  they can show anything. Panels below the first screen load as you reach them.
tags: [performance, mobile]
badges: [minor]
areas: [frontend]
---

Pages that pulled in every country's full configuration or large rule modules
now load only the labels and fields they display, and secondary panels load
on demand behind a sized placeholder.
