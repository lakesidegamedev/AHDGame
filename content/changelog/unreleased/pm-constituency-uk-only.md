title: "Constituency picker no longer shows for non-UK Prime Ministers"
summary: >-
  Prime Ministers outside the UK no longer see a constituency control they
  could not use.
tags: [profile, politics]
badges: [patch]
areas: [fullstack]
---

Prime Ministers of countries other than the UK saw a "Prime Minister's
Constituency" control on their profile with nothing to choose from. Constituency
selection is a UK feature, so the control now only appears for UK Prime
Ministers and Commons MPs.
