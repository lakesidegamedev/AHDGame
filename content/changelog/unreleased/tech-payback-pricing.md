title: Tech prices match what each tech earns you
summary: A tech's cash price is now about one game year of what it saves or earns your corporation, and each tech card shows its value per turn and payback time.
tags: [corporations, research, plants]
badges: [minor]
areas: [fullstack]
---

- A tech's cash price is now based on what it is worth to your corporation: the labour, inputs or extra output it gives your own plants, times 48 turns. Large and small corporations wait about the same time for a tech to pay for itself.
- Each tech card shows what it is worth per turn and, before you buy it, how many turns it takes to pay back.
- Techs without a running effect, such as build discounts, market shields, marketing strength or new production methods, cost 5% of a day's gross revenue instead of 15%.
- The minimum tech price for very small corporations is unchanged.
