## What changed

- New on a company's Shares tab, under Private sale: **Sell to a specific
  player at an agreed price**. Pick the buyer, the number of shares and the
  total you agreed. The form shows the price per share, the market value of
  those shares and how far the deal sits above or below market.
- The named buyer sees the offer highlighted on the same tab and gets a
  notification. **Pay and take shares** pays the full amount in one payment
  and moves every share at once. No other player can buy into it.
- The seller's shares are held until the buyer pays, the seller withdraws, or
  24 turns pass, the same as an ordinary private listing.
- Every pending and completed negotiated sale is visible to everyone on the
  company page: seller, buyer, shares, price paid and the multiple of market.
- A negotiated sale is refused when either character is still inside its
  first 24 turns, when both characters belong to the same account, while an
  admin has paused player transfers, or when the two accounts are strongly
  linked by alt detection and no moderator has cleared them.
