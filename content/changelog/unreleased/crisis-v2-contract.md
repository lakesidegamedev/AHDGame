Crises are being rebuilt as one system with stages (early signal, onset, peak,
aftermath, resolved), a pressure level that rises and falls with the economy
and with what officeholders do, and a record of who acted and who did not.
This step adds the rules and tests for that system. Nothing is switched on, so
every crisis in the game plays out exactly as before.
