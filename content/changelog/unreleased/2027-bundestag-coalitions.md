## What changed

- A 2027 world seats all 630 members of the Bundestag in the shape of the February 2025 result: CDU 164, CSU 44, AfD 152, SPD 120, Greens 85, Linke 64 and one SSW member sitting as an independent. Before, only 201 seats were filled against a 630-seat majority line, so every German government started as a minority.
- Parties can now refuse to govern with one another. In a 2027 world the CDU, CSU, SPD, Greens, Linke and FDP all refuse to form a government with the AfD. A computer-run government leaves refused parties out of its cabinet, and a party will not quietly prop up a minority government led by a party it refuses. When that costs a natural partner, the leading party reaches further across the spectrum for a majority, such as a Union and SPD coalition. If none exists, it governs as a minority.
- Refusals can wear off. If a party that refuses another loses seats at two elections in a row while the refused party is the largest in parliament, it may drop the refusal.
- In older start dates, Germany's seeded Bundestag is smaller than the full chamber until its first election. Until then, a majority is now measured against the members actually seated.
- Worlds started from other dates otherwise behave as before.
