## What changed

- The turn already worked out the charge for goods one of your sites delivers
  to another, but it was dropped before it reached the site accounts, so every
  site showed no charge and no income. The charge and the matching income are
  now recorded and shown.
- Company cash and the figures used by tax, salary and valuation are unchanged.
