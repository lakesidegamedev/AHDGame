title: "Red now means money lost or an error"
summary: >-
  Low approval and vacant posts now show in amber, and vetoes show as a plain
  outlined label, so red is kept for money lost, negative figures and errors.
tags: [ui, politics]
badges: [patch]
areas: [frontend]
---

Approval ratings no longer turn red. A healthy rating is green, a middling one
is plain, and a low one is amber. On a country's overview, a low approval also
says "low" beside the figure, so the meaning does not rest on colour alone. A
falling approval in the union panels is amber too.

Vetoed bills, and vetoes that survive an override vote, now show as a plain
outlined label in the bill lists, the bill page timeline and the executive's
record. The veto message heading is no longer red.

Vacant posts in the admin corporation lists are amber instead of red. Party
colours are unchanged: a party shown in red is still its own colour.
