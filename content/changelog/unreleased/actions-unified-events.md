title: One Events panel on the Actions page
summary: >-
  Crises, debates and random events now share a single Events panel on the
  Actions page, with anything waiting on your decision listed first.
tags: [ui, actions]
badges: [patch]
areas: [frontend]
---

The separate crisis, debate and random event boxes are replaced by one Events
panel. Each item has an icon for its kind and a short picture banner, and shows
how serious it is and how long you have to respond. Items that need your decision sit at the top and are marked
"Your decision". Finished debates and events drop to short summary rows at
the bottom. Crisis effects beyond the first three now link to the full crisis
page. The panel only appears when something is going on.
