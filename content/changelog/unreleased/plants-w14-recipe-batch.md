## What changed

- Defense plants (standard, munitions, missiles, heavy armor, naval, aerospace) use roughly a quarter to a half fewer inputs.
- Conventional and nuclear energy, logistics (standard, freight only, full service), 5G infrastructure telecom, pharmaceuticals, plastics, iron mining and fracking use about 20 to 28 percent fewer inputs.
- Outputs, build costs and prices are unchanged. Plants you already own pick up the new recipes on the next turn.
