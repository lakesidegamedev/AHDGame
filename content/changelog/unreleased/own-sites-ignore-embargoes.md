## What changed

- A company that owns a farm or mine in one country and a plant in another used to lose that supply when an embargo closed the route between the two countries, even though the goods never left the company.
- Transfers between a company's own sites now go through whatever the embargo says. The plant's own sites are offered to it first, as before.
- Companies buying from other companies are still blocked by the embargo, and tariffs are unchanged.
