## What changed

- Each country now has a technology readiness score built from its research spending, how many adults have degrees, whether it produces the energy it uses, and how open it is to trade.
- A new production method reaches a country 3 years after its research tier opens where readiness is high, 7 years where it is middling and 12 years where it is low.
- If a company in another country already runs the method, a player company can license it and open it up to 3 years sooner, though never before the tier itself opens. AI-run companies wait for their country.
- The production method list shows the year a locked method reaches your country.
- Governments can issue 10 year and 30 year bonds. Longer bonds pay a higher coupon.
- A finance minister can include 10 and 30 year bonds in the bond maturity profile.
- When nobody has set a profile, the country's debt manager chooses the mix itself, borrowing for longer when interest rates are low compared with recent years and shorter when they are high.
- Worlds started from any other date, and the live world, keep the 1, 2 and 5 year bonds and get new methods on the calendar as before.
