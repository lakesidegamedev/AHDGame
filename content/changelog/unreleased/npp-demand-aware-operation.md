title: Operate surplus NPC plants only as demand requires
summary: >-
  Independent NPC companies can reduce the operating share of losing plants
  while retaining paid capacity and restoring operation when current buyers need it.
tags: [corporations, npc, plants]
badges: [minor]
areas: [engine]
---

- Use measured losses and current physical market books to qualify reversible partial operation.
- Restore full operation for named buyers, shortages, unknown books or physical constraints.
- Preserve manual player, caretaker and state-owned operating choices and the separate media policy.
- Apply the same reversible operating rule to owner-enabled autopilot corporations.
