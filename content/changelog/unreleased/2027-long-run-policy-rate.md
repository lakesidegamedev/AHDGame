## What changed

- When inflation runs above or below target in a long-run budget projection, the policy rate now rises or falls one for one with the gap, the way autonomous central bank chairs already do in play.
- The rate has a floor, and with inflation on target it equals the neutral rate, so the published 2027 to 2050 tables are unchanged.
- A hotter-inflation scenario now feeds through into borrowing costs and the interest share of revenue.
