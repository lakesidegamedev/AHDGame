## What changed

- From 2045, legislatures can pass a longevity care act, a fossil fuel sunset act, a digital self-ownership act and a water security act.
- From 2050 they can also pass an advanced AI oversight act, a lifelong pension account act, a zero carbon economy act and a space resources act.
- The United States, Britain, Russia and East Germany get the new laws as program laws. Germany, Japan, China and Ireland get them as seven-option bills.
- Every law has winners and payers. Long-term care helps older people and families but costs taxpayers. A fossil fuel sunset cleans the air but raises bills. Strong AI oversight builds trust but slows research.
- Seven older laws close to new bills when their successor opens: the automation tax, the coal power phase-out, the data protection overhaul, the basic income pilot, the AI liability act, pension age indexation and the clean power standard. A law already in force stays in force with its costs and effects.
- All new laws start with no statute in force, so nothing changes until a bill passes.
