summary: >-
  When a government enacts an austerity package, the sovereign health panel on the
  budget page shows its size, the tax and spending cut split, and the approval it cost.
tags: [budget, countries]

---

Austerity budgets used to appear only in the news. The sovereign health panel now
shows the latest package for about a year: how big it is against the economy, how
much comes from tax rises versus spending cuts, and how many approval points it cost
and how many are still being felt.
