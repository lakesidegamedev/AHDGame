title: Government bond maturity turns are much shorter
summary: >-
  When many government bonds come due in the same turn, each country now pays
  its holders in a few large batches instead of one bond at a time. Holders
  receive exactly the same money.
tags: [performance, bonds, turn]
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine]
---

A country's maturing government bonds are funded from its Treasury in one move
and paid out in batches of 25 bonds. Bonds with a pool float, bank holdings, a
rollover or a short Treasury still settle one at a time as before.
