  An outlet whose editorial stance cost it audience used to be held to a 15% a

  audience cost was read as unsold stock. It now steps up like a neutral outlet.

## What changed

- Advertising sold through market clearing now records the audience fit the
  outlet's editorial stance applied to its offer.
- A media outlet that sold its whole offer into a book with buyers left over
  now claims that unmet demand, instead of being mistaken for a weak seller and
  creeping up 15% a turn. Neutral outlets are unchanged.
