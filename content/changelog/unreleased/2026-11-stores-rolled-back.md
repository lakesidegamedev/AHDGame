  The store system for retail companies is rolled back. Retail companies sell
  their own product again while stores are reworked to pay their way.

Retail companies went over to a store system that resold household goods at a small markup, but their running costs were still sized for their old sales, so every retail company lost most of its revenue. Retail is back to selling its own product as it did before. Stores will return once they are sized to earn a fair profit.
