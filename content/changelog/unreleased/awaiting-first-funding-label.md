## What changed

- A newly passed state law programme now reads "Awaiting first funding" on the metrics board, with how many turns remain until the first annual funding round.
- "Implementation stalled" is kept for programmes that have been funded but are not delivering.
- No game rules changed. Funding amounts, timing and metric pressure are the same.
