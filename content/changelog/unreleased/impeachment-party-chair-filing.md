title: Party chairs can have a seated party legislator file impeachment
summary: >-
  A party's national or state chair can now start an impeachment against a state
  governor through one of the party's seated legislators, even when the chair
  holds no seat. Filing directly from a seat is unchanged.
tags: [impeachment, legislature, parties, states]
badges: [patch]
areas: [backend]
---

A party chair who holds no seat in a state legislature can now direct a seated party legislator to file articles of impeachment against that state's governor. The case keeps the filing player as the filer and records the legislator who filed on their behalf. Filing directly from a seat works as before, and impeaching a president still requires a seat in the lower chamber.
