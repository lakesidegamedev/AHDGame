## What changed

- On desktop, menus open as grouped cards for every player. You can pin destinations and reorder each group from the gear menu.
- On phones, the bottom dock is the normal way to get around for every player with a character.
- To change either layout, go to Settings, then Appearance. If you already picked the standard menus, you keep them.
- The ASCII page decode is still an experiment. It only shows when you turn on experimental features.
