## What changed

- Added a seller preference rule for the producer market. When it is on, a buyer with a free choice of sellers splits its order across every eligible seller instead of buying only from the cheapest one. This covers household and business purchases of world-market goods as well as public orders.
- A seller's share falls steeply as its price rises above the cheapest seller, and rises a little with its brand loyalty and product quality.
- Each seller is still paid its own posted price, and named suppliers, contracts and a company's own sites work as before.
- The rule is switched off until it is enabled, so nothing changes for players yet.
