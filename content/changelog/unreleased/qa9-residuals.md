## What changed

- Computer-run companies no longer pay for research that only adds input savings beyond the cap. They value a research node the same way the tech tab shows it to players.
- When a computer-run supplier counters your supply proposal, the counter keeps your price mode: a market plus or minus offer gets a new percentage, a set price offer gets a new set price.
- Supply agreements between computer-run companies now use market plus or minus pricing, with the same premium they already chose. Existing agreements are unchanged.
- German translations were added for the sector forecast reasons, supply agreement money readouts, run hold messages and campaign manager notes.
