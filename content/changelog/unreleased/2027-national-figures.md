## What changed

- The United Kingdom, Germany, Japan, China and eleven background countries (Ireland, Brazil, Nigeria, France, Italy, Spain, Sweden, Turkey, Austria, Finland and Greece) now start a 2027 world with projected 2027 population, GDP, government spending, revenue, debt, interest, growth and inflation, instead of figures carried over from an earlier start date.
- Defence budgets for the United Kingdom, Germany, Japan and China start at their announced 2027 levels.
- Russia, Poland and Romania start with updated 2027 population and GDP.
- Regions in the United States, the United Kingdom, Germany, Japan and China start with 2027 conditions: public debt, house and office prices, renewable energy, robot use, health coverage, waiting times, child poverty, homelessness, birth rates and more.
- Turkey's provinces now add up to its 2027 population.
- Background countries are checked on their national figures only, because they do not run regional simulations.
- Worlds started from any other date, and the live world, behave exactly as before.
