On a phone, the seat chart and the passage summary under it on a bill page now
fit the screen instead of running past the right edge.

On a corporation's overview, the Sectors and Operations section headings had
picked up a filled colour bar meant for selected tabs. They are plain headings
again. A few status labels and count badges, such as the regional order label
and union service status, also went back to their light tint, since they are
labels rather than options you pick. On a phone, a sector's last headline
figure now spans the row instead of leaving an empty grey cell.
