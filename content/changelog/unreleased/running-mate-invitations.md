A presidential nominee could put any player on their ticket as running mate
without asking, and that player became Vice President if the ticket won. Naming
a running mate now sends that player an invitation. They answer from the
election page with Accept or Decline, and only an accepted invitation puts them
on the ticket.

The nominee sees who they invited and that the answer is still pending, on both
the election page and the campaign page. When a primary ends without a running
mate, the game invites the best-liked fellow party member instead of adding them
directly.

Accepting one ticket closes any other invitations in the same race, and a
player can only be the running mate on one ticket per race.
