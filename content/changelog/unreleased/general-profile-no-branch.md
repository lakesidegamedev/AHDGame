## What changed

- The Branch tile is gone from the general profile, and the Commissioned entry
  in the service record no longer names a branch.
- Generals have no branch of their own. The defence holder can give a general
  units of any branch, so the old label (Army for most, Navy for naval) could
  mislead players into thinking a general could be moved between branches.
