## What changed

- Figures in company lists, such as net income and net margin, always show in full. A long label shortens instead, with the full label on hover.
- A headline figure with a note, like net income marked projected, shows the note underneath so neither is cut off.
- The United States overview banner uses a wider photo of Mount Rushmore, so all four faces show instead of a slice through the eyes.
- With experimental features on, the page decode shows a small Loading label while the page assembles.
