## What changed

- New plant orders in modern-era worlds are priced at about one ninth of the old list price, before your usual rate, CEO, tech and location adjustments.
- The cost to found a sector (entry fee and starter plant) is unchanged.
- Plants you already own keep their value on the books. Nothing is written down.
- Computer-run corporations still only grow into unmet demand, so cheaper builds do not flood markets.
- 1991-start worlds already priced builds at one day of output and are unchanged.
