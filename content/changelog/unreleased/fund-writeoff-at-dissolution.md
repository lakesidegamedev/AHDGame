date: 2026-10-19
title: Index funds drop wound-down companies straight away
summary: Index funds remove shares of a company wound down with nothing left for shareholders as soon as it closes.
badges: [patch]
areas: [backend]
tags: [index-funds, corporations]
---

When a company is wound down with nothing left for its shareholders, index funds now remove its shares immediately. Before, those worthless shares stayed in the fund at their last price until the fund's next rebalance, so the fund's value read slightly high in the meantime.
