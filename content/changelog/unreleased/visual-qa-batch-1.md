## What changed

- The bottom bar shows your company's ticker symbol instead of a name cut off mid-word, and a share price that did not move is no longer coloured green.
- Your position, your party and your state no longer sit on top of each other on the profile compass.
- Campaign cash and personal cash on your profile use the same number format. Hover for the exact amount.
- The corporation sectors table fits on a normal desktop screen. Wide tables now show a soft shadow on the side that still has more columns to scroll to.
- The share price chart on a company page prints its own change beside it, so a red line under a green daily change reads as two different time spans.
- Revenue growth shows a plus or minus sign, not just an arrow.
- The CEO Office tab uses the same colour as the other tabs.
- The Management overhead line no longer prints its explanation over the label. Hover it for the explanation.
- Congress bill lists label the two vote counts Aye and No.
- Whip FOR and Whip AGAINST buttons have equal weight and neither uses the party colour.
- Bill provisions no longer repeat "Metric effect information unavailable" under every line.
- The budget balance bar on a country page grows from zero, so a large deficit shows as a long red bar instead of an empty one.
- The bond market demand box no longer shows when there is no signal to show.
- A finished crisis in your notifications is shown in green, not alarm red.
- Campaign Manager map labels no longer print over each other when zoomed in.
- World map filters wrap onto a second line instead of hiding past the edge.
- Vacant national leaders show an empty seat instead of a "V" avatar.
- Disabled buttons, such as Send on an empty reply, now look clearly switched off.
- Portfolio tables use "Gain/loss" instead of "Unr. P&L", with the meaning on hover, and the Price heading matches the others.
- Small wording fixes: "1 turn" instead of "1 turns", and "Inactive CEO share release warning" in settings.
