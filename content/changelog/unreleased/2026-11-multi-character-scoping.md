## What changed

- The one-corporation limit now applies to each character, not each account. A
  second character can found a corporation while the first still owns one.
  A character that already owns one is told "This character already owns a
  corporation."
- Corporation alerts, such as R&D breakthroughs, now go only to the character
  who runs that corporation. Other characters on the account no longer see them
  or count them in their unread badge.
- Keir's tips are re-read from your live figures every hour, and his influence
  figure uses one decimal like your profile and the Suggestions page.
- The money history line called "Office income" is now "Campaign fund income".
  It is the hourly campaign money everyone earns, plus a bonus when you hold
  office, so it appears even if you hold no office.
