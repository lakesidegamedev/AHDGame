## What changed

- In a 2027 world the presidency and vice presidency open with a fictional Republican ticket, two years into the term that began in January 2025, instead of unnamed placeholders.
- The United States, United Kingdom, Germany (through the European Central Bank), Japan and China open a 2027 world on interest rates close to their latest real decisions: 3.6%, 3.75%, 2%, 0.75% and 3%.
- The opening-week pause on company bond issues, which ended in August 2026, has been removed. Companies could already issue bonds normally, so nothing changes in play.
- Worlds started from any other date, and the live world, behave exactly as before.
