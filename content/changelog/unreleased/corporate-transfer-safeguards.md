---
title: Corporate ownership and new-player safeguards
date: 2026-10-31
tags: [corporations, shares, accounts]
---

- Corporate acquisitions and forced minority buyouts account for investment holdings, bank equity and debt when protecting shareholder value.
- New characters wait 48 turns before transferring funds or creating a corporation. Corporation creation is exempt during the world founding period; ownership-transfer restrictions still apply.
- The corporation founding countdown includes the new-character waiting period.

- Public-float retirement pays the equity market pool. Forced mergers cannot discard public investors or investment holdings.
