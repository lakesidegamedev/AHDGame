## What changed

- When founding a corporation or changing its sector focus, the sector list now shows "Manufacturing: Automobiles" right after Manufacturing and "Media: Entertainment" right after Media.
- Nothing changes in how these work: an automobile maker is a Manufacturing corporation and an entertainment house is a Media corporation, as before.
