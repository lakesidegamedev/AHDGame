- Since 10 October every business has paid a flat fee of about 8 parts in every 100 of its income. It was added when profits were much higher than they are now.
- Removing it raises every business's profit margin by about 8 points from the turn it takes effect.
- It is switched on as one step of the market recovery, checked the turn after, and switched back if anything goes wrong.
