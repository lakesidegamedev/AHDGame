- The corporation overview now has a What grows this corporation panel for the CEO.
- It lists sectors that sell everything they make, with a link straight to building more plants there.
- It lists sectors that make more than the market takes, so you know not to build there.
- It lists sectors where part of the output never reaches a buyer, so you can fix freight first.
- It points to the Tech tab and to splitting rival plants as the other ways to grow.
