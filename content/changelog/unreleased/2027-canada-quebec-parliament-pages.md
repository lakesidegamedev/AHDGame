## What changed

- Canada's legislature page shows the House of Commons: its members, bills, leadership, tariffs and subsidies. Seated MPs can propose bills from it, and the Prime Minister can call a snap election.
- If Quebec becomes independent, its National Assembly gets the same page, led by the Premier, with MNAs proposing bills.
- The seat count in the page header is the live size of the chamber, so Canada's Commons shows 265 seats after Quebec leaves.
- Independent Scotland and Wales use the same page as before. Its leadership note now says the First Minister leads the government rather than a devolved government.
