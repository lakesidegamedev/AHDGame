## What changed

- The line under the naval and air figures used to say you held enough of the water to put marines ashore. There is no landing order, so it read as an action you could take.
- It now says whether your marines on that front are supplied across the water and fight at full strength, or are cut off and fight as light infantry.
- The wiki sea control chart now marks the same line (40) instead of a landing threshold.
- The line follows the sea control level where the penalty actually starts (40), not the old 65 landing figure.
- Combat is unchanged. This is a display fix only.
