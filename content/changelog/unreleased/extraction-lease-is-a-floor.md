date: 2026-10-18
title: Extraction leases no longer limit how much you can dig
summary: A company holding an extraction lease keeps its reserved share and can still dig from the shared part of the deposit like everyone else.
tags: [economy, extraction]
badges: [minor]
areas: [backend]
---

- Holding an extraction lease used to limit a company to its leased share of the deposit, even when nobody else was mining there. Buying a 10% lease could cut a mine's output to a tenth of the deposit.
- A lease is now a guaranteed minimum. The company keeps its reserved share, and anything it wants beyond that comes from the shared part of the deposit on the same terms as every other mine.
- Leased capacity a holder does not use still stays reserved for them.
