## What changed

- The Canada page and the Referendums page explain that after Quebec secedes the House of Commons has 265 seats with 133 needed for a majority, and the Senate keeps 81 senators.
- Both pages say Quebec takes the share of Canada's federal debt and treasury cash that matches its share of Canada's GDP.
