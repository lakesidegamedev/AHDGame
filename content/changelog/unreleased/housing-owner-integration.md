- Housing costs respond to current disposable household resources, consumer prices, housing pressure and funded housing-cost relief. Restricted relief is not counted again as cash income.
- Existing housing supply, relief and homelessness laws use their enacted option and current funding. National support requires actual paid outlays; regional support uses explicitly modeled funded annual accrual, not an assumed cash receipt.
- Residential projects require financing, local construction-service capacity, planning and a construction delay. Business facilities and construction-service output are not completed homes. Background residential investment and stock geography use documented model assumptions, not bank disbursements or observed household records.
- Housing placements require funded support and available homes. Country rates use pooled payments, incomes, dwelling stocks and population instead of averaging regional percentages.
- Incomplete countries retain legacy calculations. Missing inputs after adoption retain the last valid measurement rather than reverting definitions. Updates append sampled history and preserve existing points; exact retries do not complete or fund homes twice.
