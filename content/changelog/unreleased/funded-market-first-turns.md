date: 2026-10-29
title: Companies short on cash run smaller, not stuck
summary: >-
  A company that cannot afford its whole operation now runs the share it can
  staff and supply, and prices respect their per-turn limits.
tags: [economy, corporations, prices]
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine]
---

- A company short on cash pays its site bills, then runs the share of its plants
  it can both staff and buy materials for. It no longer pays every wage with
  nothing left for materials, and it owes no wages for work that never ran.
- Commodity prices now stay within their per-turn move limit and their overall
  ceiling on the final price, not only on part of the formula.
- AI companies no longer shut a plant for having no sales when it could not
  produce anything to sell.
- A company founded after the economy switch can no longer stop a turn.
