## What changed

- Search suggests the closest page, company or politician when your word is a typo, and retries with your longest word when a full name finds nothing. Results that matched only in a description or page text now say so.
- The national history panel on a country page now shows the same GDP as the rest of the page.
- Each organisation ballot gets a one line summary of yes, no and abstain votes and who is still to vote.
- The organisation fund line says the dues rate is charged on each member's GDP.
- Country party lists show seats out of the chamber total and the vote share on separate lines.
- Older country briefs now describe a bond's coupon as fixed and its price as the price when the brief was written, and budget package amounts appear in local currency.
