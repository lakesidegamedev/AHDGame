## What changed

- On a funded sector, management overhead, the state sector size cost and the running dominance cost are added to the sector's costs as a percentage of its revenue.
- Management overhead on funded sectors has its own switch. The state-owned and dominance costs follow their existing scales.
- Nothing changes while the funded market is off.
