## What changed

- Nominal GDP that follows inflation can be switched off. Off, prices stay at their base level and GDP is not inflated each turn.
- The austerity packages and debt restructurings of AI-run countries can be switched off. Off, no package is enacted and no restructuring fires.
- Crises that start on their own can be switched off. Off, no new crises open from economic conditions, while crises already running continue and resolve as normal.
- New worlds start with all three on. The current world keeps them off until staff turn them on, so nothing shifts in play today.
