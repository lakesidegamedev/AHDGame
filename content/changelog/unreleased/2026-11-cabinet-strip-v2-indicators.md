## What changed

- Cabinet office stat strips and their province or state breakdown now read the current indicators the 2027 world tracks. Canadian offices previously showed blanks for figures such as public trust, corruption and press freedom.
- United States offices now show the same current figures as the country pages, where they could show older numbers before.
- An indicator the 2027 world does not track still shows a dash.
