The United States and Canada keep their Democratic Health on the country
metrics page. The growth drag, the extra interest on new government bonds and
the ruling party's election pressure were reading an older score that was no
longer kept up for those countries, and had slid to "Failing democracy". The
US was losing about 1.4 points of yearly growth to a score of 35 while its
country page showed 54.5.

Every one of those consequences now reads the score on the country page. For
the United States today that cuts the growth drag to about 0.2 points a year
and the bond penalty to about 0.1 points. The presidential race page shows the
same Democratic Health as the country page.

Worlds that start in 2027 now open every country from its own 2027 baseline
instead of reusing the 1979 one.
