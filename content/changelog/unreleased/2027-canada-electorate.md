## What changed

- Each of Canada's 13 provinces and territories has a 2027 population profile: language and community, age, education, income and where people live.
- Voters are grouped by community (anglophone, francophone, Indigenous, South Asian, East and Southeast Asian, other), which is what separates Quebec and the Atlantic provinces from the rest.
- Each policy area has its own preferences for these groups, with francophone and Indigenous voters leaning left on most issues.
- Provinces carry their own starting levels for housing prices, energy transition, wait times, child poverty and similar measures.
- Canada still cannot be joined. This only prepares the voter data for when it opens.
- Fixed Canada's starting army to use unit types that exist in the game.
