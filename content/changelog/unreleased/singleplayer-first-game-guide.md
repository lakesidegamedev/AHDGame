## What changed

- A new Career-mode local world shows a small Getting started card. It lists
  five steps: create your character, run a Campaign action, join a party, end
  your first turn and open your inbox.
- The card explains your funds and actions with your real numbers once you have
  a character, and links to the page for the step you are on.
- Steps are ticked from what happened in the world, not from clicks. Ending a
  turn counts once the world has moved past the turn you started on, and the
  party step only appears in worlds that have parties to join.
- Fold the card away with Hide guide, or press Skip guide. It does not come back
  for that world. Starting a new world offers it again.
- Worlds you already have, and Head of state or World simulation worlds, do not
  show the guide.
- In a Career world the multiplayer tutorial prompt no longer opens on its own.
  The tutorial page still starts it.
