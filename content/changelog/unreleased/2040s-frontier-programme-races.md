## What changed

- Three programmes open in the 2040s: fusion power (2041), orbital industry (2043) and biotech platforms (2046). A new public page, Frontier programme races, shows each one with a live leaderboard.
- A head of state or finance minister can fund a programme from the treasury for their nation, paid in the nation's own currency, and the national budget page links to the races. A corporation CEO can fund one from research points.
- The first entrant to reach a programme's threshold wins it. A winning nation gets an approval boost at home. A winning corporation gets a permanent plant output bonus of about 10% in its primary sector.
- Rivals that funded at least a quarter of the threshold lose a little approval at home. After a sunset period of 48 turns they receive a licence: nations regain a little approval (less than they lost), corporations get a half strength version of the method and 40% of their research points back.
- The winner is announced on the news wire, and the corporation tech tab links to the leaderboard.
