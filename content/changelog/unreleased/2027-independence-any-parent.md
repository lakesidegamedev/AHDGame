## What changed

- When Scotland or Wales leaves the United Kingdom, nothing changes for players. The same steps run in the same order.
- Each region that can become independent now records which country it becomes. The handover of land, money, parties, officials and elections reads that record instead of assuming a British region.
- Quebec still cannot hold a sovereignty referendum. That opens when the Quebec successor country is ready.
