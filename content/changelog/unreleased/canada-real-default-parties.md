- Replaces the fictional Canadian analogues with Liberal Party of Canada (LIB), Conservative Party of Canada (CON), New Democratic Party (NDP), Bloc Québécois (BQ) and Green Party of Canada (GRN).
- Seed reconciliation updates existing default identities in place, preserving party IDs and linked offices, members and organization records. Conflicting names stop the correction before any write.
- Both bootstrap and reset finalization apply the correction. Default identity labels and name protection remain active; the existing modeled positions and opening seat allocations are preserved.
- Updates major-party recognition, Bloc nationwide eligibility, readiness descriptions and wiki pages. The roster follows [Elections Canada's official 2025 results](https://www.elections.ca/res/rep/off/ovrGE45/62/table7E.html).

- Bloc Québécois can organize, recruit and contest elections in every Canadian province and territory. Existing outside-Quebec chapter restrictions are removed without inventing opening votes or seats.
