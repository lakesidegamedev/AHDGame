## What changed

- AI-run companies can now retool a plant onto a 2049 breakthrough method, such as commercial fusion, once the world's companies in that sector have done enough frontier research and the company has the matching technology.
- The operating strategy panel now shows the lower switching cost when your current method has been retired and you move to one of its successors. The confirmation shows the discounted fee too.
- When an AI-run party splits, a new party is founded, or a small party folds into a larger one, a news post now announces it.
- Parties that no player founded or leads now spend part of any treasury above a reserve on Political Strength each turn, so their funds no longer pile up without limit. Parties led or founded by players are unchanged.
- Sovereign debt news headlines read more cleanly.
