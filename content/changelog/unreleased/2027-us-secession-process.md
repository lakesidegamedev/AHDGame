## What changed

- Texas opens 2027 with a modest separatist mood that drifts each turn and nudges Texas governor votes toward a new Lone Star Independence Party and away from the two major parties.
- The Texas Governor sees a Sovereignty tab. A referendum cannot be requested yet, because Texas has no successor country built.
- In the United States the President, not a Prime Minister, grants or declines a referendum. A non-player President is covered by party leadership after the usual wait.
- A passed US vote creates a consent bill that goes through the House, the Senate and the President like any other bill.
- Referendum screens and Discord posts name the President, Congress and Washington for the United States.
