## What changed

- Ten bank owners were refunded the margin interest their banks paid on
  injected central bank cash, about 966 million dollars in total.
- Five closed banks were reopened with their original charter type and fresh
  books, with the required capital posted from the refund. Two owners had
  already reopened their banks and received the refund only.
