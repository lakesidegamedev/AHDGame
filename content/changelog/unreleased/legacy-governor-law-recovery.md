Added an explicit dry-run-first recovery tool for enacted legacy governor service laws in the United States, United Kingdom and Japan. The reviewed mapping preserves original bill links, existing service allocations and implementation limits, protects later policy decisions, and leaves taxes and subsidies intact.

Recovery transfers recorded funding without paying new cash or renewing stale receipts. It records transaction backups and an idempotent world receipt. Optional catch-up uses verified funded intervals and advances only fallback proxy outcomes, capped at two condition points per metric, without rewriting history or measured outcomes.
