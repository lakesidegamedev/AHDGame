## What changed

- A brand new world now starts AI-run countries on NPP autonomy v6, the same level the live world runs. Before, new worlds started one step lower.
- AI governments in a new world keep standing goals and commitments from the first turn, and seated AI politicians age, retire and are replaced.
- The live world and single-player games keep the level they already have.
