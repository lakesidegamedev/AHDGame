## What changed

- The per-turn net worth history now includes shares that companies you own hold in other companies, scaled by your share of the owner. It uses the same rule as the wealth list.
- The sidebar net worth, turn dashboard, wealth history and recaps read this history, so they no longer show a lower figure than the wealth list.
- Past turns keep the figures they were recorded with. New turns use the new total.
- No cash moves and no prices change.
