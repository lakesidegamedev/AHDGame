## What changed

- A company with a deposit-taking bank charter used to lose up to its branch share (50% by default) of its financial-services output as deposits filled its deposit limit. That cut is gone: financial sites make and sell their full output whatever the bank holds.
- The branch share still sets how many deposits the bank can hold.
- The sector run meter no longer shows capacity as reserved for the bank network, and the bank page and wiki explain the branch share this way.
