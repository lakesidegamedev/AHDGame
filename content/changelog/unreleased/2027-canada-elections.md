Canada's elections now run on a fixed four-year term, with the first regular
House of Commons election due in 2029 in the 2027 world. Each province and
territory elects its own share of the 343 seats, and a snap election restarts
the four-year clock. Premiers are elected on the shared four-year provincial
cycle.

Canada is still switched off for players and running worlds. Government
formation, laws and the cabinet follow in later steps.
