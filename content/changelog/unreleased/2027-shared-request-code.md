  Requests that the game handled with copied code, such as removing a linked

Removing a linked Google, Apple or Discord sign-in, uploading a feedback or
suggestion screenshot, accepting or declining a union presidency or central
bank chair offer, and the staff activity log, suspicious list and Patreon search
each had two or three copies of the same code. They now share one version.
Responses, limits and error messages are unchanged.
