## What changed

- A climate migration surge can start from 2035 in countries already seeing strong inflows when climate pressure is high. An orderly border policy, reception funding or integration funding each change how it goes.
- An Arctic opening can start from 2033 for countries with northern coasts, when climate pressure and world tension are both rising. A northern treaty, joint extraction concessions or a rescue fleet each help.
- Antimicrobial resistance can start from 2036 where public health systems are weak. A drug stewardship law, hospital infection control or new antibiotic research each help.
- An orbital debris cascade can start from 2040 in research-heavy economies, more likely when world tension is high. A space traffic law, backup ground networks or debris removal each help.
- Each crisis comes in waves, ends on its own even if nobody acts, and rests for two years before it can return. Nothing here is scheduled, and strong countries never roll for the matching crisis.
