title: Plants that sell out ramp up again after a retool or a supply shortage
summary: >-
  A plant is now judged on the products it currently makes, and a plant that
  sells everything it makes no longer shrinks just because its inputs ran short.

After a strategy change, leftover stock of products a plant no longer makes is
still put up for sale, but it no longer holds back how much the plant runs. A
mine that sells all of its iron now grows even while old oil and gas sit unsold.

When a plant sells out but short inputs cut what it made, next turn it plans
from what it meant to make, not from the smaller amount it managed. A supply
gap no longer shrinks the plant turn after turn. This only applies while the
plant's sales cover its wages, site and input costs; a plant losing money still
cuts back as before.
