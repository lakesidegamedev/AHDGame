title: "Canadian provincial legislatures use their real size"
summary: >-
  Provincial and territorial legislatures in Canada are now sized from their
  own seat counts everywhere, including veto override votes.
tags: [canada, legislature]
badges: [patch]
areas: [fullstack]
---

Each Canadian provincial and territorial legislature was sized from that
province's federal Senate seats, so Alberta's 87-seat chamber was treated as
six seats. The legislature page now shows the real size even when a seat is
empty, and party shares and the majority line read against it.

Overriding a Premier's veto now takes two thirds of the full legislature, for
example 58 of Alberta's 87 seats, instead of two thirds of the Senate figure.
Ordinary bill votes were not affected: they pass when more members vote for
than against.
