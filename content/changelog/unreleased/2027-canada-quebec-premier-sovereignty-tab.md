## What changed

- The Premier of Quebec's office has a Sovereignty tab. It shows the current mood, what is moving it and a forecast, using Quebec's own terms: Federalist, Autonomist and Pro-Sovereignty.
- The Premier can change their stance for office action points, with the same cooldown as the UK First Ministers.
- In Quebec elections, a high sovereignty mood moves a small share of votes toward the Laurentian Alliance and away from its two main federalist rivals. A low mood does the reverse.
- A Pro-Sovereignty Premier whose party leads a high mood gets the same stepped election bonus that UK First Ministers get.
- The referendum request button explains that a vote cannot be held until an independent Quebec can be formed.
- Canada still cannot be joined.
