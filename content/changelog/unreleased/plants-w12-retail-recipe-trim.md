## What changed

- Standard retail buys about 39% fewer inputs per unit, down to 10 input lines with no tiny ones.
- Brick and mortar retail buys about 32% fewer inputs, and its healthcare line is gone.
- E-commerce retail buys about 29% fewer inputs.
- E-commerce fulfillment is unchanged.
- Retail output and prices are unchanged. Plants keep more of each sale as profit.
