Founding a caucus alone used to give its chair 2 party influence a turn straight
away. Caucus chairs (+2) and vice chairs (+1) now earn that bonus only while the
caucus has at least 5 active members, so the bonus rewards leading a real
group. The profile breakdown shows the same rule.
