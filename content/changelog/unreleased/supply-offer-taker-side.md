title: Supply offers now say which side you are on
summary: >-
  Taking a company's offer to sell makes you the buyer, and the supply offers
  list now says so, so an offer to sell is no longer read as a chance to sell.
tags: [corporations, market]
badges: [patch]
areas: [frontend]
---

A row on the supply offers list showed only the other company's side, so an offer from a company selling its output looked like an offer to buy from you. Each row now states what taking it makes you, the list header reads "Their side", and the confirmation after taking an offer names the side, the volume and the company.
