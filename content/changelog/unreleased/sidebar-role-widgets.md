## What changed

- My race: if you are standing in an election, see your share of the vote, your
  place, the leader and the turns left, with links to the race and your campaign.
  Candidates start with it in their tray.
- Chamber floor: if you hold a seat in your national legislature, see the bills up
  for a vote in your chamber and how you voted on each. Bills you have not voted on
  come first, and the closed tray counts them.
- My office: governors, cabinet members and heads of state or government see
  approval, budget balance, growth and unemployment for the place they run, with
  links to its economy and budget. Office holders start with it in their tray.
- Since last turn: your net worth and cash change over the last turn, how your
  shares, bonds and funds moved, and your unread notifications by type.
- Role widgets only appear in the widget list for players they apply to.
