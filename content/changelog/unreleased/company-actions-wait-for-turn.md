title: Company changes wait for the turn to finish
summary: Company and sector changes pause for the few minutes companies are settled each turn.
tags: [corporations, economy]
badges: [minor]
areas: [backend]
---

- Spin-offs, dissolutions, relocations, regional sector actions and other company changes now wait during the few minutes each turn spends settling companies, and ask you to try again shortly. The rest of the turn is unaffected.
- This stops a change made during the turn from holding the turn back.
