title: Company changes wait for the turn to finish
summary: Company and sector changes are paused while a turn is being processed.
tags: [corporations, economy]
badges: [minor]
areas: [backend]
---

- Spin-offs, dissolutions, relocations, regional sector actions and other company changes now wait while a turn is being processed, and ask you to try again shortly.
- This stops a change made during the turn from holding the turn back.
