## What changed

- Partial corporate coverage no longer repeatedly increases unemployment.
- Hiring, layoffs, labor-force changes and business ownership transfers use employment counts across all countries.
- Existing national and regional laws are recognized without passing them again. Program effects respect implementation and funding.
- Existing worlds adjust gradually. Additional operating jobs and effective policy can bring unemployment below 5%.
