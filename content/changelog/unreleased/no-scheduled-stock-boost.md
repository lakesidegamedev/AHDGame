title: Company values no longer jump at a fixed turn
summary: >-
  A scheduled lift to company revenue and valuations, written for the previous
  world, is removed so it cannot fire partway through the new economy.
tags: [economy, stocks]
badges: [patch]
areas: [backend]
---

Company revenue, sector values and bank values no longer rise on a fixed turn
schedule. Share prices now follow only what companies actually earn and own,
at any point in the world.
