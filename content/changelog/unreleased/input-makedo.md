title: Plants no longer stall over a trace of construction work
summary: >-
  Construction services now trade across your whole country, and a plant that
  is short only of a small input runs anyway and pays extra for the shortfall,
  instead of idling most of its capacity.
tags: [economy, corporations, production]
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine]
---

Some plants need a tiny amount of construction work for every unit they make.
Construction services used to trade only inside the plant's own state, so a
plant in a state with almost no construction crews could plan a full run and
make a few percent of it. Construction services now sell anywhere in their own
country, the way freight already does, so a plant can hire crews from a
neighbouring state.

A plant that is short only of a minor input, one that makes up 5% or less of
its recipe's input cost, now runs its full plan. It improvises the missing
units and pays twice its bid price for them as an extra site cost, which goes
to the same local businesses that are paid its other site costs. A plant short
of a major input is still cut back in proportion, as before.
