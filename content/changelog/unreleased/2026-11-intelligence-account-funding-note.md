## What changed

- An intelligence service's account on the Allocations tab now explains that its money is set by the country's intelligence funding law and shown on the Intelligence tab, instead of listing every figure as zero.
- Defence accounts keep their yearly figure, with a plain note that defence is paid through the defence budget.
