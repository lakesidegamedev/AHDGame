date: 2026-10-29
title: The bond market no longer creates money to refill itself
summary: >-
  Each currency's bond market used to top itself up with new money every turn.
  It now holds only what buyers, coupons and repayments put in.
tags: [economy, bonds]
badges: [patch]
areas: [backend]
---

The bond market pool that buys new government bonds no longer receives a
per-turn top-up with no payer. When the pool cannot afford a whole new issue,
the rest stays unsold and sells over later turns, or a computer-run central
bank buys part of it with money that shows up in that country's money supply.
