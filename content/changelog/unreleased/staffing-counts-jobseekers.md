## What changed

- Plant staffing tightness now counts unemployed residents in the labour a
  state can still supply, instead of only people already holding corporate
  jobs.
- A state with high unemployment now reads as having hiring room, so staffing
  targets stop falling while jobseekers are still available.
- The new measure ships behind the `labourStaffingSlackStage` switch and stays
  off until the live world check is done.
