date: 2026-10-18
title: Normal businesses make a healthy profit again
summary: Staff costs and some production recipes were rebalanced so a normally run business earns 15 to 25% at ordinary prices.
tags: [economy, corporations]
badges: [major]
areas: [backend]
---

- Every industry was checked at ordinary prices. Many ordinary businesses, such as hospitals, shops, factories and offices, earned too little or lost money just for existing.
- Staff costs are lower in technology, finance, healthcare, real estate, retail, construction, manufacturing, vehicles and energy.
- 23 specialised production methods use fewer materials, so they also reach a normal profit. Ordinary production methods are unchanged.
- Nobody's costs went up.
