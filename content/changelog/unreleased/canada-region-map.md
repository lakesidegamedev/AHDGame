## What changed

- The Canada map page draws all thirteen provinces and territories instead of the economy-only placeholder.
- Map views: party organization, Commons seats by party, Premier by party, government approval, political lean, sector specialization and logistics.
- Clicking a province opens it, like on the other country maps.
