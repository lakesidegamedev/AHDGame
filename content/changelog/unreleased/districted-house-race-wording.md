title: House races decided by district no longer quote a statewide quota
summary: A House race where seats are decided district by district now says so, instead of describing seats as apportioned by statewide vote share.
badges: [patch]
areas: [frontend]
tags: [elections, redistricting]
---

- On a live House race with redistricting on, the seat panel no longer shows a Hare quota or whole-quota arithmetic. Seats are decided district by district, and the page now says so.
- Races where seats are apportioned by statewide share are unchanged.
