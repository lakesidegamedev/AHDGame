## What changed

- The no starting parties option, until now limited to the 1991 world, is
  available for the 2027 world from the reset screen and the reset command.
- With it on, the United States and Canada open with no parties and no seated
  politicians. Players found the parties, and a founding election round fills
  the offices.
- Countries run by the computer keep their real parties.
- Founders in those two countries can register the names that the default
  parties would otherwise have reserved.
- A reset now creates Canada's House of Commons seats. Before, they were
  missing until repaired by hand.
