## What changed

- Canada's 343 Commons seats start filled, province by province, matching the April 2025 federal result: 169 for the governing party, 144 for the opposition, 22 for the Quebec party, 7 for the left party and 1 Green.
- That gives the opening government a working Commons from the first turn instead of an empty chamber.
- Canada still cannot be joined, and live worlds are not changed.
