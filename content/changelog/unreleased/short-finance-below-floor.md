## What changed

- AI companies with no savings above their reserve stopped expanding, so they
  would not spend the cash their plants need to run. That also stopped them
  asking a bank to fund plants in markets that are short of goods, which was
  meant to work even below the reserve.
- A company held at its reserve can now ask for that loan again. It puts in at
  most a tenth of its own cash, and it still cannot found new businesses or
  build with its own money.
