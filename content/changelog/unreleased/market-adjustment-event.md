## What changed

- Each turn the game measures, per country, how much installed capacity sits in plants that lost money. The event opens when that share stays above 40 percent for three turns while GDP growth stays negative.
- The event stacks no margin shock and no metric ticks on top of the losses the market already books.
- The head of state can pay out 1 percent of GDP from treasury cash to households, or let the adjustment run. The payment is real treasury money.
- Nothing happens until the event is switched on.
