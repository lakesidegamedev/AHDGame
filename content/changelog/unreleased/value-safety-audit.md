## What changed

- Corporate financial views share settled revenue, expenses, margins and cash
  receipts. Unpaid obligations remain visible as liabilities, and missing
  settlement evidence is identified instead of presented as a completed result.
- Money transfers, market orders, share and bond trades, investment subscriptions,
  corporate ownership changes and government payments retain their original
  currency and payment identity across supported retries.
- Interrupted bond servicing retains the original holders and recovers unpaid
  recipients after retirement. Completed turn retries read the recorded result.
  Recorded interest comes from completed payments and excludes unverified
  historical estimates.
- Paid campaigns, union organizing, cabinet projects and bill proposals commit
  their resources and outcomes together. Rejected actions roll back their
  changes, and recorded request identities prevent duplicate supported purchases.
- Country and regional transitions preserve cash, obligations, financial
  accounts, resource stocks and ownership claims. Unsettled estates are retained
  until their obligations can be resolved.
- Financial dialogs validate quantities and current quotes before spending.
  Forecasts, historical figures and incomplete records have clearer labels.
- CEO attrition preserves plant wear and facility counts when a turn resumes.
- Turn settlement and recovery paths retain completion proofs and pending
  obligations so interruptions cannot silently erase a claim or repeat a
  completed charge.

These changes protect future actions and retries. They do not apply speculative
refunds or reconstruct missing historical payments without evidence.
