## What changed

- A nominee confirmed as Vice President now earns the Vice President achievement the moment they are seated. Before, only an election win did.
- A Vice President who succeeds to the presidency now earns the President achievement.
- The Incumbent ("Won 3 elections") now counts the elections in your career history, so winning three governor races in a row counts even though you hold only one governor seat at a time.
