summary: Supply offer lists can now be sorted by amount, price, date or commodity name in either direction, and remember your choice.

The supply offers list on the market page and the offers page for each commodity now share the same controls. Show buy offers, sell offers or both, then sort by amount (largest or smallest first), price (lowest or highest first), date posted, or commodity name on the market page. The list shows how many offers your filters hide, gives you a one-click way back to every offer when nothing matches, and remembers your choice in this browser so it does not reset each time you open the page.
