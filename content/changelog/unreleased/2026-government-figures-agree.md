## What changed

- The Budget page shows "Budget balance" with a plus for a surplus and a minus for a deficit, matching the Economy page.
- The Debt box shows the same effective borrowing rate as its "Why this rate" table, and notes the rate booked at the last rollover if it differs.
- The Economy page says which GDP its debt ratio uses. State budget tax lines show base, rate and booked amount, say when the booked amount lags, and always add up to a stated total.
- The Central Bank page separates bank reserves from Treasury cash and says the rating table is for private borrowers, not the government.
- The commodity page's global "vs base" now compares the shown price with the base price.
- The rankings table orders nations with the same score by the underlying figure, such as debt to GDP, and only shares a rank when the figures match.
- The Economy page explains that state ownership concentration and the sector tiles measure different things.
