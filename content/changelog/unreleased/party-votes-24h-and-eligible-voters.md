## What changed

- Every National Committee proposal, not just removal votes, stays open for at least 24 hours from when it was proposed. It cannot pass before then, even if enough members vote yes sooner. A proposal that can no longer reach enough yes votes still fails at once.
- When a proposal is counted, only votes from people who still sit on the committee or hold a party office count. A vote from someone who has left no longer counts toward the result, and the number of votes needed is worked out from the people still seated.
- When a party elects its leaders by committee only, only ballots from committee members and party officers count, checked when the votes are counted. Ballots from other members, including ones cast before the party switched to committee-only voting, do not count.
- Proposal cards show the earliest time any proposal can pass, and the vote counts on the cards and leadership pages match what will actually be counted.
- Proposals that already passed are not affected.
