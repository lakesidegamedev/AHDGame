date: 2026-10-20
title: R&D ecosystem modifier removed
summary: R&D points now depend only on what your corporation spends and how well it pays its workers.
tags: [corporations, research]
badges: [patch]
areas: [fullstack]
---

- The R&D ecosystem modifier is gone. It moved R&D gain up or down by as much as 15% based on world software and consulting supply, which no single corporation could influence.
- The Research position panel no longer shows the R&D ecosystem row.
- R&D gain still rises when you pay workers above the baseline wage.
