## What changed

- Building gets more expensive when your company could already supply a large
  share of what buyers want, so one company cannot corner a market.
- Freight and construction now sell anywhere in their own country, but this
  build cost still compared them with a single state's demand. In a small state
  a normal logistics arm looked like several times the market, and building
  more cost up to 45 times the usual price.
- It now compares freight and construction with demand across the whole
  country, and counts your sites across that country. Other goods are not
  affected.
