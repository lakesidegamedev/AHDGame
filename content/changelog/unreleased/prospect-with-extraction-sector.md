date: 2026-11-09
title: "Any company with an extraction sector can run surveys there"
summary: >-
  A company that operates an extraction sector in a state can now launch a
  geological survey in that state, whatever its main or secondary business.
tags: [corporations, extraction]
badges: [patch]
areas: [backend, frontend]
---

- Surveys were limited to companies whose main or secondary business was extraction, so a company that bought an extraction sector could not survey its deposits. Any company with an extraction sector in a state can now run surveys there.
- A company with no extraction sector in the state and no extraction main or secondary business still cannot survey it.
