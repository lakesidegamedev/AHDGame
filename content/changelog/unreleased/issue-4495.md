## What changed

- The exchange page and the chair's intervention tab show how far the rate sits from the nearest boundary.
- A defending band shows the turns of maximum-strength defence it needs, at the rate one turn of defence can actually move.
- A corridor more than a game year away is marked Out of reach instead of Defending, on both surfaces.
- Nothing in settlement changes: reserves, spending and the rate path are exactly as before.
