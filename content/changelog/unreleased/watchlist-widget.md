## What changed

- With experimental features on, company and commodity pages have a Watch button.
  Starred items appear in the new Watchlist widget with their current price and
  the change since last turn.
- Add the Watchlist from the side tray's Edit button, or place it beside a
  desktop menu. Remove an item with the x on its row.
- Private companies show as private, without a share price. Your watchlist holds
  up to 20 items and saves to your account.
