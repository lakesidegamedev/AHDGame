  In the 2027 world, mines that lose money can reduce their next run when they
  already have enough stock to cover recent orders. This cuts replenishment
  costs while keeping that stock available for sale.

## What changed

- A loss-making mine can trim its next production run by up to 30% if each commodity it recently sold has at least 125% of that volume in stock.
- Mines serving other sites in their company keep their regular production plan.
