- The Soviet Congress can now open the negotiated dissolution settlement by itself in the 1991 world, as long as no player holds a seat in it or runs the government.
- The settlement is an ordinary bill. Congress can vote it down, and every republic still has to ratify the same terms before the Union dissolves into Russia and the other successor states.
- A player seated in Congress still decides whether to open the settlement, the same way they already could.
