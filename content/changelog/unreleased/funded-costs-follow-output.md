- Under the upcoming market, a plant pays wages and running costs for the share of its line it actually runs. A plant cut back to 20% pays 20% of its wages, and its idle workers become unemployed.
- Under the upcoming market, plants run less when each unit sold no longer covers its inputs, wages and running costs.
