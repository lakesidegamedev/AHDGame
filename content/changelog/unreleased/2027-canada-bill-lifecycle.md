## What changed

- A Canadian bill is decided by one House of Commons vote. If it passes, it receives Royal Assent and becomes law. If it fails, it dies.
- The appointed Senate does not vote on player bills.
- Canada still cannot be joined. This only prepares Parliament for when it opens.
