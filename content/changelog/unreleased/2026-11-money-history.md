## What changed

- The Finances section of your profile has a new "Money in and out" list. It shows the turn, what the money was for, the amount, and the balance after where one was recorded.
- Dividends, CEO salary, office income, fundraising, bond coupons, share trades, transfers, taxes and more each have their own plain label. Dividends and similar lines name the company where it is known.
- You can switch between personal money and campaign money, and load older entries.
- Only you can see your list. History older than 30 days is no longer kept.
