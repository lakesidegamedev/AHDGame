title: Commons by-elections contest the whole vacated bloc
summary: Commons by-elections now contest every seat a departing MP's bloc held, instead of only one.
tags: [elections, uk, commons]
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine]
---

When an MP holding several Commons seats leaves for another office, the by-election now contests all of the seats they held, so the region keeps its full delegation. Before, only one seat was contested and the rest of the bloc was lost.
