## What changed

- A newly reset 2019, 2023 or 2027 world seats every country it contains in
  the United Nations. Before, only the United States, United Kingdom, Germany
  and Japan were members.
- Canada is a member from the start in 2027 worlds.
- 1953 and 1991 worlds are unchanged. Worlds already running keep their
  current membership.
