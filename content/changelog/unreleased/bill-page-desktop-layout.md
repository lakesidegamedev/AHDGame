## What changed

- A new header shows the bill's stage, the time left to vote, and the Aye, No
  and Abstain counts for the chamber that is voting, along with your own vote.
- Co-sponsoring, filibustering and withdrawing sit next to the bill title.
- On desktop the page is much wider, and the seating chart sits beside the vote
  tally and voting buttons.
- Provisions have their own section. The floor debate comes right after the
  vote, and the full text is folded away until you open it.
- Co-sponsors are listed in the side column.
- The discussion panel is now called Floor debate. The writing box grows with
  your text, shows how many characters you have left, and posts with Ctrl+Enter.
- Phones keep a single column in the same reading order.
