## What changed

- On desktop the bill page is wider. The sponsor, co-sponsors and bill actions
  sit in a column beside the bill text, and the seating chart sits beside the
  vote tally and voting buttons.
- The discussion panel is now called Floor debate. The writing box grows with
  your text, shows how many characters you have left, and posts with Ctrl+Enter.
- Phones keep the same single-column layout as before.
