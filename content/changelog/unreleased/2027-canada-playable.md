## What changed

- A new 2027 world now starts with Canada open to players. You can create a character there, run for the House of Commons, found corporations and trade.
- Canada's 13 provinces and territories elect 343 MPs. Premiers' offices start vacant and fill at the first provincial elections.
- The Canadian dollar trades on the currency exchange.
- Canada appears on the world map and has its own seal on the Prime Minister's office.
- Existing worlds do not change.
