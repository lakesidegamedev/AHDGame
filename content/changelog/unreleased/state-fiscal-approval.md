## What changed

- State government approval was showing Government Deficit, Fiscal Crisis and Heavy Public Debt from the national budget, so a state with a surplus and no debt still lost approval for them.
- Each state's fiscal conditions now read that state's own budget: its surplus or deficit and its own borrowing, measured against its economy. A state running a surplus with low debt can now earn Balanced Budget and Fiscal Discipline.
- The national deficit and debt conditions now apply to national government approval, where the national budget is decided.
