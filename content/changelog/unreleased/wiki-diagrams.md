## What changed

- Pages for bills, elections, parties, the military, central banks, bonds, corporations and more now include diagrams of their flows, timelines and decision paths.
- Where the wiki already had a matching live diagram, that one is used instead of a new picture.
- The diagrams show how things connect and in what order. The numbers stay in the page text and tables.
