## What changed

- New extraction strategy: **Natural Gas Focus**. It makes natural gas first (0.58) and crude oil as a byproduct (0.32), the reverse of Oil & Gas.
- It uses the same inputs as Oil & Gas and earns the same margin at normal prices, so pick whichever of the two fuels your market is short of.
- AI companies that switch mines to target a natural gas shortage now use Natural Gas Focus. They still use Oil & Gas for oil shortages.
- Suggested by a player (S#422).
