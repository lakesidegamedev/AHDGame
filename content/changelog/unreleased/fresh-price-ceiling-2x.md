title: Shortage prices top out at twice the base price
summary: >-
  In the new economy a scarce good can now rise to at most twice its base
  price, down from three times.
tags: [economy, commodities]
badges: [patch]
areas: [backend]
---

A good in short supply still gets more expensive, by at most 2% a turn, but
now stops at twice its base price. The first companies into a short market
still earn well, just not three times the normal price for as long as the
shortage lasts. New plants in short markets still build four times faster.
