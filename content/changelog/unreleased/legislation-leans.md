## What changed

Proposal options, provision reviews and bills now show their economic and social positions using the same rules as voting. Funding and delivery choices use economic positions; privacy and media choices show both axes where appropriate. Administrative transparency and procurement changes no longer imply an extreme social stance.

Scotland and Wales receive their own exact-rate national tax instruments after independence. Startup adds missing definitions without changing any existing tax rate or budget. Fresh worlds include the same instruments, and the Welsh picker resolves its established legislation scope.
