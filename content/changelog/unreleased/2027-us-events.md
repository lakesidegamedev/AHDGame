## What changed

- Thirty-six new events are tied to a decade: twelve each for 2027 to 2029, the 2030s and the 2040s. They cover shutdown standoffs, redistricting, court rulings, tariffs, grid emergencies, automation layoffs, water pacts, Senate rules fights and more.
- Eighteen regional stories run from 2027 onward, including Gulf hurricanes and rebuilding contracts, Western wildfire smoke, Colorado River water, Rust Belt automation, Sun Belt housing and heat, Atlantic sea walls and flood insurance, Plains drought, Pacific Northwest ports, Great Lakes algae, Alaskan thaw, Hawaiian tourism limits, Mississippi low water and a border trade bridge.
- Some events are for officeholders, some for company heads and some for everyone. Every choice has a spread of outcomes, and a passive option is always available.
- These events appear only for American characters. Worlds started from any other date, and the live world, behave exactly as before.
